## Related work

- Moore et al. 2018, "Quantized zero bias conductance plateau in semiconductor-superconductor heterostructures without non-Abelian Majorana zero modes", <https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.03164>. Theory: partially separated Andreev bound states give 2e²/h plateaus in end-of-wire tunneling over large ranges of barrier, gate and field.
- Chen et al. 2019, "Ubiquitous non-Majorana Zero-Bias Conductance Peaks in Nanowire Devices", <https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.02773>. InSb nanowire devices where Majorana-like peaks with zero-bias pinning become common at finite field, traced to crowded trivial subgap states.
- Anselmetti et al. 2019, "End-to-end correlated subgap states in hybrid nanowires", <https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.05549>. Three-terminal wires at zero field: end-to-end correlations counted over thousands of features are four times weaker in a 900 nm device than in a 300 nm one.
- Pan and Das Sarma 2020, "Physical mechanisms for zero-bias conductance peaks in Majorana nanowires", <https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.11413>. Theory survey of dots, inhomogeneous potentials and disorder; strong disorder generically gives trivial zero-bias peaks.
- Martinez et al. 2021, "Measurement circuit effects in three-terminal electrical transport measurements", <https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.02671>. Exact corrections for line impedances in three-terminal setups, and a test: a divider artifact is an attenuated mirror of the local conductances.
