## Related work

- Halperin, Stern, Neder and Rosenow 2011, "Theory of the Fabry-Perot Quantum Hall Interferometer", <https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.4598>. Shows that the AB and Coulomb-dominated limits give stripes in the field and gate plane with different spacings and opposite slopes, and a superposition at intermediate coupling.
- Kou, Marcus, Pfeiffer and West 2012, "Coulomb Oscillations in Antidots in the Integer and Fractional Quantum Hall Regimes", <https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.1600>. Uses gate and field periods in GaAs antidots to find a tunneling charge of (2/3)e and a single charged edge at ν = 2/3.
- Hata et al. 2024, "Coulomb oscillations of a quantum antidot formed by an airbridged pillar gate in the integer and fractional quantum Hall regime", <https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.11401>. Shows an air-bridged antidot whose area is smaller at ν = 2/3, with an effective charge near 2e/3 obtained by assuming a constant gate capacitance.
- Di Luca et al. 2025, "Quantum Hall Antidot as a Fractional Coulombmeter", <https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.04209>. Shows that in a Coulomb-dominated bilayer graphene antidot the gate period and slope give the tunneling charge: e/3 at 4/3, 5/3 and 7/3, and 2e/3 at 2/3.
- Hajigeorgiou et al. 2026, "Constriction-induced modulation of charging energy in a quantum Hall cavity", <https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19187>. Shows that the charging energy of a Coulomb-dominated cavity varies by up to 60% over 100 mT when the constrictions are weakly pinched off, linked to incompressible fractional regions in the constrictions.
