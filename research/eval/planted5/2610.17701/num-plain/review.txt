Review: "Observation of stacked vortex structure with number controllable Majorana zero mode in Bi2Te3/FeTe heterojunction" (Han, Luo, Hu et al.)

SUMMARY OF THE CLAIMS

The authors grow 1QL Bi2Te3 on 20 u.c. FeTe by MBE and study it with STM/STS at 0.4 K. They report (i) interfacial superconductivity confined to Bi2Te3-covered islands, with two coherence-peak energies (+/-1.85 and +/-2.92 meV) and a gap modulated with the 7 x sqrt3 moire period; (ii) star-shaped vortices whose LDOS modulation shares wavevectors with the Bi2Te3 normal-state QPI; (iii) two branches of vortex bound states, a dispersive X-shaped branch assigned to Bi2Te3 and a quantum-limit branch with an unsplit ZBCP plus near-integer CdGM levels assigned to FeTe ("stacked-core vortex"); (iv) MZMs in all measured vortices, with their number tunable by field and island size; and (v) an outlook toward Majorana edge modes, braiding and fusion.

The materials work is interesting, and a clean, island-confined superconducting interface would be a useful platform. As written, though, the manuscript does not support the headline claims ("compelling spectroscopic evidence" for MZMs, "number controllable" MZMs, a "stoichiometric" platform). Several numbers do not agree with each other or with the conclusions drawn from them, and parts of the physical picture contradict themselves. I would recommend major revision.

A. NUMERICAL AND INTERNAL-CONSISTENCY PROBLEMS

1. The level spacing of the dispersive branch is off by three orders of magnitude. The text gives kF ~ 0.3 nm^-1 and EF ~ 300 meV and then says the CdGM level spacing is "~0.03 ueV". With Delta = 1.85 to 2.92 meV, Delta^2/EF = 0.011 to 0.028 meV, i.e. about 10 to 30 ueV (~0.03 meV), not 0.03 ueV. The qualitative point stands (both values are far below the stated 0.37 meV resolution), but the number is wrong as printed and should be corrected, with the Delta used stated explicitly.

2. kF and EF do not agree with each other. kF ~ 0.3 nm^-1 (0.03 A^-1) with EF ~ 300 meV implies, for a linear band, hbar*vF = EF/kF ~ 10 eV*A (vF ~ 1.5 x 10^6 m/s). That is roughly 3 to 4 times the known Bi2Te3 surface-state velocity (~2.5 to 4 eV*A). For a parabolic band it implies m* ~ 0.01 me. Bi2Te3 Fermi wavevectors are typically ~0.1 A^-1 (~1 nm^-1), and the QPI scale bar in Fig. 2D-F (0.3 A^-1) points the same way. Please check the units (nm^-1 vs A^-1) and show the BdG fit itself, the parameter uncertainties, and how the fitted kF compares with the measured QPI q-vectors in Fig. S10. Note also that Ref. 48 is ARPES on Bi2Te3 films on Si. Charge transfer from FeTe (Refs. 33, 58) will shift EF, so agreement with Ref. 48 is not a strong check.

3. The quoted CdGM spacing disagrees with the fitted levels. The text says the two pairs of symmetric peaks have "an energy spacing of about 0.7 meV". The fitted levels are -2.1, -0.93, 0.003, 0.79 and 1.96 meV, so the nearest-neighbour spacings are 0.79, 1.17, 0.93 and 1.17 meV, and L+/-2 / 2 gives ~1.0 meV. A spacing near 0.7 meV fits none of them. Please reconcile.

4. The levels do not clearly follow an integer sequence. The key discriminator (Ref. 51) is integer (0, +/-1, +/-2) versus half-integer (+/-1/2, +/-3/2, +/-5/2) quantization in units of Delta^2/EF. For an integer sequence the ratio L2/L1 should be 2; for a half-integer sequence it should be 3. The data give 1.96/0.79 = 2.5 and 2.1/0.93 = 2.3, roughly midway, with error bars of 0.11 to 0.22 meV. The L+/-1 levels sit well below the integer prediction set by L+/-2. Calling this "approximately integer quantized" and "compelling spectroscopic evidence" overstates what the data show. Please fit both models to the full spectra (not just the peak positions) and give a quantitative model comparison. Also show that a pair of half-integer levels at about +/-0.4 to 0.5 meV, convolved with your ~0.37 meV resolution and thermal broadening, cannot reproduce a single "ZBCP" with 0.5 meV FWHM. The FWHM is not much larger than the stated resolution, so this check matters.

5. The bound states sit at or above the gap they are assigned to. L+/-2 is at ~2.0 meV, above the inner coherence peak (1.85 meV). If the FeTe-derived branch belongs to the 1.85 meV gap, L2 lies above it. If it belongs to the 2.92 meV gap, then Delta^2/EF ~ 1 meV implies EF_FeTe ~ 8 to 9 meV, which should be stated and justified (ARPES?). Please say which gap belongs to which layer, and give the Delta, EF and fitted xi0 used for the MZM profile in Fig. 3H-I ("Delta and EF given by experimental results" is not enough). Using EF ~ 300 meV there would contradict the quantum-limit assignment.

6. The moire period does not match the stated lattice. Fig. 1I gives a 2.9 nm period, "about 7a0". With a0(Bi2Te3) = 0.438 nm, 7a0 = 3.07 nm. With a0(FeTe) = 0.382 nm, 7a0 = 2.67 nm. Neither is 2.9 nm. A near-commensurate cell such as 7 a_FeTe ~ 6 a_BT along one axis and 2 a_FeTe ~ sqrt3 a_BT along the other would also make "7 x sqrt3" ambiguous about which lattice it refers to. Please define the supercell in both lattices (Fig. S2) and quote the measured period with its uncertainty from the FFT in Fig. 1E.

7. The vortex density needs checking against the flux quantum. At 1.0 T the Abrikosov spacing is ~1.075*sqrt(Phi0/B) ~ 49 nm, and each vortex occupies ~2070 nm^2. The star-shaped modulations extend "more than ~40 nm", comparable to the intervortex spacing. So "vortex-vortex interactions should be negligible because no second vortex appears within the island" needs the island dimensions. If the island in Fig. 3A (100 nm field of view) is much larger than ~2000 nm^2 and holds only one vortex, flux is not entering at the bulk density (screening or edge barriers). That bears directly on the "number control" claim and should be quantified. The field for Fig. 3A is not stated.

8. Figure and reference bookkeeping.
   - Fig. S8 is cited both as the normal-state QPI of Bi2Te3 (line 199) and as the vortex-edge spectra used for the BdG fit (Fig. S8A, line 216).
   - Refs. 14 and 49 are the same paper.
   - Ref. 54 (Dirac-cone-like states on AFM FeTe) is never cited, although "another group" in the FeTe ARPES paragraph presumably refers to it.
   - Refs. 34 and 35 (Caroli et al.; Hayashi et al.) are cited for "spectroscopic signatures consistent with Majorana zero modes", but they concern CdGM / quantum-limit states, not MZMs.
   - Ref. 57 (topological Hall effect in a magnetic insulator/heavy metal) does not support charge transfer or SOC enhancement in Bi2Te3/FeTe.
   - Ref. 59 (Wigner crystals) does not support moire-promoted superconductivity.
   - Ref. 6 (quasiparticle poisoning) is cited for Majorana fusion and vortex control.
   - Typos in the captions: "write dash line" should read "white", and "bule" should read "blue".

9. The energy resolution needs a derivation. "~0.37 meV at 0.4 K" is larger than 3.5 kBT (0.12 meV) plus a 0.1 mV modulation would give if added in quadrature. Please say how it was determined (e.g., a fit to a superconducting tip or reference gap, giving the effective electron temperature). Both the "quasi-continuous" argument and the ZBCP-width argument rely on it.

B. PHYSICS AND INTERPRETATION

10. The text contradicts itself on whether FeTe is superconducting. The introduction and Section 1 state that neither FeTe nor Bi2Te3 superconducts on its own, and that bare FeTe shows no coherence peaks (Fig. S3D). Later the MZM branch is assigned to "the first FeTe layer which is also superconducting", supported by Refs. 55-56 (stoichiometric FeTe is a superconductor). The proposed mechanism has the same problem. A Te-rich atmosphere during Bi2Te3 growth acting like Te annealing should also affect the uncovered FeTe regions, which the authors find non-superconducting. Please explain why the mechanism acts only under Bi2Te3, or drop it.

11. The text contradicts itself on the topology of 1QL Bi2Te3. The authors argue (correctly) that 1QL Bi2Te3 has a hybridization gap and no Dirac surface states, so the topology "cannot originate from Bi2Te3". Yet the dispersive branch is explained by "the large EF of the Dirac surface states" by analogy with Bi2Te3/NbSe2. The star shape is attributed to the anisotropic (hexagonally warped) Fermi surface of Bi2Te3, using QPI references on bulk Bi2Te3 (Refs. 43, 47). Please say which 1QL band is responsible and show its Fermi surface (ARPES on the heterostructure, or the QPI in Fig. S10 analysed properly).

12. The evidence for topology in the FeTe layer is indirect. The Dirac-like dispersion is shown on Te-annealed FeTe films (Figs. S6-S7), not on the heterostructure. Dirac-cone-like states also occur in nematic AFM FeTe/FeSe without nontrivial topology (Ref. 54). The bicollinear AFM order of Fe1+yTe, and whether it survives under Bi2Te3, is not discussed. The topological surface state of the relevant FeTe would be buried at the interface beneath 1QL Bi2Te3, and how the tip couples to it is not addressed. The "origin" section is speculation and should be labelled as such.

13. "Stoichiometric" is not demonstrated. The manuscript itself says the FeTe is Fe1+yTe with excess interstitial Fe, and that the Te-rich growth environment "may" reduce it. The Fe excess in the interfacial FeTe should be quantified (STEM-EELS from the HR-STEM work acknowledged, XPS, or STM defect counts). Otherwise the main advantage claimed over Fe(Te,Se) rests on an assumption.

14. The layer assignment of the two branches is inferred, not measured. The tip probes only the top 1QL surface. The two-branch picture should be tested against alternatives: a single proximitized band structure with two gaps, multiband CdGM states, or a Kopnin-Khaymovich-Mel'nikov multi-core vortex in one layer (Ref. 52 describes exactly such a structure). The alignment of the two cores in plane is automatic for one flux line threading both layers, so it does not by itself show a "stacked" structure. Gap-resolved, temperature-dependent maps showing two distinct Tc or Hc2 values would make the assignment stronger.

15. The standard MZM checks are missing. To move from "consistent with" to "evidence for", show the following:
   - tunnelling-barrier (setpoint) dependence of the ZBCP height, i.e. whether it saturates (cf. the quantized-conductance tests in Fe(Te,Se));
   - temperature dependence and field dependence of the ZBCP;
   - spin-polarized or spin-selective Andreev reflection (Ref. 15);
   - comparison with vortices pinned on defects;
   - an explicit trivial-scenario analysis.
   The abstract's "spatially unsplit MZMs" and the text's "compelling spectroscopic evidence" go beyond the data.

16. "MZMs observed in all measured vortices" needs statistics. Give the number of vortices, islands, samples and tips, with a histogram of ZBCP presence, energy and width. This is the central comparison with Fe(Te,Se), where only a fraction of vortices show ZBCPs.

17. "Number controllable" is not shown. Fig. 4B-D show vortex maps at different fields but no spectra. For the one-, two- and three-vortex configurations, show a ZBCP at each vortex. Report island areas and inter-vortex distances relative to the measured ZBCP decay length. Discuss MZM hybridization: in small islands with vortices tens of nm apart, splitting of the zero modes is expected (cf. Refs. 28, 37). The island-size dependence claimed in the abstract does not appear in the main figures.

18. The QPI and PDW analogy is inaccurate. The field-induced PDW halo in Bi2212 (Ref. 46) is a density-wave modulation at a distinct wavevector (period 8a0, half the CDW wavevector), not pair-breaking QPI. Saying the scenario is "the same" is misleading. The QPI argument also needs a check against setpoint (normalization) artefacts, since the setpoint bias (10 mV) lies inside the energy window where the gap changes across the vortex.

19. The moire gap modulation needs a setpoint control. The gap modulation could partly come from the topographic or LDOS corrugation through the constant-current setpoint. Please show normalized spectra or constant-height data, and give the modulation amplitude with errors. Fig. 1D was taken at 1.6 K, while the spectroscopy is said to be at 0.4 K; state the temperature for Fig. 1H-I.

20. The outlook overclaims. Majorana edge modes at the island edge would require 2D topological superconductivity at the edge, and no edge spectroscopy is shown. "Satisfy the basic requirements for braiding MEM by four terminal devices" and "a controlled platform for fuse MZMs" (abstract) are not supported by any data in the paper and should be toned down or removed.

C. PRESENTATION

21. Methods are referenced ("see details in Methods") but missing: MBE conditions, the Te flux during Bi2Te3 growth (central to the mechanism), tip preparation and calibration, the lock-in amplitude convention (rms or peak), and the fitting procedures (multigap Dynes, multi-Lorentzian, BdG). The Supplementary Material was not available to me, and several key arguments depend on it.

22. Data and code are available only "upon reasonable request". For a Majorana claim, the raw spectral maps and fitting code should be deposited.

23. The English needs editing throughout, e.g. "In many of these platform", "To detailed explore", "provides the microscopic origin", "topological quantum qubit".

RECOMMENDATION

Major revision. The platform may well be valuable, but the paper should: fix the numerical inconsistencies (A1-A6); resolve the two internal contradictions about superconductivity in FeTe and topology in 1QL Bi2Te3; give vortex statistics and spectra for the multi-vortex configurations; add the standard MZM checks; and rephrase the MZM, "number control", MEM and fusion language as "consistent with" until those checks are in. The title should change with it.

TL;DR: The most concrete problems are numerical. The Bi2Te3-branch level spacing is printed as 0.03 ueV but kF/EF/Delta give ~0.01-0.03 meV. kF ~ 0.3 nm^-1 with EF ~ 300 meV implies an implausible ~10 eV*A velocity. The stated ~0.7 meV CdGM spacing does not match the fitted levels (~0.8-1.2 meV). The levels (L2/L1 ~ 2.3-2.5) do not clearly separate integer from half-integer quantization. L+/-2 (~2.0 meV) sits above the 1.85 meV inner gap. The 2.9 nm moire period matches neither 7 a_BT nor 7 a_FeTe. The physics also contradicts itself (FeTe "not superconducting" yet hosts the MZM; 1QL Bi2Te3 gapped yet its "Dirac surface states" are invoked). There are no vortex statistics, no spectra for multi-vortex islands, and none of the standard MZM checks. Major revision.
