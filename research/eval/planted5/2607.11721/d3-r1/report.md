## Related work

- Ben Shalom et al. 2016, "Quantum oscillations of the critical current and high-field superconducting proximity in ballistic graphene", <https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.03286>. Reports random pockets of supercurrent above 1 T in ballistic graphene, each carrying close to the single-mode limit. The authors attribute them to near-edge Andreev states that end at 2rc = L.
- Amet et al. 2016, "Supercurrent in the quantum Hall regime", <https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.09083>. Shows supercurrent through a quantum Hall region in graphene up to 2 T. A small dc bias suppresses it and reveals the plateaus underneath.
- Draelos et al. 2018, "Investigation of Supercurrent in the Quantum Hall Regime in Graphene Josephson Junctions", <https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.01447>. Uses junctions whose mesa extends past the contacts on one side, to tell supercurrent along the free edges apart from modes along the superconductor interface.
- Indolese et al. 2018, "Signatures of van Hove singularities probed by the supercurrent in a graphene - hBN superlattice", <https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.10184>. In a graphene/hBN superlattice junction, the field dependence of the supercurrent reveals edge currents at the van Hove singularities.
- Barrier et al. 2024, "One-dimensional proximity superconductivity in the quantum Hall regime", <https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14451>. Domain walls in minimally twisted bilayer graphene carry supercurrent at fields close to the electrodes' upper critical field, with a critical current that doesn't oscillate.
