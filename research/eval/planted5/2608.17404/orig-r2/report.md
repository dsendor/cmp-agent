## Related work

- Valentini et al. 2021, "Nontopological zero-bias peaks in full-shell nanowires induced by flux tunable Andreev states", <https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.02348>. Dots in the tunnel junction host flux-tunable Andreev levels that give robust zero-bias peaks without topology, and short tunneling regions show no subgap states.
- Hess et al. 2023, "Trivial Andreev band mimicking topological bulk gap reopening in the non-local conductance of long Rashba nanowires", <https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03507>. A trivial Andreev band can produce an apparent gap closing and reopening in nonlocal conductance, alongside trivial end ZBPs.
- Pan and Das Sarma 2020, "Physical mechanisms for zero-bias conductance peaks in Majorana nanowires", <https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.11413>. Strong disorder generically gives trivial ZBPs, and end-to-end correlation has limits for telling topological from trivial, especially in short wires.
- Ciaccia et al. 2023, "Gate Tunable Josephson Diode in Proximitized InAs Supercurrent Interferometers", <https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.00484>. A trivial two-junction InAs SQUID shows a gate-tunable diode efficiency up to about 30%. η is zero at half flux, and its sign mirrors about half flux when the junction asymmetry changes sign.
- Hou et al. 2023, "Ubiquitous Superconducting Diode Effect in Superconductor Thin Films", <https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.09276>. Plain Nb and V films show a diode effect from asymmetric vortex barriers and Meissner screening, at fields as small as 1 Oe.
