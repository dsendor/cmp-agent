**The claim, as I read it.** Your three columnar BiSbTeSe2/Nb nano-SQUIDs (devices A, B and C) show several things. Ic oscillates with period h/2e. The superconducting diode effect reverses sign at half-integer flux (Figs. 1e, 3a, S2a, S4). The local and nonlocal tunneling spectra jump at the same fields (Figs. 1f-g, 3b-e, S2b-e). You read these together as a flux-driven global transition consistent with topological superconductivity. Zero-bias peaks appear inside the predicted topological window in device A (Fig. 1f-g). They also appear in trivial windows and at zero field in device B (Figs. 3, 4) and device C (Fig. S3), and you read those as at least partly driven by disorder.

They show that two trivial junctions with non-sinusoidal current-phase relations give a diode effect that vanishes at every Φ = nΦ0/2. The sign reversal at half-integer flux therefore needs the surface-state argument to carry the topological reading.

## Related work

- Nikodem et al. 2024, "Topological Insulator nano-SQUID: Flux-tunable platform for topological superconductivity", <https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.07993>. Predicts a topological phase recurring in flux for asymmetric junctions, and measures supercurrent carried only by the top and bottom surfaces.
- Schluck et al. 2024, "Robust gap closing and reopening in topological-insulator Josephson junctions", <https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.08265>. Reports tunnel-probe gap closing at phase (2n+1)π in a TI junction, robust over a wide range of chemical potential.
- Valentini et al. 2020, "Nontopological zero-bias peaks in full-shell nanowires induced by flux tunable Andreev states", <https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.02348>. Shows robust zero-bias peaks in flux lobes coming from dots in the tunnel junction region.
- Hess et al. 2022, "Trivial Andreev band mimicking topological bulk gap reopening in the non-local conductance of long Rashba nanowires", <https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03507>. Shows that a trivial Andreev band plus trivial end peaks can mimic a nonlocal gap reopening.
- Souto, Leijnse and Schrade 2022, "The Josephson diode effect in supercurrent interferometers", <https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.04469>. Shows flux-tunable diode effects up to about 40% in interferometers of trivial junctions with skewed current-phase relations.
