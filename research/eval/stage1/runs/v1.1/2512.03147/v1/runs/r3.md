Review outline:

**1. Significance and novelty**

The manuscript reports the first synthesis of graphite intercalated with α-RuCl3, grown by two-zone vapour transport with a Cl2 source from photolysed AgCl. Stage 2 and stage 4 compounds are identified by X-ray diffraction, Shubnikov–de Haas (SdH) oscillations with frequencies of about 170–176 T (and 91 T in stage 4) are measured, and the results are interpreted with unfolded non-spin-polarized GGA band structures of model stacking sequences. A bulk graphite intercalation compound built from a Kitaev candidate is a new and useful materials result, and it gives a three-dimensional counterpart to the graphene/α-RuCl3 heterostructures. The broader claim that the system is a platform for exotic correlated, magnetic and topological phases is not yet backed by any measurement of those properties.

**2. Potential reasons for acceptance**

- New compound with a reproducible-sounding recipe. The paper also reports a non-obvious practical finding: excess Cl2 is required for intercalation.
- Two distinct stages are obtained by controlling reaction time. Their c-axis periods (25.4 Å and 58.2 Å) are consistent with standard staging arithmetic for a metal-chloride gallery of about 9.4 Å plus 3.35 Å graphene spacings: 2 × 12.7 Å and 3 × 19.4 Å.
- The SdH frequencies are about 40 times those of the parent graphite, which shows strong hole doping and a reconstructed Fermi surface.
- The DFT work systematically compares A|A, AB|AB, ABAB|ABAB and ABCA|ABCA stackings and benchmarks the strained reduced cell against a larger unstrained cell.

**3. Concerns**

**A. Must address**

1. **The DFT description of α-RuCl3 is not adequate for a quantitative Fermi-surface comparison, and the agreement with experiment relies on a fitted Fermi-level shift.**
   - *Method.* All calculations are non-spin-polarized GGA without Hubbard U or spin–orbit coupling (Methods; captions of Figs. 4, 5, S3, S5). At that level α-RuCl3 is a metal: Fig. S5b,c shows Ru t2g bands crossing E_F.
   - *Why it matters.* The graphene-to-RuCl3 charge transfer is then set by pinning to a spurious metallic Ru band rather than to the Mott-gap/upper-Hubbard-band alignment. Yet that charge transfer is the single quantity that fixes the SdH frequencies. The earlier graphene/α-RuCl3 work the authors cite (Refs. 12, 13, 35) treats RuCl3 with U, SOC and magnetic order for this reason.
   - *Ru-derived pockets.* The Fermi surfaces in Fig. 5 are projected onto graphene only, so any Ru-derived Fermi sheets in the calculation are silently dropped.
   - *Mismatch with the data.* Without adjustment the computed frequencies do not match: AB|AB gives 131 and 444 T against the measured 170 T. A rigid 0.1 eV shift is then introduced as a free parameter, and only for the S4 stackings. The shifted results (27/183 T for ABAB|ABAB; 126/214 T for ABCA|ABCA) still do not reproduce the measured pair of 91 and 176 T. No shifted result is given for S2, and the sign of the shift (more or less hole doping) is not stated.
   - *What the authors should do.*
     - Repeat at least the AB|AB and one stage-4 calculation in GGA+U(+SOC) with zigzag or ferromagnetic order, and report the charge transfer per graphene layer and the resulting frequencies with no free shift.
     - Show the full Fermi surface, not only the graphene projection.
     - State the shift direction and the frequencies for all stackings with the same shift.
     - If agreement then remains qualitative, the abstract and conclusions ("consistent with the experimental SdH frequencies", "governed by the stacking sequence") should be reworded to match.

2. **The SdH frequencies are not tied to the intercalated phase by the necessary controls, and the carrier density is not measured independently.**
   - *Missing controls.* The key observation is that β ≈ 170–176 T appears in both S2 and S4 (Fig. 2g,h). Three things are missing:
     - SdH data for the Cl2-annealed graphite control. Fig. 2b shows only its ρ(T), and that curve differs from pristine Kish graphite, so Cl or Cl2 doping of graphite without RuCl3 is a live alternative.
     - Hall data. Transverse resistivity was measured and antisymmetrized (Methods) but is not shown. Its sign and magnitude would directly test hole doping. For four-fold degenerate Dirac pockets, 170 T corresponds to about 1.6×10^13 cm^-2.
     - Evidence that the specific pieces cut for transport were single-stage. The paper itself notes residual graphite, mixed staging at longer reaction times, and that cutting damages samples.
   - *Why it matters.* The near-identical β in two stages that DFT says should differ is striking. It suggests a common origin: graphene layers bounding a RuCl3 gallery, a common impurity phase, or Cl-doped graphite.
   - *What the authors should do.* Show:
     - SdH and Hall data on the Cl2-annealed control under the same conditions;
     - Hall data for S2 and S4, with the extracted carrier densities compared with the Onsager-derived pocket areas and the Bader charge transfer;
     - XRD (00l) of the same pieces used for transport, or at least of pieces from the same batch.
   - *Interpretation.* The authors should also say explicitly how a stage-independent β fits their stacking-dependent interpretation.

**B. Should address**

3. **Experimental and calculated interlayer geometry do not agree.**
   - *What the paper says.* The calculations fix d_Gr–RuCl3 = 3.60 Å as "the average experimental value" and d_Gr–Gr = 3.54 Å (Methods). XRD gives only one c-period per stage, so these two distances cannot be separated from the data.
   - *The arithmetic.* The measured periods imply a graphene–graphene spacing close to 3.35 Å and a gallery of about 9.4 Å. The A|A cell in Fig. S4a has c ≈ 10.0 Å. With 3.54 Å inner spacings, the model S2 and S4 periods would be about 27 Å and 60.6 Å rather than 25.4 Å and 58.2 Å.
   - *Other inconsistencies.* The SI quotes 3.765 Å and 10.241 Å, apparently with different definitions.
   - *Why it matters.* Fig. S4a shows that the charge transfer depends on the gallery height, and the paper attributes the residual mismatch to interlayer spacing. The geometry therefore matters for the comparison with experiment.
   - *What the authors should do.* Define each distance once and use the measured c-periods to constrain the model cells. Alternatively, show the frequency dependence over the experimentally allowed range.

4. **The stage and stacking assignment rests on analogy, and the commensurate supercell is not demonstrated.**
   - *The supercell.* The 29.6 Å in-plane cell is the coincidence 12 × a_graphite ≈ 5 × a_RuCl3. The single-crystal reflections in Fig. S1 carry indices that are multiples of 5 (RuCl3 sublattice) or belong to graphite. That is equally consistent with the incommensurate structure assumed in assumption 1 of the Methods, which contradicts the supercell statement in the main text.
   - *Stacking.* No l ≠ 0 reflections are accessible, so AB versus ABC stacking in S4 ("AB|BCAB|BC or AB|BABA|AB", Table I) is not determined.
   - *Composition.* No compositional analysis is given (EDS/WDS, mass uptake, Ru:Cl ratio, absence of Ag). Nor is there evidence that the intercalant is the α polytype rather than another RuClx layer.
   - *What the authors should do.*
     - Report the composition and the in-plane epitaxial relation between graphite and RuCl3 reflections.
     - Address stage purity: (00l) linewidths and the absence of neighbouring-stage peaks.
     - Present the supercell as a description of in-plane registry, not as a demonstrated commensurate structure.

5. **The "harmonic" assignments are questionable.**
   - *The numbers.* In S2, 2β is labelled 359 T, while 2 × 170 = 340 T. With a 4–9 T window the FFT resolution is about 1/Δ(1/B) ≈ 7 T, so the 19 T difference is well beyond resolution. In S4, 347 T sits against 2 × 176 = 352 T.
   - *Why it matters.* The 359 T peak may be a second pocket, perhaps related to the higher-frequency pocket DFT predicts for AB|AB. That would change the comparison in concern 1.
   - *What the authors should do.*
     - Show Δρ against 1/B, and state the background polynomial order and the FFT window and windowing function.
     - Check whether the peak positions shift with the field range.
     - Show the temperature dependence of each peak. Lifshitz–Kosevich effective masses for β, α and the 359 T feature would also give an independent test of the computed bands (Dirac-like against bilayer-like dispersion).

6. **There is no angle dependence to support the "three-dimensional" Fermi-surface picture.** All SdH data are taken with B ∥ c. Rotating the field and plotting F(θ) would show whether the pockets are 2D cylinders confined to the graphene layers, as the model implies, and whether interlayer coupling through RuCl3 produces any warping. That is relevant to the "three-dimensional platform" framing.

7. **The headline framing ("exotic quantum phases", "magnetically active", "flat-band physics") is not tested.**
   - *What is missing.* No magnetization, susceptibility or specific-heat data show whether the intercalated RuCl3 orders, near 7 K in bulk α-RuCl3 or otherwise. The low-temperature ρ(T) is not discussed for anomalies.
   - *The flat bands.* In the calculation they lie roughly 0.5 eV away from E_F after hole doping (Fig. 4c,d), so the link to rhombohedral flat-band physics is not supported by the authors' own bands.
   - *What the authors should do.* Add at least χ(T) and a low-temperature ρ(T) close-up, or else temper the title, abstract and conclusions.

8. **Sample count and reproducibility are not stated.** The paper does not say how many S2 and S4 crystals were grown and measured, whether β and α reproduce across samples, or what the spread is. α appears only in S4, and the paper attributes its absence to "sample-dependent" Fermi-level shifts. That attribution needs at least two S4 samples, with and without α, to be credible.

**C. Minor**

9. **Strain values disagree.** The main text quotes about 4% tensile strain on α-RuCl3 in the reduced cell, while the SI says about 1%. The SI geometry (Ru–Ru 3.443 Å) appears to be the unstrained one. Please reconcile the two.

10. **Pocket counts disagree between pages 5 and 6.** Page 5 says ABAB|ABAB and ABCA|ABCA both "exhibit three hole pockets". Page 6 gives two pockets (70, 296 T) for ABAB|ABAB.

11. **Captions and cross-references.**
    - Fig. S1: the caption calls (a,b) XRD images and (c,d) integrated profiles, but the panels are the other way round. The main text cites S1a,b and S1c,d inconsistently.
    - Fig. S3 is labelled "S1 and S2" although it shows orientations A and B.
    - Table I caption: "α-CuI3" should read α-RuCl3.

12. **Fig. 2 units and normalization.**
    - The axis of Fig. 2c reads "mΩ cm" while the other panels use μΩ cm. Please check, since the Kish graphite values in Fig. 2b are of order μΩ cm.
    - S4 has a residual resistivity roughly 10 times that of S2 (Fig. 2d,e) and its ρ(T) is not shown in Fig. 2b. Please comment, given the stated uncertainty in geometry.

13. **The Cl2-annealing control is under-specified.** "Several days" should be replaced by the actual duration, Cl2 pressure and Cl2 source.

14. **Typos.** "heretostructures", "29.5(A) Å", "Graphte" in Fig. 2a, and the inconsistent italic and roman "ab initio".

**4. Suggestions for improvement**

- Add a figure combining Hall density, Onsager pocket areas and DFT/Bader charge per graphene layer for S2 and S4. This is the most direct quantitative test of the charge-transfer picture.
- Present Δρ(1/B) together with Lifshitz–Kosevich mass fits, and if possible angle-dependent SdH. Together these would turn the SdH section from frequency matching into a Fermi-surface determination.
- Re-run the key calculations with U and SOC and include all bands at E_F. Report results for a range of gallery heights constrained by the measured c-periods.
- Add basic magnetic characterization of the intercalated RuCl3 layers. Without it, the Kitaev and "exotic phase" motivation remains speculative.

**5. Verdict**

Verdict: major revision
