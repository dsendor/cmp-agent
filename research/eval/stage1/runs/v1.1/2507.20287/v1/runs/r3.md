Review outline:

1. Significance and novelty

The paper asks how superconductivity in ambient-pressure La3Ni2O7 thin films on SrLaAlO4 can arise if the bonding d_z2 band (the "γ-pocket") lies below the Fermi level, as some ARPES studies report. Starting from a DFT+U band structure with no γ-pocket, the authors run a weak-coupling multi-orbital RPA and a strong-coupling slave-boson mean-field (SBMF) study of a two-orbital bilayer t-J model. Both give nodeless s± pairing driven by interlayer d_x2-y2 pairing. The question is timely and the two-limit approach is useful. Much of the methodology, however, carries over from the authors' earlier work on the pressurized bulk. The new element is the γ-pocket-free band structure and what it does to the pairing, and that is where the paper's evidence is thinnest.

2. Potential reasons for acceptance

- It addresses an open, much-debated question in a fast-moving field: whether the γ-pocket is needed for pairing in bilayer nickelates.
- The weak-coupling (RPA) and strong-coupling (SBMF) calculations reach the same qualitative answer (s± with dominant interlayer d_x2-y2 pairing), which strengthens the conclusion more than either would alone.
- The TB parameters are given in full (Table I, Eqs. 6–8), so the model is reproducible.
- It makes direct contact with experiment: the Fermi surface is compared with ARPES (Refs. 102, 111), and a computed STM spectrum is compared with Ref. 108 (Fig. 4b).

3. Concerns

A. Must address

1. **The strong-coupling result depends on assuming δ_z = 0, which the paper does not compute.** In Sec. B (p. 5) the paper states: "Due to absence of γ-pocket formed mainly by the d_z2 orbital, we have δ_z = 0, resulting in Δ^z_SC = 0." That assumption decides the outcome: with δ_z = 0 the d_z2 channel cannot superconduct, so d_x2-y2 interlayer pairing dominates by construction. The SBMF itself gives a d_z2 spinon pairing (Δ^z_⊥ = 0.151 eV) about 100 times larger than the d_x2-y2 one (1.28 × 10^-3 eV). Even a small finite δ_z would therefore make the d_z2 channel competitive or dominant. A missing γ-pocket does not by itself make the d_z2 orbital exactly half-filled. Fig. 2(b) shows substantial d_z2 weight on the α and β pockets, and the d_z2 count also depends on the antibonding band and on d_z2–d_x2-y2 hybridization (t^xz terms in Table I).
   - What to do:
     - Report the orbital occupations n_z and n_x from the TB model (Table I) at the chemical potential used.
     - Repeat the SBMF with δ_z set to the computed value, or scan a range of small δ_z, and show how δ_zΔ^z_⊥ compares with δ_xΔ^x_⊥.
     - State δ_x explicitly; it is never given numerically.

2. **The RPA preference for s± over d_x2-y2 is marginal and shown only for one parameter set.** Fig. 3(b) shows the s-wave and d-wave eigenvalues nearly degenerate for U = 1.1–1.25 eV (the text says λ_d is "slightly smaller"). This is at a single Hund's ratio (J_H = U/6), with V fixed by U = V + 2J_H, at one filling and one (unstated) temperature and k-mesh. The paper's final choice of s± then rests partly on an external argument: the ARPES report of no node near the pocket-touching momentum (Ref. 106). This is a central claim, since the abstract says the two approaches "provide consistent results."
   - What to do:
     - Show λ_s and λ_d as functions of J_H/U (for example from 0 to 0.25) and of small changes in filling or chemical potential.
     - Show that the s± leading order is robust, or state clearly the parameter range in which it holds.
     - Show convergence in k-mesh and temperature.
     - If d-wave wins in part of the parameter space, the abstract and conclusions should say so.

B. Should address

3. **The γ-free band structure is not tied quantitatively to experiment and is tested at only one parameter set.** The paper's premise rests on a single DFT+U calculation: U = 3.5 eV, a fixed at the substrate value, a pure La3Ni2O7 bilayer. It places the bonding d_z2 band top about 300 meV below E_F at M, attributed to a larger t^z_⊥ (−0.64 eV) than in previous TB models (Refs. 95, 98).
   - The paper does not explain why its t^z_⊥ differs from those works, which use similar film structures.
   - It does not show how the γ-band position depends on U, on the c-axis or apical-oxygen geometry, or on Pr/Sr substitution. The experimental films are (La,Pr)3Ni2O7, and Sr interdiffusion from the substrate has been discussed.
   - It does not compare the 300 meV offset with the binding energy of the bonding d_z2 band top measured in Refs. 102 and 111.
   - What to do:
     - Add the U dependence of the γ-band top.
     - Compare the offset with the ARPES value. If the offset is overestimated, show that the pairing results survive when the band is placed at the experimental energy (for example by tuning t^z_⊥ or ε_z).
     - Ideally, show how the leading pairing evolves as the γ-pocket is continuously removed. That would directly support the title's claim that the γ-pocket is not needed.

4. **The STM comparison (Fig. 4b) does not discriminate s± from other states, and its parameters are not stated.** The RPA fixes the gap's form factor, not its amplitude. The paper does not say how the gap magnitude, the broadening (0+) and the vertical normalization in Fig. 4(b) were chosen; they appear to be adjusted to match Ref. 108. A local DOS from Eq. (13) does not depend on the relative sign of the gap between the α and β pockets, so an s++ gap with the same magnitudes would give the same curve. The statement that the spectrum indicates "the possibility of the s±-wave pairing," and the Discussion's statement that the results are "consistent with STM," therefore carry little weight on the pairing symmetry.
   - What to do:
     - State all parameters used in Fig. 4(b).
     - Overlay the spectra for s++ and the subleading d_x2-y2 state computed the same way.
     - Present the STM agreement as consistency with a nodeless gap, not as support for s± specifically.

5. **The magnitude of the SBMF gap is not compared with the measured gap.** The superconducting d_x2-y2 order parameter is δ_x Δ^x_⊥, with Δ^x_⊥ = 1.28 meV and δ_x < 1. This is apparently well below the gap scale in the STM data the paper compares with (Fig. 4b), and below the bulk-under-pressure value invoked on p. 5. The p. 5 claim that the film gap is "significantly smaller than that in the bulk … consistent with experimental observations" is also made without any numbers in the main text.
   - What to do:
     - Give δ_x and the resulting gap in meV for the film, and the corresponding bulk (30 GPa) values.
     - Compare both with the measured gaps (and with T_c ratios).
     - Discuss whether the roughly 100-fold suppression of Δ^x_⊥ relative to Δ^z_⊥ is physical or a consequence of how J⊥ enters the d_x2-y2 channel (see item 6).

6. **The effective t-J model's handling of Hund's coupling is not justified.** Eq. (3) gives the d_x2-y2 orbital the same interlayer exchange J⊥ as the d_z2 orbital (J⊥ ≈ 2J∥ ≈ 0.4 eV), described as "transmitted" through Hund's coupling. How much exchange is transferred in a strong-coupling derivation depends on J_H relative to U and on the occupation of the d_x2-y2 orbital. The model also omits any explicit interorbital spin coupling (S_x·S_z on-site) that would encode the Hund's rule constraint. The choice J⊥ ≈ 2J∥ via J ∝ t² uses t^z_⊥ and t^x_1 but neglects t^xz contributions.
   - What to do:
     - Justify the effective J⊥ for the d_x2-y2 channel, for example from a perturbative estimate or a citation that derives it.
     - Show how Δ^x_⊥, and the s± result, change when J⊥ for the x orbital and J∥ are varied around the chosen values.

7. **Results that the conclusions rely on are deferred to a Supplementary Material that is not provided.** The text points to the Supplementary Material for "the complete set of obtained mean-field spinon order parameters" and for the "numerical results" of the bulk 30 GPa comparison. That comparison underpins the Results' claim that the film gap is smaller because of reduced hoppings and the missing γ-pocket. Without these data the two-factor explanation on p. 5 cannot be checked.
   - What to do: include the bulk TB parameters, the order parameters and the gap comparison, at least as a table.

8. **The orbital and bonding character of the α and β pockets, which sets the s± sign structure, is not explained.** In the SBMF, an interlayer s-wave pairing gives a sign change only if α and β have opposite bonding/antibonding (k_z = 0/π) character. Fig. 2(b) shows only the d_z2 / d_x2-y2 orbital weight.
   - What to do: show or state the layer-parity character of each pocket. This would make clear why the RPA and SBMF sign structures agree, and why the RPA spin susceptibility peaks at Q ≈ (0.64π, 0.64π).

C. Minor

9. **Several model and calculation details are missing:**
   - the electron filling (per Ni) and chemical potential of the TB model; the large on-site energies ε_z = 9.10 eV and ε_x = 9.90 eV in Table I imply an unstated μ;
   - the RPA temperature, k-mesh and number of pairing eigenvectors examined;
   - the SBMF temperature, k-mesh and self-consistency tolerance;
   - the DFT treatment of magnetism (spin-polarized or not) and whether it is a slab or a bulk cell.

10. **The STM calculation's equations are presented inconsistently.** Eq. (2) and Eq. (9) are identical. Eq. (12) introduces u_km and v_km with a site-diagonal sum but no normalization. The connection between the band-resolved Δ_km and the real-space RPA gap of Fig. 4(a) should be stated.

11. **The legend of Fig. 4(b) gives "arXiv:2506.01788"; it should cite Ref. [108].** The y-axis normalization in that panel should also be stated.

12. **The paper reports s±, but the reasons Fig. 3(d) (d-wave) is shown and the Ref. 106 argument should be stated once, early.** At present, "This is inconsistent with the ARPES results" (p. 3) appears before the reader knows which state is being ruled out.

13. **Small editing errors and organization:**
    - "spionon" → "spinon" (p. 4);
    - "ab inito" → "ab initio" (p. 5);
    - Sections A and B sit under Results while C–G sit under Methods, but the lettering runs continuously;
    - the title and text say La3Ni2O7, while the experiments cited are on (La,Pr)3Ni2O7; this should be noted explicitly.

14. **The Fig. 1(c) caption refers to "red and blue markers," while the legend uses squares and circles.** Matching the descriptions would help.

4. Suggestions for improvement

- Add a figure that tunes the γ-band top continuously from above to below E_F (via ε_z or t^z_⊥) and tracks the leading RPA eigenvalue and symmetry, and the SBMF order parameters. This would turn "pairing without the γ-pocket" from one data point into a demonstrated trend.
- Report and use the computed d_z2 occupation in the SBMF, rather than assuming exact half filling.
- For the STM comparison, show the s±, s++ and d-wave spectra together. Suggest a phase-sensitive experiment, such as quasiparticle-interference sign analysis or Josephson junctions, that could tell them apart.
- Provide the Supplementary Material, including the bulk-versus-film comparison, with quantitative gap and T_c estimates.

5. Verdict: major revision
