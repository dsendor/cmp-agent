Review outline:

1. Significance and novelty

The paper asks what pairs the electrons in ambient-pressure La3Ni2O7 thin films on SrLaAlO4 if, as some ARPES groups report, there is no γ-pocket from the bonding d_z2 band. It builds a DFT+U band structure with no γ-pocket and a four-orbital bilayer tight-binding model, then studies pairing two ways: multi-orbital RPA (weak coupling) and slave-boson mean field on a two-orbital bilayer t-J model (strong coupling). Both give interlayer s± pairing that lives mainly in the d_x2-y2 orbital, with opposite gap signs on the α and β pockets. The question matters given the conflicting ARPES reports. The methods and the general idea that Hund's coupling carries interlayer exchange into the d_x2-y2 orbital are not new, including from this group (Refs. 25, 41, 98, 103). The new element is applying them to a film band structure without a γ-pocket. The paper does not say how its conclusions differ from the other film calculations it cites (Refs. 95, 98, 99–105, 110, 114).

2. Potential reasons for acceptance

- It addresses a timely, well-defined question: is the γ-pocket needed for pairing in the films?
- The weak-coupling and strong-coupling routes reach the same pairing structure: interlayer, d_x2-y2-dominated s± with a sign change between α and β (Figs. 3c, 6b).
- The tight-binding model is given in full (Eqs. 4–8, Table I), so others can reproduce it.
- It makes a concrete, testable prediction: a nodeless gap at the momenta where the α and β pockets approach each other. It also attempts a direct comparison with an STM spectrum (Fig. 4b).

3. Concerns

A. Must address

1. **The strong-coupling conclusion is fixed by setting δ_z = 0 by hand.** In the SBMF section (p. 5, left column), the authors argue that because there is no γ-pocket, the d_z2 orbital is exactly half-filled. They therefore set δ_z = 0, which makes Δ_SC^z = δ_z Δ_spinon^z vanish identically.
   - **Why this does not follow.** No γ-pocket means the *bonding* d_z2 band is full. It does not mean the d_z2 orbital filling is exactly 1. Fig. 2b and the text say β has mixed orbital content, so the antibonding d_z2 states are partly occupied, and t_∥^xz hybridization moves weight between orbitals.
   - **How much it matters.** The spinon amplitudes reported on p. 4 are Δ_⊥^z = 1.51 × 10⁻¹ eV and Δ_⊥^x = 1.28 × 10⁻³ eV, two orders of magnitude apart. A d_z2 hole density as small as about 1% would make δ_z Δ_⊥^z comparable to, or larger than, δ_x Δ_⊥^x. The headline strong-coupling statements ("the d_x2-y2 orbital electrons form interlayer s-wave pairing" and "the dominant superconducting channel becomes the interlayer pairing of the d_x2-y2 orbitals") would then no longer hold.
   - **What to do.**
     - Compute the orbital-resolved fillings n_z and n_x from the TB model of Table I at the chemical potential used, and report δ_x and δ_z.
     - Redo the SBMF with δ_z set to the value implied by the band structure, or show Δ_SC^x and Δ_SC^z over a plausible range of δ_z.
     - State the total electron count per Ni used in the RPA and SBMF.

B. Should address

2. **The SBMF energy scale is not reported and may be inconsistent with experiment.** With Δ_⊥^x = 1.28 meV and δ_x < 1, the superconducting order parameter δ_x Δ_⊥^x is at most about 1 meV. That looks small next to T_c ≈ 40 K (k_B T_c ≈ 3.4 meV) and next to the gap features of tens of meV on the Fig. 4b axis.
   - The bulk comparison ("the superconducting gap in the film system at AP is significantly smaller than that in the bulk", p. 5) is stated without numbers; they are deferred to the Supplementary Material.
   - The authors should:
     - give δ_x and the resulting Δ_SC^x for the film and for the 30 GPa bulk in the main text;
     - give the ratio between the two, and compare it with the ratio of experimental T_c values;
     - comment on whether the absolute scale is meaningful at mean-field level.

3. **The STM comparison (Fig. 4b) has no stated parameters and no discriminating test.** The abstract and Discussion say the results are consistent with STM (Ref. 108). However:
   - RPA fixes only the gap's form, not its size. The paper does not say how the magnitude of Δ_km in Eq. (12) was chosen, what broadening replaces i0⁺, or how the theory and experimental curves were normalized and aligned.
   - The y-axis of Fig. 4b is unlabelled.
   - Eq. (13) is a bulk k-summed DOS. It ignores the surface-layer selectivity of STM and orbital-dependent tunnelling matrix elements.
   - The authors should:
     - list all parameters entering the curve, and say which were adjusted to match the data;
     - show what the subleading d_x2-y2 solution of Fig. 3d, and a sign-preserving s++ gap, give under the same settings, to establish whether the spectrum actually discriminates s± from the alternatives.

   Without these, "the shape of the theoretical curve is similar" cannot be read as support for s±.

4. **The γ-pocket-free band structure rests on one parameter choice.** Everything follows from one DFT+U calculation: U = 3.5 eV, with a and c fixed to experiment and internal positions relaxed. This gives the bonding d_z2 band top about 300 meV below E_F at M, and t_⊥^z = −0.64 eV. The authors note this hopping is larger than in earlier TB models (Refs. 95, 98).
   - Whether the films have a γ-pocket is exactly what is disputed. The position of this band is known to depend on U, on the c-axis length and on apical-oxygen relaxation.
   - The paper does not compare the 300 meV binding energy quantitatively with the ARPES it cites as support (Refs. 102, 111).
   - It also does not say whether the DFT is non-magnetic, and the calculation omits the Pr substitution of the measured (La,Pr)3Ni2O7.
   - The authors should:
     - show how the bonding d_z2 band top and t_⊥^z vary with U (for example 0–5 eV);
     - compare with the measured binding energy of the bonding band;
     - state what parts of the pairing result survive if the band top lies closer to E_F.

5. **RPA s- and d-wave are nearly degenerate.** In Fig. 3b, λ_s and λ_d differ by only a few percent over the whole U range shown, at a single ratio J_H = U/6. The choice of s± is then made partly by appeal to ARPES (Ref. 106, p. 3), not by the calculation itself.
   - The paper should:
     - show the λ_s/λ_d ordering as J_H/U varies, for example 0.1–0.25;
     - show it under small changes of filling or of t_⊥^z;
     - report the RPA temperature and k-mesh.

   The claim that "both approaches give s±" needs this, because the leading RPA instability could change with modest parameter changes.

6. **"γ-pocket not necessary" is asserted without a same-model comparison.** The Discussion concludes that "the presence of the γ-pocket is not necessary to the pairing mechanism". The paper shows that pairing occurs without the pocket. It does not show what the pocket changes when it is present within the same framework; the 30 GPa bulk results are only in the Supplementary Material.
   - The authors could raise the bonding d_z2 band through E_F in the film TB model (a rigid shift of ε_z, or the bulk-like t_⊥^z). They could then compare λ, the leading pairing symmetry and the real-space amplitudes against Fig. 3 and Fig. 4a.
   - This would also clarify how the work differs from the earlier film calculations by the same and other groups (Refs. 98, 103, 104, 110, 114).

7. **The effective model is not tied to the DFT-derived hoppings.** In Eq. (3), the full J_⊥ is applied to the d_x2-y2 spins as if Hund's coupling passed it on without loss. J_⊥ ≈ 2J_∥ ≈ 0.4 eV is adopted rather than derived from Table I. No intralayer J for d_z2 is included.
   - Using (t_⊥^z/t_1^x)² ≈ 1.8 from Table I gives roughly this ratio, so the authors should say so explicitly.
   - They should also state how sensitive the Δ_⊥^x/Δ_∥^x hierarchy is to the effective interlayer coupling acting on the d_x2-y2 orbital. This coupling could plausibly be reduced by a Hund's-coupling factor, and the d_x2-y2 pairing is already weak (concern 2).

8. **The model is a bulk crystal at film lattice constants, not a film.** The DFT uses a periodic bulk cell with in-plane strain from SrLaAlO4, but the films are a few unit cells thick. Several effects are not discussed:
   - the interface and surface;
   - possible oxygen off-stoichiometry and the resulting doping;
   - Pr substitution.

   At minimum, the paper should say these are left out and estimate how a modest hole doping (say ±0.05 per Ni) would shift the α and β pockets and the RPA result.

C. Minor

9. **Wording of the γ-pocket on p. 2.** The text says "the d_z2 band does not cross the Fermi level at the Γ point, and thus forms no electron pocket." In the bulk, the γ-pocket is a hole-like pocket around M, as the preceding sentence about the band top at M implies. Please correct or clarify.

10. **Typos and labels in Eq. (12) and Fig. 4b.** Eq. (12): "ε_kα is the m-th eigenvalue" should read ε_km. Fig. 4b needs a y-axis label (normalized dI/dV, in arbitrary units?). The legend should cite Ref. [108] rather than an arXiv number.

11. **Eq. (1) notation.** The V term has no spin indices, and the J_H term mixes orbital labels. Please state the conventions explicitly, including the meaning of α in n_iμα.

12. **Spinon amplitude definition.** On p. 4, "Δ_spinon^α = (3/8)J⟨…⟩" does not say which J (J_∥ or J_⊥) belongs to which channel. Please make it consistent with Eqs. (15)–(16).

13. **DFT k-mesh.** The 8 × 8 × 2 mesh is coarse for fixing a band edge about 300 meV below E_F and for the Wannier fit. Please state that the band positions are converged.

14. **Undefined colour scales.** Figs. 3c,d and 6b use colour scales without saying whether the gaps are normalized. Also, the signs on α and β in Fig. 3c are the reverse of those in Fig. 6b. This is harmless, but should be noted as a global phase so readers do not see a contradiction.

15. **Reference list.** Refs. [92]–[114] mix arXiv and journal formats, and some are incomplete (for example Ref. [94], "Nature Materials, 1 (2025)"). Please complete them.

4. Suggestions for improvement

- Report the orbital fillings and δ_x, δ_z from the TB model. Present the SBMF result as a function of δ_z, so the strong-coupling conclusion does not depend on an assumed exact half-filling.
- Add a same-model "with versus without γ-pocket" comparison: shift the bonding d_z2 band through E_F and compare the RPA and SBMF results. This would turn "not necessary" into a quantitative statement about what the γ-pocket does.
- Make the STM comparison reproducible and discriminating by listing all its parameters and adding s++ and d-wave curves.
- Move the key film-versus-bulk numbers from the Supplementary Material into the main text, and set them against the experimental T_c ratio.

5. Verdict

Verdict: major revision
