Review outline:

1. Significance and novelty

The paper asks how bilayer La3Ni2O7 thin films at ambient pressure can superconduct when some ARPES studies find no γ-pocket (the bonding Ni-dz2 band stays below EF). The authors build a DFT+U band structure without the γ-pocket, fit a four-band bilayer tight-binding (TB) model to it, and study pairing two ways: multi-orbital RPA (weak coupling) and slave-boson mean-field (SBMF) theory of a two-orbital bilayer t-J model (strong coupling). Both give s±-wave pairing, with the sign change between the α and β pockets and interlayer dx2-y2 pairing dominant. The question is timely and the two-limit approach is a reasonable strategy. However, the strong-coupling half largely builds its main conclusions into the model, and the weak-coupling half picks s over d by a small margin that is never tested for robustness. As it stands, the novelty is mainly that a previously proposed interlayer dx2-y2 mechanism is applied to a γ-free Fermi surface.

2. Potential reasons for acceptance

- The question is timely and experimentally motivated: ARPES reports conflict on whether the γ-pocket exists in (La,Pr)3Ni2O7/SrLaAlO4 films.
- The DFT → Wannier → TB pipeline is documented well. The full TB Hamiltonian and parameters are given (Eqs. 4–8, Table I), and the DFT, Wannier and TB bands agree near EF (Fig. 2a), so others can reproduce the model.
- Two complementary limits are treated, and they give the same sign structure on the Fermi surface (Fig. 3c vs Fig. 6b).
- A nodeless s± state is consistent with the ARPES finding of no node near where the α and β pockets touch, and a calculated tunneling spectrum is compared with STM data (Fig. 4b).

3. Concerns

A. Must address

1. In the strong-coupling study, the main conclusions are inputs rather than results. In Sec. B, the authors set the dz2 hole doping to δz = 0 "due to absence of the γ-pocket", which forces Δ_SC^z = δz·Δ_spinon^z = 0. This is the step that makes dz2 drop out of superconductivity. Yet the SBMF finds a dz2 spinon pairing (Δ⊥z = 1.51×10⁻¹ eV) about 100 times larger than the dx2-y2 one (Δ⊥x = 1.28×10⁻³ eV).
   - Absence of the γ-pocket does not imply that dz2 is exactly half-filled. Fig. 2b shows strong dz2 weight mixed into both α and β, and the dz2 orbital hybridizes with dx2-y2 through t^xz. The dz2 occupancy of the TB model at the experimental filling can therefore deviate from 1.
   - The interlayer exchange acting on dx2-y2 (the J⊥ term in H_J,x, Eq. 3) is put in by hand as the "Hund's-transmitted" coupling. It is effectively set to the same J⊥ ≈ 0.4 eV used for dz2, without derivation; it is also unclear whether "J⊥" in "J⊥ ≈ 2J∥ ≈ 0.4 eV" refers to the x or the z channel. In a perturbative treatment the transmitted coupling should be reduced by a factor that depends on JH/U and on how localized dz2 is.
   - With δz = 0 and a large interlayer J⊥ on x, an interlayer s-wave dx2-y2 state is close to guaranteed. The statement that the strong-coupling result "independently" confirms the weak-coupling one is therefore weaker than presented.
   - What to do:
     - Compute the orbital-resolved fillings nz and nx from the TB model at the stated electron count, and use δz = 1 − nz.
     - Report δx, which is currently not given.
     - Show how the leading pairing channel and the physical gaps δαΔ⊥α change as δz and the x-channel J⊥ are varied over a physically justified range.
     - Justify the size of the transmitted coupling (for example from second-order perturbation theory in t⊥z with JH).

2. In the RPA, s± wins over d-wave by a small and untested margin. Fig. 3b shows λ_s and λ_d nearly degenerate over the whole range considered, U = 1.10–1.25 eV (about 0.16 vs 0.15 at U = 1.25 eV). The results are shown for one ratio, JH = U/6, and one TB parameter set. Choosing s over d then rests partly on an outside argument: ARPES sees no node.
   - A near-degeneracy like this is often reversed by modest changes in JH/U, the filling, or the position of the bonding dz2 band top. The headline "s± pairing" from the weak-coupling side is therefore not yet established.
   - What to do:
     - Show λ_s and λ_d as functions of JH/U (for example 0.1–0.25).
     - Show them as a function of electron filling (or hole doping), since films are often oxygen-off-stoichiometric and Pr-substituted.
     - Show them under a rigid shift of the bonding dz2 band top (see concern 3).
     - State the electron count and chemical potential used in the RPA, and give the number of k-points and the temperature.
     - Report the s/d margin quantitatively, including how close U is to Uc in each case.

B. Should address

3. The premise of a band structure without the γ-pocket rests on a single DFT+U choice that is not tested. The bonding dz2 band top lies about 300 meV below EF at M (Fig. 1c, Sec. Results), and t⊥z = −0.6408 eV is larger than in earlier TB models.
   - Both features depend on U = 3.5 eV, on the fixed c = 20.72 Å with relaxed internal positions, and on whether the calculation is spin-polarized. None of these is discussed or varied.
   - The ARPES works cited as showing no γ-pocket (Refs. 102, 111) would locate the band top at some specific binding energy, and the paper does not compare against it quantitatively. If the experimental band top is much closer to EF, the γ-band states near M still lie within the energy scales that matter for the RPA susceptibility and pairing kernel.
   - What to do: show the band top at M (and t⊥z) for several U values, and compare it directly with the ARPES binding energy. Then repeat the pairing calculation with the band top placed at the experimental value.
   - As a control for the title claim, also run the same RPA/SBMF pipeline on a variant of the same TB model in which the dz2 band is shifted to just cross EF. This isolates what the γ-pocket changes, rather than comparing with other groups' models.

4. The STM comparison (Fig. 4b) is not specified well enough to support "indicating the possibility of the s±-wave pairing".
   - The RPA gives only the form of the gap, not its size. The gap amplitude used for the spectrum, the broadening (the "i0⁺" in Eqs. 12–13 must be finite to produce Fig. 4b), and any normalization or scaling to the experiment are not stated.
   - The text does not say which sample or conditions Ref. [108] measured (film or bulk, pressure, temperature).
   - The comparison does not test the claim unless the same procedure, with the same amplitude and broadening, gives a visibly worse match for the competing d-wave state (Fig. 3d) and for a sign-preserving s++ state.
   - What to do: provide these competing-state curves and list all parameters used.

5. The size of the strong-coupling gap appears inconsistent with experiment. The physical dx2-y2 gap is δx·Δ⊥x with Δ⊥x = 1.28 meV, which is at most about 1 meV for any δx < 1. The STM data in Fig. 4b show gap features of order 20–30 meV.
   - What to do: give δx and the resulting gap in meV, and comment on whether the SBMF scale (or the J⊥ chosen) is compatible with Tc ≈ 40 K and the measured gap. If the mean-field scale is known to be unreliable, say so and drop the quantitative statements.

6. The comparison between the film and the bulk under 30 GPa (end of Sec. B) is stated only qualitatively: the gap is "significantly smaller", "consistent with experimental observations". The numbers are deferred to a Supplementary Material that is not part of this submission.
   - What to do: give the bulk and film gaps, the orbital fillings and the main hoppings in a main-text table, and say which experimental observations they are consistent with (gap values, Tc ratio).
   - The explanation offered (the x orbital becomes "more overdoped") needs the δx values for both systems.

7. The interaction scales are inconsistent across the three calculations. The DFT+U uses U = 3.5 eV, the RPA uses U ≈ 1.1–1.25 eV (just below Uc = 1.33 eV), and the t-J model uses J∥ ≈ 0.2 eV and J⊥ ≈ 0.4 eV. Using U in DFT+U and then again in the RPA also raises a double-counting question.
   - What to do: briefly justify why the RPA U is a reasonable effective interaction for this band structure, and why the t-J exchange values follow from the same microscopic parameters (for example J = 4t²/U with which U).

8. The real-space pairing amplitudes from RPA (Fig. 4a, Sec. A) need context. The RPA eigenvector is defined only up to normalization, and its real-space decomposition depends on the basis and gauge.
   - What to do: say how the amplitudes Δ⊥x, Δ0z and the others were extracted (Fourier transform of the orbital-basis gap at the Fermi surface or over the whole Brillouin zone), and check that the dominance of Δ⊥x persists for the other U values in Fig. 3b.

C. Minor

9. Several pieces of the SBMF setup are missing from the main text and Methods: the electron count per bilayer, the temperature, the k-mesh and convergence criteria, the values of δx and δz, and whether χ order parameters are kept for both orbitals. The full set of order parameters is deferred to a Supplementary Material that the submission does not include.

10. The onsite energies in Table I (ε_z = 9.0974 eV, ε_x = 9.8963 eV) are absolute Wannier energies. Give the Fermi level (or μ) on the same scale so that the orbital splitting and filling can be read off. Also note that t⊥1^x = t⊥2^x = 0.

11. The DFT details omit whether the calculation is spin-polarized, whether spin–orbit coupling is included, which DFT+U flavour is used (Dudarev or Liechtenstein), and why the 8×8×2 k-mesh is adequate for the Fermi-surface-sensitive Wannier fit.

12. Fig. 3a: state the quantity plotted (the largest eigenvalue of the χ matrix, or the trace) and its units. The colour scale 4–12 has no label.

13. Section labelling runs from A and B in Results straight into C–G in Methods. Relabel the Methods subsections separately. Eqs. (2) and (9) are duplicates, and the text could refer back instead.

14. Fig. 4b: label the vertical axis (dI/dV or A(ω), normalized) and state the normalization.

15. Language: "a lot of interests", "Then some teams study", "it is needed to explain", "pairing nature". Also, "s-wave pairing dominates over other pairing symmetries" (Sec. A) overstates the near-degenerate result of Fig. 3b.

4. Suggestions for improvement

- Add a controlled comparison within one model: the same TB model with the bonding dz2 band top moved from about −300 meV through EF, with λ_s, λ_d and the SBMF gaps tracked. This would directly answer the title question, "what changes without the γ-pocket".
- Replace the imposed δz = 0 with fillings computed self-consistently from the TB model, and show a phase diagram in the (δz, J⊥x) plane.
- Make the STM comparison discriminating: overlay the s±, d and s++ spectra computed with identical parameters.
- Bring the quantitative film-versus-bulk comparison into the main text with a short table.

5. Verdict

Verdict: major revision
