A. Must address

1. The strong-coupling conclusion that interlayer d_x2-y2 pairing dominates is imposed by setting δ_z = 0, not derived. In Sec. B the SBMF gives a d_z2 interlayer spinon pairing Δ⊥^z = 0.151 eV, about 100 times larger than the d_x2-y2 amplitude Δ⊥^x = 1.28×10⁻³ eV. The d_z2 channel is then removed only by the statement "Due to absence of the γ-pocket formed mainly by the d_z2 orbital, we have δ_z = 0, resulting in Δ_SC^z = 0." A missing bonding-d_z2 pocket does not imply exactly one electron per d_z2 orbital: Fig. 2(b) shows substantial d_z2 weight on the α- and β-pockets, the antibonding d_z2-derived states mix with d_x2-y2, and t∥^xz = 0.216 eV hybridizes the two orbitals. Even a d_z2 hole density of a few percent, from hybridization or from Pr/Sr substitution or oxygen doping, multiplied by a spinon amplitude 100 times larger, would give δ_z Δ⊥^z comparable to or larger than δ_x Δ⊥^x and reverse the conclusion about the dominant channel. In addition, holon condensation with δ_z = 0 would renormalize all z-orbital hopping, including t∥^xz, to zero, which would change the Fermi surface onto which the gap is projected in Fig. 6(b). The paper does not report δ_x either, although it sets the gap scale. The authors should compute the orbital occupations n_z and n_x from the TB model of Table I at the stated filling and use the resulting δ_z and δ_x in the SBMF; show Δ_SC^z, Δ_SC^x and their ratio as functions of δ_z (say 0 to 0.1); explain how the renormalized band structure used for Fig. 6(b) is built; and explain why Δ⊥^x is so small relative to Δ⊥^z when the same J⊥ acts on both. The claim of a dominant interlayer d_x2-y2 channel in the strong-coupling limit holds only if these checks come out in its favour.

B. Should address

2. The absence of the γ-pocket, the starting point of the whole paper, comes from a single DFT+U calculation whose sensitivity is never tested (Results, Figs. 1(c) and 2(a), Methods C). The calculation uses PBE+U with U = 3.5 eV, lattice constants fixed at a = 3.756 Å and c = 20.72 Å, an ideal I4/mmm bilayer with 180° Ni-O-Ni bonds, and stoichiometric La3Ni2O7; it is not stated whether it is spin-polarized. A static Hubbard U is known to push the occupied d_z2 bonding band down. The fitted t⊥^z = -0.64 eV is larger than in earlier film models (Refs. 95, 98, including Ref. 98 by some of the same authors, which kept a γ-pocket), and the text says this larger value produces the ~300 meV gap between the band top and E_F at M, but does not explain why it is larger. The ARPES reports the paper cites (Refs. 102, 111) put the band top much closer to E_F than 300 meV, and the paper gives no quantitative comparison with them: the depth of the d_z2 band top, the α and β Fermi momenta and areas, and Fermi velocities. Without such a comparison the nesting vector Q ≈ (0.64π, 0.64π) that drives the RPA result is not anchored to the real film, and it is unclear whether the band structure is a realistic model of γ-free films or an exaggerated limit. The authors should show the d_z2 band-top energy at M, t⊥^z and the Fermi surface as functions of U (for example 0 to 5–6 eV), of the functional, of a magnetic versus non-magnetic DFT state, of the in-plane lattice constant and c, of the Wannierization/apical-oxygen relaxation, and of modest hole doping (rigid band, virtual crystal, or extra apical oxygen as in ozone-annealed films); compare the result quantitatively with the ARPES dispersions; and, if the γ-pocket reappears for reasonable parameters, state the conclusions as conditional on that range.

3. In the RPA the s- and d-wave channels are nearly degenerate, and s± is chosen largely on external grounds (Fig. 3(b), Sec. A). λ_s lies only slightly above λ_d, and only over the window U = 1.10–1.25 eV just below U_c = 1.33 eV, where RPA is least reliable and both eigenvalues are small (about 0.15). The calculation fixes U = V + 2J_H and J_H = U/6, and the RPA temperature and k-mesh are not reported, although both affect a nearly degenerate eigenvalue competition. The statement "the pairing symmetry of the system should be s±" is then justified by the ARPES report of no node near the α/β touching point (Ref. 106), which is experimental evidence rather than an outcome of the calculation. The authors should show λ_s, λ_d and their ratio as functions of J_H/U (for example 0.1–0.25), of filling or chemical potential (for example ±0.1 e per Ni), of the d_z2 band position, of U/U_c, and of the RPA temperature and k-mesh, demonstrating convergence of the ordering; plot orbital- and layer-resolved spin susceptibilities so readers can see which fluctuation channel favours s over d; and plot the gap as a function of Fermi-surface angle on each pocket, with the minimum near the α-β touching points marked, so that "relatively small but not zero" becomes a quantitative statement comparable with the ARPES gap anisotropy. If the ordering is fragile, they should state that weak coupling cannot decide between s± and d-wave for this band structure.

4. The STM comparison (Fig. 4(b), text after Eq. (2), Methods F) does not distinguish s± from other states, yet the paper and abstract present it as support. Eqs. (12)–(13) give a momentum-summed spectrum with band-diagonal coherence factors (assuming no interband pairing), which depends only on |Δ_k| and not on the relative gap sign between pockets, so it cannot separate s± from s++ with the same magnitudes; with amplitude and broadening free, a d-wave state might also look similar. The paper also does not state the gap amplitude used (RPA yields only the form factor, so it was presumably scaled to the data), the finite broadening that replaced i0⁺, the normalization and background treatment, whether the energy axis or gap magnitude was fitted, or whether the asymmetry of the experimental spectrum is captured. The authors should list all parameters and say which were fitted; compute with the same procedure the spectra for the subleading d-wave state of Fig. 3(d) and for a sign-preserving s++ state with matched gap scale, and show them on the same axes; and present the comparison as consistency rather than evidence for s±, moderating the abstract accordingly. A gap-sign-sensitive observable, such as phase-referenced quasiparticle interference or nonmagnetic-impurity bound states, would be the discriminating test and could be computed or proposed.

5. The physical gap scale from the strong-coupling calculation appears far too small to account for the experiments, and the paper gives no gap magnitudes or T_c estimate. The physical d_x2-y2 order parameter is δ_x × Δ⊥^x with Δ⊥^x = 1.28 meV, so it is at most about 1 meV and smaller once δ_x (not given in the main text) is included. The features in the STM spectrum of Fig. 4(b) lie at roughly ±20–35 meV, and the film T_c is about 40 K. The authors should report the physical gap magnitudes in meV from both approaches, give a mean-field T_c estimate or upper bound, compare these quantitatively with the STM and ARPES gaps (including the α/β gap ratio) and with T_c, and explain the discrepancy.

6. The strong-coupling exchange parameters are set by hand, and they generate the key pairing channel (Eq. 3). J⊥ ≈ 2J∥ ≈ 0.4 eV is fixed from J ∝ t² without a derivation of the absolute scale (J∥ = 0.2 eV implies an effective U of about 4.5 eV for t_1^x = 0.48 eV), and it is not checked whether J⊥/J∥ ≈ 2 matches (t⊥^z/t_1^x)² ≈ 1.8 from Table I. The full J⊥ is then assigned to the d_x2-y2 channel, H_J,x, which is the infinite-J_H limit; in Refs. 25 and 41 the transferred coupling depends on J_H and on orbital filling and on how localized the d_z2 moments are. There is also no intralayer exchange for d_z2. Interlayer d_x2-y2 pairing, the main result, and the ratio of interlayer to intralayer pairing (Δ⊥^x = 1.28 meV versus Δ∥^x = 0.011 meV) come directly from these choices. The authors should justify J⊥^x = J⊥^z and show how Δ⊥^x and Δ∥^x vary as J⊥^x/J⊥^z goes from 0 to 1 and as J⊥/J∥ and J∥ vary.

7. Adding a Hubbard U in the RPA on top of DFT+U bands double counts the interaction, and the paper does not discuss it (Sec. A, Methods C). The band structure already contains a static U = 3.5 eV, which is likely what pushes the bonding d_z2 band below E_F (concern 2); the RPA then adds U ≈ 1.1–1.25 eV plus V and J_H. This mixes static mean-field and fluctuation treatments of the same interaction and means the band input and interaction strength are not independent. The authors should explain how double counting is handled, or repeat the RPA on bands from plain GGA or a smaller U (re-tuned to remove the γ-pocket if necessary) to show the pairing symmetry does not depend on this choice, and comment on how physical it is to operate this close to U_c.

8. The claim that "the presence of the γ-pocket is not necessary" is not supported by a controlled comparison with the case that has a γ-pocket (Discussion, last paragraph of Sec. B). Showing pairing without the pocket supports the statement as literally worded, but the paper is motivated by the experimental conflict and attributes the smaller film gap partly to the missing pocket. The authors should, within the same film TB model, shift the d_z2 band continuously (via ε_z or the chemical potential) so that the γ-pocket appears, and track the RPA λ and pairing symmetry and the SBMF δ_x × Δ⊥^x through the Lifshitz transition. This would separate the effect of the γ-pocket from strain-induced changes in hopping and test the title claim directly; otherwise the conclusions should be worded more narrowly.

10. The thin film is modeled as a bulk, stoichiometric crystal under strain. The DFT uses a periodic bulk cell with fixed lattice constants, I4/mmm symmetry and 180° Ni-O-Ni bonds, and the TB model describes a single bilayer without k_z. Real films are (La,Pr)3Ni2O7, a few unit cells thick on SrLaAlO4, with a substrate interface and a surface, possible octahedral tilts, possible Sr interdiffusion, and oxygen stoichiometry set by ozone annealing. Each of these can shift the d_z2 band by an amount comparable to the 300 meV gap, on which the premise depends, or change the interlayer hopping that controls J⊥. The authors should discuss these effects and, where feasible, test the most important one (a slab calculation with the substrate interface, relaxed octahedral tilts, or a rigid-band estimate of how much doping restores the γ-pocket); untested effects should be stated as limitations.

11. The agreement between the RPA and SBMF results is partly built in, and the paper presents it as two independent confirmations. Both methods end up with interlayer d_x2-y2 pairing, and an interlayer s-wave pair projected onto bonding and antibonding bands is s± between α and β essentially by construction (Fig. 6(b)). The agreement therefore checks the pairing channel, not the sign structure independently. The text should say so.
