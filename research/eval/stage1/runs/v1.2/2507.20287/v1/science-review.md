A. Must address

1. The strong-coupling headline result is fixed by the assumption δ_z = 0, which is imposed rather than derived. In Sec. B the paper's own SBMF gives a d_z2 interlayer spinon pairing amplitude Δ⊥^z = 0.151 eV, about 100 times larger than the d_x2-y2 amplitude Δ⊥^x = 1.28×10⁻³ eV. The d_z2 channel is then removed only by the statement "Due to absence of the γ-pocket formed mainly by the d_z2 orbital, we have δ_z = 0, resulting in Δ_SC^z = 0." A missing bonding-d_z2 pocket does not imply exactly one electron per d_z2 orbital: the color scale of Fig. 2(b) shows substantial d_z2 weight on the α- and β-pockets, and t∥^xz = 0.216 eV hybridizes the two orbitals, so n_z in the TB model need not be 1. Even a δ_z of a few percent, from hybridization or from Pr/Sr substitution or oxygen doping, multiplied by a spinon amplitude 100 times larger, would give δ_z Δ⊥^z comparable to or larger than δ_x Δ⊥^x and would reverse the conclusion about the dominant channel. Holon condensation with δ_z = 0 would also renormalize all z-orbital hopping, including t∥^xz, to zero, which changes the Fermi surface onto which the gap is projected in Fig. 6(b). The authors should report the orbital-resolved fillings n_z and n_x from the Table I model at the stated chemical potential (and from the DFT occupancies), use the resulting δ_x and δ_z in the SBMF, report δ_x in the main text since it sets the gap scale, show Δ_SC^z and Δ_SC^x as functions of δ_z (say 0 to 0.1), and explain how the renormalized band structure used for Fig. 6(b) is built. The claim of a dominant interlayer d_x2-y2 channel in the strong-coupling limit holds only if this check comes out in its favour.

B. Should address

2. The γ-pocket-free band structure, on which the whole paper rests, comes from a single DFT+U calculation whose sensitivity is never tested (Results, Figs. 1(c) and 2(a), Methods C). The calculation uses PBE+U with U = 3.5 eV, lattice constants fixed at a = 3.756 Å and c = 20.72 Å, I4/mmm symmetry with 180° Ni-O-Ni bonds, stoichiometric La3Ni2O7, and an apparently non-magnetic state. A static Hubbard U is known to push the occupied d_z2 bonding band down. The fitted t⊥^z = -0.64 eV is, as the text notes, larger than in earlier film models (Refs. 95, 98), and it is what opens the ~300 meV gap between the band top and E_F at M; earlier calculations of the strained film, including Ref. 98 by some of the same authors, kept a γ-pocket, and the paper does not explain the difference (whether it comes from U, the apical-oxygen relaxation or the Wannierization). The ARPES reports cited (Refs. 102, 111) appear to place the band top much closer to E_F than 300 meV, and no quantitative comparison is given with the measured band-top depth, α/β Fermi momenta, Fermi-surface areas or Fermi velocities; without it, the nesting vector Q ≈ (0.64π, 0.64π) that drives the RPA result is not anchored to the real film. Because the paper explicitly frames itself as the γ-absent scenario, its conclusions stand within that scenario, but whether this band structure is a realistic model or an exaggerated limit is untested. The authors should show the d_z2 band-top energy at M, t⊥^z and the Fermi surface as functions of U (0 to 5–6 eV), the functional, magnetic versus non-magnetic DFT states, in-plane strain and c, and modest hole doping (rigid band, virtual crystal, or extra apical oxygen as plausible for ozone-annealed films); compare the result quantitatively with ARPES; and, if the γ-pocket reappears within a reasonable range, state the conclusions as conditional on that range.

3. In RPA the s- and d-wave channels are nearly degenerate, and s± is selected partly on experimental grounds. In Fig. 3(b), λ_s is only slightly above λ_d over the narrow window U = 1.10–1.25 eV just below U_c = 1.33 eV, where RPA is least reliable, and both are small (about 0.1–0.15). The calculation fixes U = V + 2J_H and J_H = U/6, and the RPA temperature and k-mesh are not reported. The conclusion "the pairing symmetry of the system should be s±" is then justified in part by the ARPES report of no node near the α/β touching point (Ref. 106), which is experimental evidence rather than an outcome of the calculation, so the statement that weak and strong coupling "consistently" give s± rests on a margin that may lie within model uncertainty. The authors should show λ_s and λ_d (and their ratio) as functions of J_H/U (e.g. 0.1–0.25), filling (e.g. ±0.1 e per Ni), the d_z2 band position, U/U_c, and the RPA temperature and k-mesh, demonstrating convergence of the ordering; plot orbital- and layer-resolved spin susceptibilities so readers can see which fluctuation channel favours s over d; and, if the ordering is fragile, state that weak coupling cannot decide between s± and d-wave for this band structure.

4. The physical strong-coupling gap appears far too small to account for the observed superconductivity. The physical d_x2-y2 order parameter is δ_x × Δ⊥^x, i.e. at most about 1 meV and smaller once the (unreported) δ_x is included. This is hard to reconcile with the coherence-peak scale of roughly 20–35 meV in the STM spectrum of Fig. 4(b) and with T_c ≈ 40 K, and the paper does not explain why Δ⊥^x is two orders of magnitude smaller than Δ⊥^z when the same J⊥ acts on both. The authors should give the physical gap magnitudes from both approaches in meV, a mean-field T_c estimate or upper bound, a quantitative comparison with the STM and ARPES gaps and the film T_c, and an explanation of the Δ⊥^x/Δ⊥^z ratio.

5. The strong-coupling exchange parameters are imposed by hand, and they produce the key channel. Above Eq. (3), J⊥ ≈ 2J∥ ≈ 0.4 eV is set from J ∝ t² with no derivation of the absolute scale (J∥ = 0.2 eV implies an effective U of about 4.5 eV for t_1^x = 0.48 eV). The full J⊥ is then assigned to the d_x2-y2 channel (H_J,x), which is the infinite-J_H limit; in Refs. 25 and 41 the transferred coupling depends on J_H and on orbital filling. There is also no intralayer exchange for d_z2. Interlayer d_x2-y2 pairing, the main result, and the ratio Δ⊥^x = 1.28 meV to Δ∥^x = 0.011 meV, follow directly from these choices. The authors should justify J⊥^x = J⊥^z, show how Δ⊥^x and Δ∥^x vary as J⊥^x/J⊥^z goes from 0 to 1 and as J⊥/J∥ and J∥ vary, and check whether J⊥/J∥ ≈ 2 matches (t⊥^z/t∥^x)² ≈ 1.8 from Table I.

6. The STM comparison in Fig. 4(b) does not discriminate between pairing states, and its procedure is not stated. Eqs. (12)–(13) give a momentum-summed DOS that depends only on |Δ_k|, not on the relative gap sign between pockets, so it cannot separate s± from s++ with the same magnitudes; with amplitude and broadening free, a d-wave state might also be made to look similar. Eq. (12) also uses band-diagonal coherence factors, assuming no interband pairing. Methods F does not say how the gap amplitude was set (RPA gives only the form factor; the SBMF amplitude is sub-meV), what finite broadening replaced 0⁺, how the curve was normalized or the background treated, or whether the energy axis or gap magnitude was fitted. The authors should list all parameters and say which were fitted; compute s++ and the subleading d-wave state of Fig. 3(d) with the same procedure on the same axes; state whether the asymmetry of the experimental spectrum is captured; present the comparison as consistency rather than evidence for s± (moderating the abstract accordingly); and propose or compute a sign-sensitive observable such as phase-referenced quasiparticle interference or nonmagnetic-impurity bound states.

7. Adding a Hubbard U in RPA on top of DFT+U bands double counts the interaction. The band structure already contains a static U = 3.5 eV, likely what pushes the bonding d_z2 band below E_F (concern 2), and the RPA then adds U ≈ 1.1–1.25 eV plus V and J_H to bands derived from it. This mixes static mean-field and fluctuation treatments of the same interaction and makes the band input and the interaction strength interdependent. The authors should explain how double counting is handled, or repeat the RPA on bands from plain GGA or a smaller U (re-tuned to remove the γ-pocket if necessary) to show the pairing symmetry does not depend on this choice, and comment on how physical it is to operate this close to U_c.

8. The conclusion that "the presence of the γ-pocket is not necessary", and the attribution of the smaller film gap partly to the missing pocket, lack a controlled comparison with the γ-present case. Showing pairing without the pocket supports "not necessary" as stated, but the paper's motivation is the experimental conflict, and separating the effect of the pocket from strain-induced hopping changes requires running the same film TB model with the d_z2 band shifted continuously (via ε_z or μ) through the Lifshitz transition, tracking λ (RPA) and δ_x Δ⊥^x (SBMF). This would test the title claim directly and also yield predictions that ARPES and STM could use to distinguish the two scenarios (e.g. α versus β gap magnitudes, or T_c versus hole doping as the d_z2 band approaches E_F). The authors should add this comparison or narrow the wording of the conclusions.

10. The thin film is modelled as an idealized bulk crystal under strain. The DFT uses a periodic, stoichiometric bulk cell in I4/mmm with 180° Ni-O-Ni angles, and the TB model describes a single bilayer without k_z. The real films are (La,Pr)3Ni2O7, a few unit cells thick on SrLaAlO4, with a substrate interface and surface, possible octahedral tilts, Sr interdiffusion, and oxygen stoichiometry set by ozone annealing. Each of these could shift the d_z2 band by an amount comparable to the 300 meV margin or change the interlayer hopping that controls J⊥. The authors should discuss these effects, give a rigid-band estimate of how much doping would restore the γ-pocket, test the most important one where feasible (e.g. a slab with the substrate interface, or relaxed tilts), and otherwise state them as limitations; the title and abstract should also state explicitly that La3Ni2O7 stands in for (La,Pr)3Ni2O7.

11. The agreement between the two approaches is partly built in and should not be presented as two independent confirmations of the sign structure. Both methods end up with interlayer d_x2-y2 pairing, and an interlayer s-wave pair projected onto bonding and antibonding bands is s± between α and β essentially by construction (Fig. 6(b)). The agreement therefore checks the pairing channel, not the sign structure independently, and the text should say so.
