Review outline:

1. Significance and novelty

The manuscript asks how La3Ni2O7 ultrathin films on SrLaAlO4 superconduct at ambient pressure if the bonding Ni-dz2 band (the gamma pocket) lies below the Fermi level, as some ARPES studies report. The authors build a two-orbital bilayer tight-binding model from strained-lattice DFT+U with no gamma pocket. They then treat it with multi-orbital RPA (weak coupling) and with slave-boson mean-field theory on a two-orbital bilayer t-J model (strong coupling). Both give s+- pairing, dominated by interlayer pairing of dx2-y2 electrons, with opposite gap signs on the alpha and beta pockets. The question is timely. But the combination of methods closely follows the authors' earlier work on the bulk and on films (Refs. 25, 41, 98). The key results depend on inputs that the manuscript does not test: the absence of the gamma pocket, the half-filled dz2 orbital, and the choice between s and d in RPA. The novelty therefore lies mainly in applying established methods to one band-structure scenario, and the paper does not yet show that its conclusions are robust within that scenario.

2. Potential reasons for acceptance

- The question is topical. Experiments disagree on whether the gamma pocket is present in the films, and a careful theory for the gamma-absent case would be useful.
- Two complementary limits (RPA and SBMF on the t-J model) are applied to the same band structure and give the same qualitative answer: interlayer dx2-y2 pairing that projects onto an alpha/beta sign-changing s+- gap.
- The tight-binding parameters are given in full (Table I and Eqs. 6-8), which helps others build on the model.
- The paper offers a physical picture: the localized, half-filled dz2 orbital passes its interlayer superexchange J_perp to the itinerant dx2-y2 orbital through Hund's coupling.
- It makes a direct, if qualitative, comparison with a recent STM spectrum (Fig. 4b).

3. Concerns

A. Must address

1. The gamma-pocket-free band structure, on which the whole paper rests, is not tested for robustness.
   - **What the paper does.** The absence of the gamma pocket comes from a single calculation: non-magnetic(?) GGA+U with U = 3.5 eV, lattice constants fixed at a = 3.756 Å and c = 20.72 Å, an idealized I4/mmm structure with 180° Ni-O-Ni bonds, and a periodic bulk-like crystal (Fig. 1, Methods C).
   - **How large the margin is.** The bonding dz2 band top lies about 300 meV below E_F at M. The fitted t_perp^z = -0.64 eV is noticeably larger than in earlier tight-binding models (Refs. 95, 98), which the text says directly drives the larger bonding-antibonding splitting. It is unclear whether this 300 meV gap is a robust feature of the strained structure or a consequence of U, of relaxing the apical oxygen, or of the Wannierization.
   - **Why it matters.** The title and the main conclusion ("the presence of the gamma-pocket is not necessary") depend on this band structure being a realistic description of the films. Several factors may decide whether the gamma pocket appears, and the conflicting ARPES results (Refs. 96, 102, 111) may reflect them. The calculation ignores all of these:
     - the real film is only a few unit cells thick, on a substrate, with a surface;
     - it contains Pr substitution (La,Pr);
     - its oxygen stoichiometry and hole doping are set by ozone annealing.
   - **What to do.** Show how the dz2 band-top position at M, and t_perp^z, change with:
     - U (for example 0 to 5 eV);
     - the functional;
     - a magnetic versus non-magnetic DFT state;
     - in-plane strain and c-axis length;
     - a modest hole doping (rigid band, or extra apical oxygen).
     Also discuss, at least, how a slab geometry with a substrate or surface would change the result. If the gamma pocket reappears within a reasonable range of these parameters, the conclusion should be stated as conditional on that range.

2. The strong-coupling result is imposed through the assumption delta_z = 0 rather than derived.
   - **Where.** Sec. B states: "Due to absence of the gamma-pocket formed mainly by the dz2 orbital, we have delta_z = 0, resulting in Delta_SC^z = 0." This is what eliminates the large dz2 channel. The paper's own SBMF gives Delta_perp^z = 0.151 eV for the dz2 spinon pairing, about 100 times larger than Delta_perp^x = 1.28 × 10^-3 eV, so the dz2 channel would dominate if delta_z were even slightly nonzero.
   - **Why delta_z = 0 does not follow.** A missing bonding-dz2 pocket does not imply exactly one electron per dz2 orbital. Fig. 2(b) shows substantial dz2 weight on the alpha and beta pockets, and the antibonding dz2-derived states mix with dx2-y2.
   - **What to do.**
     - Compute the orbital occupations n_z and n_x from the TB model of Table I at the stated filling, and use the resulting delta_z and delta_x in the SBMF.
     - Report delta_x, which is not given in the main text even though it sets the gap scale.
     - Show how Delta_SC^z / Delta_SC^x depends on delta_z. Even delta_z of a few percent, multiplied by a spinon pairing 100 times larger, could make the dz2 interlayer channel comparable to or larger than the dx2-y2 one.
     - The claimed "dominant dx2-y2 interlayer channel" in the strong-coupling limit holds only if this check comes out in its favour.

3. In RPA the s and d channels are nearly degenerate, and the paper picks s+- largely on external grounds.
   - **Where.** In Fig. 3(b) the s-wave and d-wave eigenvalues are almost on top of each other over the whole U range shown (1.10-1.25 eV, just below U_c = 1.33 eV). The text says that lambda_d is "slightly smaller". The conclusion "the pairing symmetry of the system should be s+-" is then partly justified by an ARPES report that there are no nodes (Ref. 106). That is an experimental argument, not a theoretical result.
   - **Why it matters.** A weak-coupling result that differs by a few percent can flip with small changes in J_H/U, the filling, the temperature or momentum mesh of the RPA, or the ratio V = U - 2J_H. The headline claim that weak and strong coupling "consistently" give s+- needs a clearer margin.
   - **What to do.**
     - Show lambda_s and lambda_d (and their ratio) as functions of J_H/U (for example 0.1 to 0.25), of the electron filling (for example +-0.1 e per Ni, relevant to ozone-annealed films), and of U/U_c.
     - Give the RPA temperature and k mesh, and show that the ordering converges.
     - Plot the orbital- and layer-resolved spin susceptibilities, so that readers can see which fluctuation channel (interlayer dx2-y2 versus intralayer) favours s over d.
     - If the ordering is fragile, state that weak coupling cannot decide between s+- and d-wave in this band structure.

B. Should address

4. The STM comparison (Fig. 4b) is presented as support for s+- but cannot tell the candidate states apart as shown.
   - **What is missing.** Methods F gives the formula (Eqs. 12-13) but not:
     - the gap amplitude used;
     - the broadening (the "i0+" must have been replaced by a finite Gamma to produce the spectrum shown);
     - how the curve was normalized;
     - whether the energy axis or gap magnitude was fitted to the data.
     RPA fixes only the gap form factor, not its size. The reported SBMF amplitude, delta_x × 1.28 meV, appears to be at the sub-meV scale, whereas the features in Fig. 4(b) lie at roughly +-20-35 meV.
   - **Why it matters.** "The shape of the theoretical curve is similar" is weak evidence unless the competing candidates fail to reproduce it under the same procedure. With amplitude and broadening free, a nodeless s+- spectrum on a multi-pocket Fermi surface may look much like a single-band s-wave, or a d-wave with suitable broadening.
   - **What to do.**
     - List all parameters and say which were fitted.
     - Compute spectra for the subleading d-wave state of Fig. 3(d), and for a gap without sign change, with the same procedure, and show them alongside.
     - State whether the asymmetry of the experimental spectrum is captured and why.
     - Present the comparison in Fig. 4(b) as consistency rather than evidence for s+-.

5. Adding U in RPA on top of DFT+U bands is a double-counting issue that the paper does not discuss.
   - **The problem.** The band structure already contains U = 3.5 eV at the DFT+U level, and that U is likely what pushes the bonding dz2 band below E_F (see concern 1). The RPA then adds a Hubbard U of about 1.1-1.25 eV, plus V and J_H, to bands derived from it.
   - **Why it matters.** This mixes static mean-field and fluctuation treatments of the same interaction. It also means the band input and the interaction strength are not independent.
   - **What to do.** Justify the procedure, or repeat the RPA on bands from plain GGA (re-tuned to remove the gamma pocket if necessary) to show that the pairing symmetry does not depend on this choice. Also comment on how physical it is to operate this close to U_c, where lambda is only about 0.1-0.15.

6. The strong-coupling model parameters are set by hand, and their effect is not shown.
   - **The parameters.**
     - J_perp ≈ 2 J_par ≈ 0.4 eV is stated with no derivation of the absolute scale; J_par = 0.2 eV implies an effective U of about 4.5 eV for t_1^x = 0.48 eV.
     - The Hund's-transferred interlayer exchange on the x orbital is set equal to the full J_perp (Eq. 3), which is the infinite-J_H limit.
     - There is no intralayer exchange for the dz2 orbital.
   - **Why it matters.** The ratio of interlayer to intralayer dx2-y2 pairing (Delta_perp^x = 1.28 meV versus Delta_par^x = 0.011 meV) depends directly on these choices.
   - **What to do.**
     - Show how Delta_perp^x and Delta_par^x change with the transferred exchange J_perp,x for J_perp,x < J_perp (a finite J_H reduces the transfer), and with J_par.
     - Give the dopings delta_x and delta_z, the temperature, and the convergence criteria in the main text.

7. The film-versus-bulk comparison and its claimed agreement with experiment are not supported in the main text.
   - **The claim.** The Results say that the gap in the film at ambient pressure is "significantly smaller" than in the bulk at 30 GPa, "consistent with experimental observations". The numbers are deferred to the Supplementary Material, which was not available for this review.
   - **What is missing.** The main text does not quantify the ratio. It does not say which experimental quantity is compared: bulk gaps under pressure have not been measured directly, so presumably Tc ratios are meant. It also does not separate the two proposed causes, smaller hoppings and the missing gamma pocket.
   - **What to do.**
     - Give the gap ratio and the corresponding Tc ratio in the main text, and name the experimental comparison.
     - Separate the two effects, for example by computing the bulk with its hoppings rescaled to film values while keeping its filling.
     - SBMF gaps at the meV scale are hard to compare with tens-of-meV spectroscopic gaps (see concern 4), and the authors should comment on this.

8. The manuscript needs a clearer comparison with existing theory of the films.
   - **Prior work.** The introduction cites several theoretical studies of the films (Refs. 95, 98, 100, 104, 105, 110, 113, 114), including the authors' own Ref. 98, but does not say:
     - which of them assumed or obtained a gamma-free Fermi surface;
     - which pairing states they found;
     - how the present results differ.
   - **Why it matters.** As written, the reader cannot tell what is new beyond moving to a gamma-free band structure. Some studies of related models report d-wave or other states, and the near-degeneracy in concern 3 makes the comparison particularly relevant.
   - **What to do.** Add a short paragraph, or a table, comparing Fermi-surface assumptions, methods and pairing results with these works. Explain the larger t_perp^z found here (-0.64 eV versus the values in Refs. 95, 98).
