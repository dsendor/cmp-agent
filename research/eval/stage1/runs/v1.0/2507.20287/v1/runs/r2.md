Review outline:

1. Significance and novelty

The manuscript asks how superconductivity can arise in compressively strained La3Ni2O7 thin films on SrLaAlO4 if, as some ARPES groups report, the bonding d_z2 band lies below the Fermi level and there is no gamma pocket. Starting from a DFT+U band structure (U = 3.5 eV) with no gamma pocket, the authors fit a four-band bilayer tight-binding model. They then study pairing from two directions: multi-orbital RPA (weak coupling) and slave-boson mean-field theory on a two-orbital bilayer t-J model (strong coupling). Both give an s± state dominated by interlayer d_x2-y2 pairing, with opposite gap signs on the alpha and beta pockets. The question matters given the conflicting ARPES reports, and treating it from both coupling limits is useful. However, the main conclusions depend on one band-structure input whose sensitivity is not tested, on a near-degenerate competition between pairing channels, and on strong-coupling amplitudes that look too small to account for the observed gap. As written, the evidence that the result is robust is limited.

2. Potential reasons for acceptance

- The question is timely and well posed. The ARPES results on the gamma pocket in (La,Pr)3Ni2O7/SrLaAlO4 films disagree, and existing pairing theories mostly assume a gamma pocket is present.
- The weak- and strong-coupling treatments are complementary, and they agree on the sign structure (interlayer d_x2-y2 pairing, s± between alpha and beta).
- The tight-binding parameters are given in full (Table I, Eqs. 6-8), so others can reuse and test the model.
- The paper makes direct contact with experiment: the nodeless gap near the point where the alpha and beta pockets touch (ARPES, Ref. [106]) and the STM spectrum of Ref. [108] (Fig. 4b).

3. Concerns

A. Must address

1. The premise that there is no gamma pocket comes from a single DFT+U calculation, and its sensitivity is never tested (Results, Fig. 1c, Fig. 2, Methods C).
   - **What the paper does.** The bonding d_z2 band sits about 300 meV below E_F at M. This result comes from one nonmagnetic(?) PBE+U calculation with U = 3.5 eV, in-plane lattice constant fixed at a = 3.756 Å, c = 20.72 Å, and an ideal I4/mmm bilayer.
   - **Why it matters.** Whether a gamma pocket exists is exactly what the experiments dispute. The band position is known to depend on U, on the treatment of strain, and on the electron count.
   - **Missing information.** The paper does not say:
     - whether the calculation is spin-polarized;
     - how U is applied (Dudarev or Liechtenstein, Ni only);
     - the orbital occupancies n_z and n_x;
     - the chemical potential or total filling used in the TB model. Table I gives only absolute on-site energies of about 9-10 eV.
   - **Hopping and nesting.** The enlarged interlayer hopping t⊥z = -0.64 eV is what opens the large bonding-antibonding gap. The authors note it is larger than in earlier models [95, 98], but do not explain why.
   - **Comparison with ARPES.** The paper does not compare quantitatively with the ARPES it cites [102, 111]: the measured depth of the d_z2 band top, the alpha and beta Fermi-surface areas, and Fermi velocities. Without this, the nesting vector Q ≈ (0.64π, 0.64π), which drives the RPA result, is not anchored to the real film.
   - **Requested.** Show the d_z2 band-top energy and the Fermi surface as functions of U (for example 0 to 6 eV) and of filling, including modest hole doping from excess oxygen, which is plausible for ozone-annealed films. Compare the resulting Fermi surface quantitatively with the ARPES data. If the gamma pocket reappears for reasonable U or doping, the scope of the conclusions should be stated accordingly.

2. The s±-wave answer in the RPA is not shown to be robust against the nearly degenerate d-wave channel (Fig. 3b, Sec. A).
   - **The competition is close.** Over the narrow window U = 1.1-1.25 eV with J_H = U/6 fixed, λ_d is only slightly below λ_s. Both are small (about 0.15 at U = 1.25 eV).
   - **The deciding argument is experimental.** The text settles the leading symmetry partly by appeal to ARPES ("Thus, the pairing symmetry of the system should be s±"), not by a clear margin in the calculation.
   - **Requested sweeps.** Show λ_s/λ_d as functions of:
     - J_H/U (for example 0.1-0.25);
     - filling or chemical potential;
     - the d_z2 band position;
     - the RPA temperature and k-mesh. Neither is reported; both affect a nearly degenerate eigenvalue competition.
   - **Requested gap profile.** Plot the gap as a function of Fermi-surface angle on each pocket, with the minimum near the alpha-beta touching points marked. This would turn "relatively small but not zero" into a quantitative statement that can be compared with the ARPES gap anisotropy.
   - **Why it matters.** Without these tests, the claim that weak coupling yields s± rests on a margin that may fall within model uncertainty.

3. In the strong-coupling calculation, the dominant physical pairing channel is very weak, and its dominance follows from an imposed condition (Sec. B, Fig. 6, Eqs. 3, 14-16).
   - **Amplitude problem.** The reported spinon pairing amplitudes are Δ⊥z = 0.151 eV but Δ⊥x = 1.28 × 10^-3 eV, two orders of magnitude smaller. The physical d_x2-y2 order parameter is δ_x × Δ⊥x, so it is at most about 1 meV, and smaller still once the actual δ_x is included (its value is not given in the main text). This seems hard to reconcile with the gap of order 10-20 meV implied by the STM spectrum in Fig. 4b, and with T_c ~ 40 K.
   - **δ_z = 0 is imposed.** The d_x2-y2 channel wins only because δ_z = 0 is set by hand, which kills the much larger d_z2 channel. Fig. 2b shows the alpha and beta pockets have mixed orbital character, so n_z is not exactly 1. A small but finite δ_z multiplying 0.151 eV could compete with or exceed δ_x × 1.28 meV.
   - **Full transfer of J⊥ to the x orbital is assumed.** H_J,x carries the full J⊥ ≈ 0.4 eV through Hund's coupling. In Refs. [25, 41] the effective transferred coupling depends on J_H and on orbital filling.
   - **Requested.**
     - Report δ_x and δ_z obtained from the DFT orbital occupancies.
     - Show the SC order parameters as functions of δ_z and of the transferred J⊥x.
     - Give the physical gap magnitude, and a mean-field T_c estimate (or an upper bound on it), and compare these with experiment.
     - Explain why Δ⊥x is so small relative to Δ⊥z when the same J⊥ acts on both.

B. Should address

4. The STM comparison does not discriminate between pairing states (Fig. 4b, Methods F, Eqs. 12-13).
   - **Missing procedure.** The text does not explain how the gap magnitude in the theoretical curve was fixed. RPA yields only the gap's form, not its size, so it was presumably scaled to the data. The broadening (the "0+"), the normalization, and the background treatment are also not given.
   - **Simplifying assumption.** Eq. 12 uses band-diagonal coherence factors, which assumes no interband pairing.
   - **Not unique to s±.** A similar U-shaped spectrum would likely follow from a nodeless s++ gap of the same size. "Similar shape" is therefore not evidence specific to s±.
   - **Requested.** Plot, with the same procedure, the spectra for the subleading d-wave and for an s++ state with matched gap scale. State all fitted parameters. Moderate the abstract's statement of consistency with STM accordingly. Phase-sensitive probes, such as quasiparticle interference or impurity-bound states, would be the discriminating test and could be suggested.

5. The two approaches may double count interactions and may not be independent (Sec. A versus Sec. B, Methods C).
   - **Double counting.** The band structure already contains a static U = 3.5 eV from DFT+U, and this U is what pushes the gamma band down. The RPA then adds a Hubbard U ≈ 1.25 eV on top. The authors should explain how double counting is handled, or else repeat the RPA on bands from U = 0 (or a smaller U).
   - **The agreement is largely built in.** Both methods end up with interlayer d_x2-y2 pairing. Projected onto bonding and antibonding bands, an interlayer s-wave pair is s± between alpha and beta essentially by construction (Fig. 6b). The agreement therefore checks the pairing channel, not the sign structure independently. The text should say so rather than present it as two independent confirmations.

6. The claims that the gamma pocket is "not necessary" and that the film gap is smaller than the bulk gap are not supported by a controlled comparison (Sec. B last paragraph, Discussion).
   - **Bulk comparison.** The 30 GPa bulk calculation and the statement that the film gap is smaller, "consistent with experimental observations", appear only in the Supplementary Material. No gap values, δ_α, or J values are given in the main text.
   - **Two causes not separated.** The explanation offers two mechanisms (smaller hoppings, and the missing gamma pocket making d_x2-y2 more overdoped). Neither is separated from the other.
   - **Requested.**
     - Within the film model, shift the d_z2 band continuously (via ε_z or the chemical potential) so that the gamma pocket appears. Track λ (RPA) and δ_x × Δ⊥x (SBMF) through the transition.
     - Add a main-text table comparing film and bulk: hoppings, J, δ_x, δ_z, and gap amplitudes.
   - **Why it matters.** This control would directly test the title claim and the proposed reason the film gap is smaller.

7. The structural model is idealized compared with the real films (Results paragraph 1, Fig. 1a).
   - **What the model assumes.** The calculation treats bulk-like, stoichiometric La3Ni2O7 with a 180° Ni-O-Ni angle in I4/mmm.
   - **What the experiments measured.** The films are (La,Pr)3Ni2O7, a few unit cells thick, on SrLaAlO4. They have an interface with the substrate, a surface, and possibly octahedral tilts, oxygen off-stoichiometry, or Sr interdiffusion.
   - **Why it matters.** Each of these can shift the d_z2 band by an amount comparable to the 300 meV gap, or change the interlayer hopping that controls J⊥.
   - **Requested.** Discuss these effects and, where feasible, test the most important one. A slab calculation with the substrate interface, or relaxed octahedral tilts, would be natural choices. If they are not tested, state these as limitations.

C. Minor

8. The description of the missing pocket is confusing (Results paragraph 2). The text says the bonding d_z2 band lies about 300 meV below E_F "at M point". It then says that it "does not cross the Fermi level at the Γ point, and thus forms no electron pocket". In the convention of Fig. 2b, the gamma pocket would be a hole pocket around M. Please make the location and character of the missing pocket consistent with the BZ convention used in Figs. 1b and 2b.

9. Table I and Methods D do not give the chemical potential or the electron filling for the TB model, and the absolute on-site energies (ε_z = 9.0974, ε_x = 9.8963 eV) cannot be used without it. Please give μ and the per-Ni orbital occupancies. Also check the naming in the Table I caption, where t_3^xz, t_4^xz and t_5^xz are described as "intralayer nearest neighbor, interlayer nearest neighbor, intralayer third nearest neighbor" hybridizations. Make sure this matches the subscripts and the Fourier factors in Eq. 8.

10. Eq. 1: the Hund's coupling term is written as a bare four-fermion product without a sign convention or the usual spin-exchange form, and the pair-hopping term is grouped inside the same J_H bracket. Please write it in a standard form. Please also state the RPA numerical details: temperature, k-mesh, and the number of Matsubara frequencies.

11. Figures 3 and 4:
    - Fig. 3a and the gap plots in Figs. 3c-d have color bars without units or normalization.
    - The text does not discuss the p-wave curve in Fig. 3b.
    - In Fig. 4b, the y axis is unlabeled (normalized dI/dV versus A(ω)).
    - The legend cites "arxiv:2506.01788" instead of the reference number used in the caption ([108]).

12. Fig. 6b: the color scale runs from -1 to 1, apparently normalized. Please state the normalization and the actual magnitude of δ_x Δ⊥x.

13. Methods subsections are lettered C-G, continuing from the Results subsections A-B. The Matsubara Green's function is defined twice (Eqs. 2 and 9). Please renumber and remove the duplicate.

4. Suggestions for improvement

1. Make the gamma-band position a tunable control parameter. Present RPA eigenvalues and SBMF order parameters as functions of the d_z2 band-top energy across the Lifshitz transition. This would test the title claim directly and would cover both sides of the ARPES dispute.
2. Add a short robustness section on how the s±/d competition depends on U_DFT, J_H/U, filling and k-mesh. Present it as a phase diagram in place of a single parameter set.
3. Report physical gap magnitudes and T_c estimates from both approaches. Compare them quantitatively with the STM and ARPES gaps, including the gap ratio between the alpha and beta pockets.
4. Move the key film-versus-bulk numbers from the Supplementary Material into a main-text table, and state the limitations of the bulk-like, stoichiometric, substrate-free structural model.
