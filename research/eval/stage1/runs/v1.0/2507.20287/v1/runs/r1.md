Review outline:

1. Significance and novelty

The paper asks how pairing works in compressively strained La3Ni2O7 films on SrLaAlO4 if the bonding d_z2 band (the γ-pocket) lies below the Fermi level, as some ARPES studies report. Starting from a DFT+U band structure with U = 3.5 eV that has no γ-pocket, the authors run multi-orbital RPA (weak coupling) and slave-boson mean-field theory on a two-orbital bilayer t-J model (strong coupling). Both give an s±-wave state dominated by interlayer d_x2-y2 pairing, with opposite gap signs on the α- and β-pockets. The question matters given the conflicting ARPES reports. The methods, however, are the ones the same group and others have already applied to the bulk and to films (Refs. 41, 98, 103 and 100, 104, 105, 110, 114). The main new input is the band structure without a γ-pocket, so the result is only as robust as that starting point and the parameter choices built on it. The paper does not yet test either of these.

2. Potential reasons for acceptance

- It addresses a timely open question: whether the γ-pocket is needed for superconductivity in nickelate films.
- It approaches the problem from two complementary limits, weak-coupling RPA and strong-coupling SBMF, which agree on the gap structure. The agreement adds some confidence.
- It gives a simple physical picture. Without the γ-pocket, d_z2 is close to half-filled and localized, and it passes its interlayer superexchange to d_x2-y2 through Hund's coupling. This picture makes a testable prediction: a sign change between the α- and β-pockets.
- It gives the full TB parameters (Table I), which makes the work reproducible.

3. Concerns

A. Must address

1. The absence of the γ-pocket is put in through the DFT+U choice, and its robustness is never shown. Section RESULTS and Figs. 1(c) and 2(a) rest on a single DFT+U calculation: U = 3.5 eV, lattice constants fixed at a = 3.756 Å and c = 20.72 Å, I4/mmm symmetry imposed, a 180° Ni-O-Ni angle, and the La3Ni2O7 stoichiometry. A static Hubbard U is known to push the occupied d_z2 bonding band down. The text itself notes that the fitted t⊥^z = -0.64 eV is larger than in earlier film models (Refs. 95, 98). That larger value is what produces the ~300 meV gap between the band top and E_F at M. Earlier calculations of the strained film, including Ref. 98 by some of the same authors, kept a γ-pocket. So the paper's premise may reflect the choice of U rather than the material.

   The ARPES reports the paper cites (Refs. 102, 111) put the band top much closer to E_F than 300 meV. The paper gives no quantitative comparison with them. Without one, it is unclear whether this band structure is a realistic model of the films without a γ-pocket or an exaggerated limit.

   Requested:
   - Show the energy of the d_z2 band top at M as a function of U, from 0 to 5 eV.
   - Show how it depends on the in-plane lattice constant and on c.
   - Show how it depends on plausible hole doping, for example Pr/Sr substitution or oxygen off-stoichiometry, through a rigid shift or virtual-crystal calculation.
   - Compare the band top and the α/β Fermi momenta directly with the ARPES dispersions.

2. The headline strong-coupling result is fixed by the assumption δ_z = 0, which the paper does not justify. In Section B, the d_z2 interlayer spinon pairing is by far the largest amplitude (Δ⊥^z = 0.151 eV). The d_x2-y2 amplitude is about 100 times smaller (Δ⊥^x = 1.28×10⁻³ eV). The d_z2 channel is then removed only because the paper sets the holon density δ_z = 0 "due to absence of the γ-pocket".

   But the α- and β-pockets carry substantial d_z2 weight, as the color scale of Fig. 2(b) shows, and t∥^xz = 0.216 eV hybridizes the two orbitals. So the d_z2 occupancy in the TB model need not be exactly 1. Even a small d_z2 hole density from hybridization or from substitution or oxygen doping would give δ_z Δ⊥^z ≫ δ_x Δ⊥^x, which would reverse the conclusion about the dominant channel. Holon condensation with δ_z = 0 would also renormalize all z-orbital hopping, including t∥^xz, to zero. This would change the Fermi surface onto which the gap is projected in Fig. 6(b).

   Requested:
   - Report the orbital-resolved fillings n_z and n_x obtained from the TB model at the stated chemical potential, and the resulting δ_x and δ_z.
   - Show the SC order parameters in both channels as a function of δ_z, say from 0 to 0.1.
   - Explain how the renormalized band structure used for Fig. 6(b) is built.

3. The STM comparison does not distinguish s± from other states, and the gap magnitude is not explained. In Fig. 4(b) and the text after Eq. (2), the authors compare a computed spectrum with the STM data of Ref. 108 and say the similarity indicates "the possibility of the s±-wave pairing". Eqs. (12) and (13) give a momentum-summed density of states. A DOS of this kind depends on |Δ_k| only, not on the relative gap sign between pockets. It therefore cannot separate s± from s++ with the same magnitudes.

   The paper also does not explain:
   - how the gap amplitude in Fig. 4(b) was set, given that RPA yields only the gap form factor;
   - what broadening (0⁺) and normalization were used;
   - why the coherence-peak scale of roughly 20 to 30 meV is consistent with the SBMF amplitude of order 1 meV, i.e., δ_x × 1.28 meV.

   Requested:
   - Plot the s++ and d_x2-y2 spectra computed in the same way on the same axes.
   - State all fitting and broadening parameters.
   - Either remove the claim that the STM data support s± specifically, or propose an observable that is sensitive to the gap sign, such as a quasiparticle-interference or impurity-bound-state calculation.

4. In RPA the s-wave and d-wave states are nearly degenerate, and the paper does not test how sensitive this is to its parameters. Fig. 3(b) shows λ_s only slightly above λ_d, and only over the window U = 1.10 to 1.25 eV just below U_c = 1.33 eV. RPA is least reliable in that region. The calculation fixes U = V + 2J_H and J_H = U/6. It also adds U on top of DFT+U bands that already include a 3.5 eV Hubbard U, so the interaction is double counted.

   Given that near-degeneracy, the conclusion "the pairing symmetry of the system should be s±" depends on parameters the paper does not vary. The ARPES argument used to break the tie (Ref. 106, no node near the α/β touching point) adds evidence for s±, but it is not the outcome of the calculation.

   Requested:
   - Show λ_s and λ_d as functions of J_H/U, of doping (filling), and of the RPA temperature and k-mesh.
   - Ideally, show them for a band structure computed without U, or explain how double counting is handled.

B. Should address

5. The paper does not compare its results with the case that has a γ-pocket. The Discussion concludes that "the presence of the γ-pocket is not necessary". Showing that pairing occurs without the pocket supports this as stated. But the paper's motivation is the experimental conflict, and its film-versus-bulk comparison credits the smaller gap partly to the missing γ-pocket. Both points call for a controlled comparison: the same film TB model with the d_z2 band rigidly shifted so that a γ-pocket appears, run through the same RPA and SBMF. That would separate the effect of the γ-pocket from strain-induced changes in hopping. Please add this comparison, or narrow the wording of the conclusions.

6. The film-versus-bulk gap comparison is qualitative, and its numbers are only in the SM. Section B states that the film gap is "significantly smaller" than the bulk gap at 30 GPa, "consistent with experimental observations". It gives two causes: weaker hopping, and d_x2-y2 becoming "more overdoped". Requested:
   - Give the film and bulk values of δ_x, δ_z, Δ_SC and the hopping parameters in the main text.
   - Quantify the experimental ratio being compared (Tc of about 40 K versus about 80 K, and the gap values from STM or ARPES).
   - Separate the two proposed causes, for example by running the film model with bulk hopping values.

7. The strong-coupling exchange parameters are imposed, not derived, and they produce the key channel. Above Eq. (3), J⊥ ≈ 2J∥ ≈ 0.4 eV is set from J ∝ t². The same J⊥ is then given to the d_x2-y2 channel, H_J,x, as if Hund's coupling passed it on in full. Interlayer d_x2-y2 pairing, the main result, comes directly from this transferred term. The size of the transfer depends on J_H and on how strongly the d_z2 moments are localized.

   Requested:
   - Justify the J⊥^x = J⊥^z assumption.
   - Show how Δ_SC^x varies as J⊥^x/J⊥^z goes from 0 to 1 and as J⊥/J∥ varies.
   - Check whether the ratio J⊥/J∥ ≈ 2 matches (t⊥^z/t∥^x)² ≈ 1.8 from Table I.

8. Inconsistent description of where the γ-pocket would sit. In the band-structure paragraph, the text says the bonding d_z2 band lies about 300 meV below E_F "at M point". The next sentence says this band "does not cross the Fermi level at the Γ point, and thus forms no electron pocket". In the convention of Fig. 2(b), the γ-pocket is a hole pocket centered at M. Please correct the text so that it matches Figs. 1(c) and 2(b).

9. The spin-susceptibility peak in Fig. 3(a) is hard to check against the nesting vector. The bright maxima in Fig. 3(a) appear near the zone corners, while the arrow marks Q ≈ (0.64π, 0.64π). Please add a line cut of χ^(s)(k) along Γ–M with Q marked. Also state the temperature and k-mesh used for χ, and show how the peak position and strength change with U.

10. The thin film is modeled as a bulk crystal under strain. The DFT uses a periodic bulk cell with fixed lattice constants, and the TB model describes a single bilayer without k_z. Real films are a few unit cells thick and have a substrate interface and a surface. They also have possible Sr interdiffusion from SrLaAlO4, Pr substitution in (La,Pr)3Ni2O7, and oxygen stoichiometry that depends on ozone annealing. Any of these could shift the d_z2 band relative to E_F, which is the quantity the paper's premise depends on. The authors should at least discuss these factors. A rigid-band estimate of how much doping would restore the γ-pocket would help.

C. Minor

11. Table I gives absolute onsite energies (ε_z = 9.0974 eV, ε_x = 9.8963 eV) but no chemical potential or electron count. Please give μ, the total filling, and the orbital fillings so that readers can reproduce Fig. 2(b).

12. The RPA methods section (Section E) should state the temperature, the k-mesh, the number of Matsubara frequencies, and how the gap functions are normalized in Figs. 3(c) and 3(d).

13. The SBMF details and the full set of order parameters are said to be in the Supplementary Material, which was not available with this submission. The main text should at least state the mean-field equations solved, the temperature, and the convergence criteria.

14. Fig. 3(b) is hard to read because the s and d curves overlap. Please use an inset, or plot λ_s − λ_d.

15. Fig. 4(b) labels the experimental curve "arXiv:2506.01788". Please use the reference number [108] and state whether the experimental curve was rescaled or normalized.

16. Eqs. (2) and (9) are duplicates. Section labels run A and B under RESULTS, then C to G under METHODS. The METHODS section has the typo "ab inito". The phrase "the overall hopping amplitudes in the film at AP are smaller" should cite the specific parameters.

17. The DFT k-mesh (8×8×2) and the convergence checks should be stated or justified. The paper should also state whether the internal coordinates were relaxed with or without U.

4. Suggestions for improvement

1. Add a phase diagram of the d_z2 band-top energy against U and strain or doping. Map onto it where the RPA and SBMF pairing symmetry and strength change, including the regime where the γ-pocket returns. This would turn the single-point calculation into a controlled study of the γ-pocket.
2. Compute an observable sensitive to the gap sign, such as phase-referenced QPI or the in-gap states induced by nonmagnetic impurities, for s±, s++ and d. This would let the STM comparison actually discriminate between them.
3. Report the strong-coupling results as functions of δ_z and J⊥^x/J⊥^z, so that readers can see how much of the "interlayer d_x2-y2" conclusion rests on these inputs.
4. State the gap magnitudes from both approaches in meV, and compare them quantitatively with the film's Tc and the gap size seen in STM.
