A. Must address

1. The absence of the γ-pocket, on which the whole paper rests, comes from a single DFT+U calculation and its robustness is never tested (Results, Figs. 1(c), 2(a), 2(b), Methods C). The bonding d_z2 band top sits about 300 meV below E_F at M in one PBE/GGA+U calculation with U = 3.5 eV, lattice constants fixed at a = 3.756 Å and c = 20.72 Å, an idealized I4/mmm structure with 180° Ni-O-Ni bonds, a periodic bulk-like cell and La3Ni2O7 stoichiometry. A static Hubbard U is known to push the occupied d_z2 bonding band down, and the band position also depends on strain, the functional, the magnetic state and the electron count. The text itself notes that the fitted t⊥^z = -0.64 eV is larger than in earlier film models (Refs. 95, 98) and that this larger value produces the large bonding-antibonding splitting, but does not explain why it is larger; earlier calculations of the strained film, including Ref. 98 by some of the same authors, kept a γ-pocket. It is therefore unclear whether the 300 meV gap is a property of the material or of U, of the apical-oxygen relaxation or of the Wannierization. The paper also gives no quantitative comparison with the ARPES it cites (Refs. 96, 102, 111), which put the band top much closer to E_F: the measured depth of the d_z2 band top, the α and β Fermi-surface areas, Fermi momenta and Fermi velocities. Without this, the nesting vector Q ≈ (0.64π, 0.64π) that drives the RPA result is not anchored to the real film, and the model may be an exaggerated limit rather than a realistic gamma-free film. The authors should show the d_z2 band-top energy at M, t⊥^z and the Fermi surface as functions of U (for example 0 to 5 or 6 eV), of the functional, of a magnetic versus non-magnetic DFT state, of in-plane strain and c-axis length, and of modest hole doping (rigid band, virtual crystal for Pr/Sr substitution, or extra apical oxygen as is plausible for ozone-annealed films); compare the resulting band top and α/β Fermi momenta directly with the ARPES dispersions; and, if the γ-pocket reappears within a reasonable range, state the conclusions as conditional on that range.

2. The headline strong-coupling result is fixed by the assumption δ_z = 0, which is imposed rather than derived (Sec. B, Eqs. 3, 14-16, Fig. 6). The SBMF gives a d_z2 interlayer spinon pairing Δ⊥^z = 0.151 eV, about 100 times larger than the d_x2-y2 amplitude Δ⊥^x = 1.28×10⁻³ eV. The d_z2 channel is removed only because the text states "Due to absence of the γ-pocket ... we have δ_z = 0, resulting in Δ_SC^z = 0". A missing bonding-d_z2 pocket does not imply exactly one electron per d_z2 orbital: Fig. 2(b) shows substantial d_z2 weight on the α- and β-pockets, the antibonding d_z2-derived states mix with d_x2-y2, and t∥^xz = 0.216 eV hybridizes the two orbitals. Even a δ_z of a few percent, from hybridization or from substitution or oxygen doping, multiplied by a spinon pairing 100 times larger, could make δ_z Δ⊥^z comparable to or larger than δ_x Δ⊥^x and reverse the conclusion about the dominant channel. Holon condensation with δ_z = 0 would also renormalize all z-orbital hoppings, including t∥^xz, to zero, changing the Fermi surface onto which the gap is projected in Fig. 6(b). The authors should compute the orbital-resolved fillings n_z and n_x from the TB model of Table I at the stated chemical potential and use the resulting δ_x and δ_z in the SBMF; report δ_x in the main text, since it sets the gap scale; show the order parameters in both channels, and Δ_SC^z/Δ_SC^x, as functions of δ_z (say 0 to 0.1); and explain how the renormalized band structure used for Fig. 6(b) is built. The claim of a dominant interlayer d_x2-y2 channel in the strong-coupling limit holds only if this check comes out in its favour.

3. In the RPA the s-wave and d-wave channels are nearly degenerate, and the choice of s± rests largely on an external experimental argument rather than on the calculation (Fig. 3(b), Sec. A). The eigenvalues λ_s and λ_d lie almost on top of each other, both small (about 0.1-0.15 at U = 1.25 eV), over only the narrow window U = 1.10-1.25 eV just below U_c = 1.33 eV, where RPA is least reliable; the text says only that λ_d is "slightly smaller". The conclusion "the pairing symmetry of the system should be s±" is then justified partly by the ARPES report of no node near the α/β touching point (Ref. 106), which adds evidence but is not an outcome of the calculation. A margin of a few percent can flip with small changes in J_H/U (fixed here at 1/6), the relation V = U - 2J_H, the filling, the d_z2 band position, or the RPA temperature and k-mesh, neither of which is reported. The claim that weak and strong coupling "consistently" give s± needs a clearer margin. The authors should show λ_s, λ_d and their ratio as functions of J_H/U (for example 0.1 to 0.25), of filling (for example ±0.1 e per Ni), of the d_z2 band position, of U/U_c, and of RPA temperature and k-mesh, demonstrating convergence of the ordering; plot the orbital- and layer-resolved spin susceptibilities so readers can see which fluctuation channel favours s over d; plot the gap versus Fermi-surface angle on each pocket with the minimum near the α-β touching points marked, to make "relatively small but not zero" quantitative and comparable with the ARPES gap anisotropy; and, if the ordering is fragile, state that weak coupling cannot decide between s± and d-wave in this band structure.

5. The strong-coupling gap is too small to account for the observed gap, and the paper never states physical gap magnitudes (Sec. B, Figs. 4(b), 6(b)). The physical d_x2-y2 order parameter is δ_x × Δ⊥^x = δ_x × 1.28 meV, so at most about 1 meV and smaller once δ_x (not given in the main text) is included. This is hard to reconcile with the coherence features at roughly ±20-35 meV in the STM spectrum of Fig. 4(b) and with Tc ≈ 40 K. The authors should also explain why Δ⊥^x is so much smaller than Δ⊥^z when the same J⊥ acts on both. They should give the physical gap magnitudes in meV from both approaches, a mean-field Tc estimate or upper bound, and compare these quantitatively with the film Tc and with the STM and ARPES gaps, including the α/β gap ratio.

6. The STM comparison in Fig. 4(b) is presented as support for s± but cannot distinguish candidate states, and its procedure is not given (Fig. 4(b), text after Eq. (2), Methods F, Eqs. 12-13). Eqs. 12-13 give a momentum-summed density of states that depends only on |Δ_k|, not on the relative sign between pockets, so it cannot separate s± from s++ with the same magnitudes; with amplitude and broadening free, a d-wave may also fit. Eq. 12 additionally uses band-diagonal coherence factors, which assumes no interband pairing. The paper does not explain how the gap amplitude was set (RPA fixes only the form factor), what finite broadening replaced 0⁺, the normalization and background treatment, whether the energy axis or gap magnitude were fitted, or how the 20-35 meV scale relates to the ~1 meV SBMF amplitude (see concern 5). The authors should list all parameters and say which were fitted; compute spectra for an s++ state with matched gap scale and for the subleading d-wave of Fig. 3(d) with the same procedure and show them on the same axes; state whether the asymmetry of the experimental spectrum is captured; present the comparison as consistency rather than evidence for s±, moderating the abstract accordingly; and propose or compute a sign-sensitive observable such as phase-referenced quasiparticle interference or in-gap states from nonmagnetic impurities.

7. The strong-coupling exchange parameters are imposed rather than derived, and they produce the key channel (Eq. 3, Sec. B). J⊥ ≈ 2J∥ ≈ 0.4 eV is set from J ∝ t² with no derivation of the absolute scale (J∥ = 0.2 eV implies an effective U of about 4.5 eV for t_1^x = 0.48 eV). The full J⊥ is then given to the d_x2-y2 channel H_J,x, which is the infinite-J_H limit; in Refs. 25 and 41 the transferred coupling depends on J_H and on orbital filling and on how strongly the d_z2 moments are localized. There is also no intralayer exchange for d_z2. Interlayer d_x2-y2 pairing, the main result, and the ratio of interlayer to intralayer pairing (Δ⊥^x = 1.28 meV versus Δ∥^x = 0.011 meV) come directly from these choices. The authors should justify J⊥^x = J⊥^z, show how Δ⊥^x, Δ∥^x and Δ_SC^x vary as J⊥^x/J⊥^z goes from 0 to 1 and as J∥ and J⊥/J∥ vary, and check whether J⊥/J∥ ≈ 2 matches (t⊥^z/t∥^x)² ≈ 1.8 from Table I.

8. Adding U in RPA on top of DFT+U bands double counts the interaction, and this is not discussed (Methods C, Sec. A). The bands already contain a static U = 3.5 eV, which is likely what pushes the bonding d_z2 band below E_F (concern 1); the RPA then adds U ≈ 1.1-1.25 eV, plus V and J_H. This mixes static mean-field and fluctuation treatments of the same interaction and makes the band input and interaction strength interdependent. The authors should explain how double counting is handled, or repeat the RPA on bands from U = 0 or smaller U (re-tuned to remove the γ-pocket if necessary) to show that the pairing symmetry does not depend on this choice, and comment on how physical it is to operate so close to U_c where λ is only about 0.1-0.15.

10. The thin film is modelled as a stoichiometric bulk crystal under strain (Results paragraph 1, Fig. 1(a), Methods C-D). The DFT uses a periodic bulk cell, and the TB model describes a single bilayer without k_z. The real films are (La,Pr)3Ni2O7, a few unit cells thick on SrLaAlO4, with a substrate interface and a surface, possible octahedral tilts, possible Sr interdiffusion, and oxygen stoichiometry set by ozone annealing. Each of these can shift the d_z2 band relative to E_F by an amount comparable to the 300 meV margin, or change the interlayer hopping that controls J⊥. The authors should discuss these effects; give a rigid-band estimate of how much doping would restore the γ-pocket; where feasible test the most important one (for example a slab with the substrate interface, or relaxed octahedral tilts); and otherwise state them as limitations.

11. The agreement between the two approaches is partly built in and should not be presented as two independent confirmations of the sign structure (Sec. A versus Sec. B, Fig. 6(b)). Both methods end up with interlayer d_x2-y2 pairing, and an interlayer s-wave pair projected onto bonding and antibonding bands is s± between α and β essentially by construction. The agreement therefore checks the pairing channel, not the sign structure independently. The text should say so.
