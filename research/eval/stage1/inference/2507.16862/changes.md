  Bose–Einstein Condensate" (old p. 1) becomes "Emergence of phase coherence in a magnon

- **c2 Abstract rewritten and softened.** Old: "These findings confirm all postulated fundamental
  properties of quasiparticle condensates" and the information-processing outlook (old pp. 1–2).
  New: "Here we provide evidence of spontaneous phase formation in a magnon Bose–Einstein
  condensate" and "confirming a central prediction of Bose–Einstein condensation theory"
  (new p. 1). The history paragraph (helium, atoms, excitons) and the list of spatial-phase
  phenomena move from the abstract into the opening of the main text.
- **c4 "Novel" and "for the first time" removed.** "a novel, electronic approach" (old p. 2)

## Prior work and novelty

- **c3 Why earlier work could not see spontaneous emergence.** New sentence: detection methods
  such as Brillouin light scattering "rely on ensemble averaging over many excitation cycles"
  and give no access to the instantaneously selected phase (new p. 1). New ref. 30 (BLS).
- **c21 References.** Added: Bunkov & Volovik on magnon BEC in superfluid ³He (new ref. 26),
  Büttner et al. on BLS (ref. 30), Gurevich & Melkov textbook (ref. 40, cited for the
  parametric-pair phase argument). Removed from the main list: Mohseni et al. (qubit-logic
  outlook, old ref. 21) and Noack et al. 2021 (old ref. 41, cited in the old Supplementary).
  Demidov et al. 2008 ("spontaneous coherence") is still cited only for the in-plane geometry
  (new p. 2). The acknowledgements add thanks for help with "the underlying theoretical
  questions" (new p. 7).

## Geometry, dispersion and detection

- **c5 Why k ≈ 0 matters for detection.** New: the out-of-plane geometry "enables us to
  efficiently use inductive detection of the BEC-signal with a 100-μm-wide microstrip antenna"
  (new p. 2).
- **c6 New Fig. 1b: dispersion and pumping scheme.** A calculated dispersion of the
  out-of-plane YIG film (fundamental mode with minimum at k = 0, higher thickness modes), the
  pump at 7.8 GHz, f_p/2 and the BEC at the band bottom (new p. 2). Old Fig. 1 had panels a and
  b only (old p. 3). The caption of the spatiotemporal sketch (now c) changes "coherent
  high-energy magnons are introduced" (old p. 3) to "incoherent high-energy magnons are
  introduced into a narrow frequency band" (new p. 2).
- **c7 Antenna wavevector selectivity.** The old one-line claim "The detection path is not
  sensitive to the parametrically introduced magnons" (old p. 5) is replaced by two paragraphs

  hundred MHz above the Kittel frequency; parametric magnons (~18 μm wavelength) would still
  give a signal reduced by about a factor 18; "Nevertheless, no coherent signal at the
  parametric frequency is observed", read as phase randomization in the parametric ensemble.

## The phase argument

- **c8 Why the pump phase does not reach the k ≈ 0 mode.** New paragraph (new p. 3): parametric
  pumping fixes "the phase sum within each pair (k, −k)", but different pairs are mutually
  uncorrelated (ref. 40), so the parametric population has no global phase; four-magnon
  scattering and kinetic instability redistribute magnons "without introducing a deterministic
  phase reference for the k ≈ 0 mode".
- **c9 Pump-phase reference in the Fig. 2 histograms.** The Fig. 2 caption adds "The grey bars
  denote the phase distribution of the pump, whereas the red bars denote the phase
  distribution of the magnon signal" (new p. 3). The main text adds "see the distribution of the
  pumping phase indicated by the grey sectors in the first and second insets in Fig. 2a"
  (new p. 4). The "few degrees" stability statement is otherwise unchanged. The Extended Data
  Fig. 1 caption still says "The oscilloscope was excluded due to technical difficulties"
  (new p. 8; old Ext. Data Fig. 5, p. 12).
- **c22 Histogram timing wording.** "histograms of the phase at t0" (old p. 5) becomes
  "histograms of the phase measured at the beginning of the indicated time intervals"
  (new p. 4); the caption's "at the beginning of each frame" (old p. 4) becomes "at the start
  time of each panel" (new p. 3). No radial scale or bin width is added.

## Timing of condensation and coherence dynamics

- **c10 Condensation begins during pumping.** Old: "After the pump is switched off,
  condensation occurs" (old p. 5). New: the periodic traits during pumping are read as
  "indicating the onset of Bose–Einstein condensation", and "After the pump is switched off,
  condensation develops further in the absence of perturbation" (new p. 4).
- **c11 Discussion: during and after pumping; transverse pumping outlook.** New: "We were able to
  observe the emergence of coherence during and after pumping, which strongly indicates the
  existence of a BEC even in a heavily disturbed environment", followed by a paragraph on
  enhancing the magnon flux to the band bottom "under transverse pumping" for supercurrents and
  Josephson dynamics (new p. 5). Neither appears in v1.
- **c12 Oscillations in the coherence plateau.** New text (new p. 5): after the dip, "a plateau
  of high coherence is observed"; "The oscillations in coherence present in this plateau are
  still a subject of investigation", with a tentative explanation (incoherent magnons reflected
  from the sample edge crossing the antenna), supported by coinciding oscillations in amplitude
  and coherence in Fig. 4.
- **c15 Frequency shift explanation pointed to SI.** Fig. 3 caption adds "(Supplementary
  Section 2)" after the net-magnetization explanation (new p. 4). The main text still cites the
  Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya paper (now ref. 41) for the nonlinear shift (new p. 5).
- Unchanged: "the BEC does not undergo dephasing" and the 3.5 μs "remanence in coherence"
  (new p. 5); the Fig. 4 threshold reading ("a condensation threshold of 21 dBm") and
  "a higher amplitude can be observed" above 23 dBm (new p. 5).
