# Changes from arXiv v1 to the accepted version: 2606.18647

Trion Hall effect in electron-hole double layers. Old = arXiv v1 (25 pages, preprint layout).
New = the accepted journal version (18 pages, journal layout, Extended Data after the main text).
Page numbers are PDF pages from the `=== old|new PAGE n ===` markers. Device labels were swapped:
the old "device 2" (Hall data) is the new "device 3", and the old "device 3" (optics, drag map) is
the new "device 2". All changes are inferred from the text; no referee report or response letter
was available.

## Title, abstract and framing

- c1 (softened, reframed). Abstract rewritten. Old: the Hall effect is "manifested in both Hall
  drag measurements and standard Hall effect measurements", and the work "opens the door for
  realizing quantum oscillations and the quantum Hall effect for trions" (old p1). New: "by
  combining magnetotransport and Coulomb drag measurements", an electron-like Hall effect on the
  hole layer appears "because of Coulomb drag", and the results "suggest" the heterostructures
  are "a candidate" for trion-based quantum Hall phenomena (new p1). Likely referee-driven.
- c2 (reframed). Introduction now states the goal as verifying that "trions behave as mobile
  charge carriers" and explains the B-field evolution of trion regions in the intro (new p2).
  Old intro promised to "map out the phase diagram" and "demonstrate a Hall effect" (old p2).
- c3 (added). New schematic phase diagrams at B = 0 and B > 0 (new Fig. 1d,e, p2), with an
  explanation that the trion regions expand with B "probably due to the field-induced
  confinement" and shrink when valley Zeeman splitting exceeds the binding energy, giving a
  ~10 T window (new p2). The old measured B = 0 Rxx map (old Fig. 1d, p3, p13) is gone; the old
  constructed phase diagram (old Fig. 1f) is replaced by these schematics.

## Identification of trion regions (transport)

- c4 (new_analysis). New Fig. 2 (new p3): Rxx map at 4 T (device 1, now with Δ = 13 V), Rxx vs
  Vg at Vb = 0.762 V for temperatures up to 9 K (Fig. 2b), and Rxx(T) at selected Vg (Fig. 2c).
  The trion boundaries are now "identified by tracing the metal–insulator transition" (new p3),
  and the authors state they "verified that the lower boundaries" follow p = 2n and n = 2p using
  the device capacitances (new p2). Old: boundaries drawn "corresponding to those in Fig. 1f",
  a constructed diagram (old p13-p16). The old ED Fig. 7 (Rxx temperature dependence, old p24)
  is folded into Fig. 2b,c. Likely referee-driven.
- c5 (added). New Methods section "Identification and characterization of trions" (new p7)
  describes the MIT-based outlining, the expansion with B and that the trion regions "move up in
  Vb due to the valley Zeeman splitting". All figure captions now say trion boundaries were
  "identified from the Rxx measurements" and give Δ for each device (new p3-p4, p12-p18).
- c6 (reframed). Why (X+X−) is more insulating. Old: "because holes in negative trions are much
  more tightly bound" (old p4). New: Rxx is set by free holes from exciton dissociation in
  (X+X−), and their density is lower "because the exciton binding energy is an order of magnitude
  larger" than the trion binding energy (new p2). Likely referee-driven.

## Optical identification and binding energy

- c7 (editorial/correction). RC line cut now quoted at Vg = −0.08 V (new p3), matching the figure;
  old text said −0.06 V (old p3). RC figure now Fig. 3 (new p3), with Rxx,drag map at 6 T
  (device 2, Δ = 11 V). The old text linking the 6 T drag map to the 4 T Rxx map of another device
  (old p3) is gone.
- c8 (new_data, new_analysis). Binding-energy estimate rebased. Old: shift relative to "the
  expected injection threshold without Coulomb interactions" (red line), unexplained (old p8). New:
  shift between the minority-valley threshold at 1.5 K and at 10 K, where "interlayer Coulomb
  interactions are negligible" (new p7). New 10 K RC panels: ED Fig. 5c (W layer) and ED Fig. 6d
  (Mo layer, negative trions) (new p15-p16). Value unchanged (25 mV, ≈1.2 meV); the capacitance
  formula, gs gv = 2, mh = 0.45 m0 and d ≈ 2 nm are unchanged, and "good agreement" with the
  0.7 meV transport scale is retained (new p7). Likely referee-driven.
- c9 (added caveat). Methods note that RC in the (X+X+) region is finite at 1.5 K "although it is
  very weak in its lower part" (new p7).
- c10 (editorial/correction). Negative-trion RC figure (now ED Fig. 6, new p16) relabels the trion
  zone as "region III", adds trion boundaries from Rxx and a 10 K panel. The red/black dashed-line
  caption wording (Mo- and W-layers) is unchanged. No negative-trion binding-energy value is given.

- c11 (added). Bilayer MoSe2 "behaves effectively like a monolayer" (new p2) because "under high
  Δ, electrons are confined to the layer that is closer to the W layer" (new p7). Old: only a
  mechanical-stability remark (old p2). Likely referee-driven.
- c12 (editorial). Device relabeling (see top). ED Fig. 1 now shows devices 1 and 3 (the transport
  Hall device), as before but renamed (old p7, new p7, p10). The optical device (new device 2)
  is still not imaged. Each figure now states Δ.
- c13 (added). Measurement-circuit detail: the a.c. bias is applied through a transformer across a
  potentiometer whose ends connect to electrodes Mo-10 and Mo-7, keeping the time-averaged Mo-layer
  potential at Vb (new p7); ED Fig. 2a names pin Mo-10 for Vb (new p11). No leakage, antisymmetrization
  or excitation-dependence data were added.

- c14 (reframed). Fig. 3a,b,d and Fig. 4 merged into a new Fig. 4 (new p4): Rxy,drag and Rxy maps

- c15 (new_data, moved). The old Fig. 3c (Rxy vs B and Vg at Vb ≈ 0.88 V) moves to ED Fig. 4e

- c16 (softened). Crossover temperature: old "decreases gradually from ≈8K (or 0.7meV) near charge

- c17 (reframed). Hall-density description: new text says nH at 1.5 K "vanishes at Vg = 0, where

## Transport model

- c18 (new_analysis). Model rewritten (new p4, p7-p8). Main text: states assumptions ("perfect
  Coulomb drag for excitons and trions and negligible frictional drag for free electrons and
  holes"; free electrons ignored in the positive-trion region and free holes in the negative one);
  gives a no-exciton limit Rxy ≈ −(B/e)(1/nT + 1/(ne/2)) and explains it by counterflow of free
  electrons (new p4). Methods: Σ− and Σ+ written as separate equations (2), (3); Eq. (6) linearizes
  in "a small perpendicular magnetic field"; Eqs. (7), (8) now give Rxx, Rxy, Rxx,drag and Rxy,drag
  for both trion types (new p8); free-hole neglect justified by the larger exciton binding energy
  (new p8). The trion blocks remain asymmetric ([2,−2],[−1,1]), as in old Eq. (1) (old p9). The
  model gives Rxy,drag ≈ −B/(ne e) in (X+X−) (new p8), which depends only on the free-electron
  density. No fit to data is shown. Likely referee-driven.
- c19 (retained claim, reframed). "The negative Hall resistance unambiguously demonstrates the
  presence of Coulomb drag and the trion Hall effect" (new p4) replaces "unambiguously demonstrates
  the presence of a trion Hall effect and trion drags" (old p6). Essentially kept.
- c20 (added). "Rxy ≈ B/(nh e) (for small B fields)" states the regime (new p4).

## Quantum oscillations and conclusion

- c21 (new_data, new_analysis). New ED Fig. 7 (new p17): temperature-dependent oscillation
  amplitude in (X+X+) and pi regions with a Lifshitz-Kosevich fit; cyclotron mass ≈0.65 me in
  (X+X+), "substantially smaller than the expected trion mass (≈ 1.4-1.6 me)". The conclusion uses
  this to argue the oscillations are not trion Landau levels (new p4-p5). The old claim that the
  vertical stripes "demonstrate the survival of exciton binding" (old p6) is removed. Oscillation
  orientation is now described in the main text (vertical in pi and (X+X+), diagonal in pn, new p3).
  Likely referee-driven.
- c22 (unchanged number). The free-hole cyclotron energy at 4 T is still "about 0.8 meV" (new p5,
  old p6), with mh ≈ 0.45 m0 still in Methods (new p7).
