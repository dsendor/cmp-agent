## Symmetry argument and what the CD shows

- **c5 (reframed).** The explanation of why time reversal alone does not force RIXS-CD to vanish was rewritten. Old: "non-Hermiticity inherent in the coherent RIXS process" (old p3). New: based on the boundary conditions of the experiment, the time-reversed process not contributing to the measured intensity (new p3). Likely referee-driven (clarification).
- **c7 (added caveat).** New paragraph (new p4): the CD onset below T_N "does not, by itself, uniquely identify bi-altermagnetism"; it is the m vs m' selection rule that identifies the signature, and a ferrimagnetic phase would obey different selection rules. Likely referee-driven.
- **c9 (added caveat).** New paragraph (new p4): the local ionic model "does not directly reveal the momentum-dependent spin splitting", the hallmark of altermagnetism; its size needs future ARPES. Likely referee-driven.
- **c10 (removed / reframed).** The comparison with other probes was rewritten (old p4, new p4). The claim that XMCD and AHE "generally do not have sublattice resolution" was removed. New text cites RIXS-CD in ferro- and ferrimagnets (new Refs. 42, 43), adds alpha-Fe2O3 (Ref. 46) to the list of high-T_N altermagnets where the paramagnetic phase is inaccessible, and says that magnon RIXS-CD remains controversial while dd excitations allow a simple model analysis. The sentence on MnTe/CrSb not showing the disappearance moved from old p3 into this paragraph. Likely referee-driven.

## Models and simulations

- **c8 (added).** Simulation description (new p4): the two Oh (and two Td) magnetic sites have different crystal-field Hamiltonians, related by crystallographic symmetries, unlike in conventional collinear antiferromagnets. Likely referee-driven.
- **c11 (new analysis).** New simulation of a ferrimagnetic phase (ferromagnetic within each Oh and Td unit, antiparallel between them) at the same 3 meV Weiss field (new p4-5 main text; SI pp. 15-16; new Fig. S9 on SI p. 22). Result: in the ferrimagnetic phase CD is allowed in both m and m' geometries and is much stronger; in the bi-altermagnetic phase the m-plane CD vanishes and the two sublattices "exhibit partial cancellation in the m′ geometry". Fig. S9 also shows the bi-altermagnetic simulation for the m-plane geometry, per site. Presented as a route for future studies of competing orders. Likely referee-driven.
- **c13 (strengthened).** "a CD signal that is well reproduced by the atomic/impurity model" (old p5) became "the measured CD is quantitatively reproduced by an atomic (impurity) model" (new p5); "wave-vector dependence" of the dipole operators became "angle dependence". No new quantitative comparison accompanies the word "quantitatively". Probably a rewording in the rewrite of this section.
