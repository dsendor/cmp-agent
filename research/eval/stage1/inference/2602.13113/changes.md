# Changes between arXiv v1 and the accepted version (2602.13113)

Old: arXiv v1, 57 pages (main text pp. 2-16, references pp. 17-24, supplement pp. 26-57).
New: accepted journal version, 10 pages, main text and Methods only. The new supplement is
not available here, so changes inside it are seen only through the main-text pointers.
Inferred from the text alone; no referee reports or response letter were available.

## Title, abstract and framing

- Abstract narrowed (old p. 2, new p. 1). Old: "generalizes across materials, establishing
  design principles for ultrafast control of material properties". New: "generalizes across
  antiferromagnetic insulators", "design principles for ultrafast control of antiferromagnetic
  order". Likely referee-driven (scope of the generality claim).
- Introduction scope (old p. 3, new p. 2): "the electronic, magnetic, and lattice systems"
  becomes "the magnetic and lattice systems". The electronic subsystem is no longer claimed
  as studied. Possibly referee-driven.
- "Conclusions" renamed "Discussion" (new p. 7). Editorial (journal format). The Discussion
  still says the processes are "generalizable to any gapped magnetic insulators" and keeps the
  attosecond sentence (new p. 7), so the narrowing is in the abstract only.

## Claims and arguments in the main text

- Energy dependence of the upturn (new p. 3, after Fig. 2e): new sentence explaining why the
  RDS spectrum lacks the RXD self-absorption dip, attributed to a larger near-surface magnon
  density with a depth profile. No new data. Likely referee-driven.
- High-fluence decrease of the upturn (old p. 4, new p. 4): "larger at temperatures closer"
  becomes "larger and nonlinear at temperatures closer". Small wording change.
- Peak-width argument (old p. 4, new p. 4): the claim that the RXD peak width stays constant
  now points to a supplementary figure (Fig. S4) instead of Fig. 2C, whose top is saturated.
  This suggests a new supplementary plot of the peak profile versus delay. Likely
  referee-driven.
- New sentences (new p. 4): the excitation "does not quench the AFM order parameter", so the
  recovery "is not described by the Kibble-Zurek mechanism" and coarsening of small domains.
  New reference 34 (Orenstein et al., PRX 2025). Likely referee-driven.
- New analysis paragraph (new p. 4): tr-RDS traces show self-similarity (new Fig. S10a);
  using the scaling exponents of ref. 43 (Li, Glorioso, Rodriguez-Nieva, PRB 2023) the
  authors obtain a power-law exponent of 2.28 ± 0.08, close to 2.33 for an inverse particle
  cascade with conserved particle number (new Fig. S10b, Supplementary Note 8). Discrepancy
  attributed to dimensionality, non-conserving processes and finite pump duration. Likely
  referee-driven (more quantitative support for the quasi-thermalization picture).
- tr-NRDS thermal-expansion exclusion (old p. 8, new p. 6): now points to "Supplementary
  Note 9 and Fig. S11c" rather than only a figure. Minor; possibly a clarified SI section.
- New Discussion paragraph (new p. 7): tr-RDS captures ultrafast magnon dynamics that static
  techniques (inelastic neutron scattering, EELS, Brillouin light scattering) cannot access.
  New refs 52 and 53. Likely referee-driven (positioning against other magnon probes).
- Unchanged and still present: "Magnon-electron scattering can be excluded since the
  electrons are already thermalized" (new p. 4); "The scattering rate (1/τRDS) is
  proportional to momentum (Fig. 3i)" (new p. 4); the hydrodynamic slowdown sentence (new
  p. 4); the ~59 µeV effective coupling (new p. 7); the twin-domain statement (new p. 8);
  T_N1 = 213 K and T_N2 = 230 K (new p. 8).
