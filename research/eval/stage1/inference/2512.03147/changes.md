# Changes from arXiv v1 to the accepted version (2512.03147)

Old = arXiv v1 (main text pp. 1-10, SI pp. 11-14). New = Communications Materials version (pp. 1-8).
The new text has no supplementary pages, so changes to the SI cannot be checked. Figures 2-5 have
the same captions and the same quoted frequencies; their content looks unchanged in the text layer.

## Title, abstract and framing

- **Title (c1, softened, minor).** "a new three-dimensional platform" (old p1) became "as
  three-dimensional platform" (new p1). The word "new" was dropped. The claim of a platform for exotic
  quantum phases stays.
- **Abstract and Introduction add STEM (c2).** The list of methods now includes "scanning transmission
  electron microscopy" (new p1, abstract and Introduction). Otherwise the abstract is unchanged,
  including "comprehensive characterization" and "access to novel correlated and topological states".
- **Conclusions softened (c8).** Old p6: "Our results show a promising first step of the realization of
  3D structures ... opening a route to engineered bulk phases". New p5: "Our results provide an initial
  step toward 3D structures ... subject to further spectroscopic and magnetic validation." The claim to
  have synthesized the compound "for the first time" stays (new p5).
- **Fig. 1f added (c9).** A new panel shows charge transfer from graphene to α-RuCl3 and the band
  evolution of ABC-stacked graphene (new p2 caption; Introduction now ends "as illustrated in Fig. 1").

## New data

- **STEM (c2).** New paragraph on new p3: cross-sectional STEM images (in the SI) "reveal an alternating
  contrast between adjacent layers". The authors say a fully resolved layer-by-layer pattern is not seen
  everywhere and call it "qualitative evidence". New Methods section "STEM measurements" (new p6:
  cryogenic HAADF, JEOL NEOARM, 80 kV, 100 K). New coauthor (ORNL) and acknowledgement of CNMS (new p1,
  p7). No EDS or composition analysis is mentioned.
- **Reproducibility (c3).** New p3: a second piece of the same S2 crystal shows the same β = 170 T peak
  and harmonics; "Measurements on multiple independently prepared samples reproduced the same FFT
  peak". No sample count, spread of frequencies or stage-4 repeat is given.
- **Mixed staging argument (c3).** New p3: disorder or mixed staging "would be expected to broaden or
  complicate the oscillation spectrum, which is not observed".
- **Hall carrier density (c4).** New p3: "Hall measurements yielded a carrier density of approximately
  7.8 × 10^20 cm^-3" from the slope of ρxy. No plot, no carrier sign, no stage named, and no comparison
  with the DFT charge transfer or the pocket areas. Conclusions now list "Hall effect" (new p5).

## Analysis and theory

- **Fit freedom (c6).** New p5 adds: "Our analysis shows that only a limited range of physically
  reasonable parameters, including charge transfer, reproduces the observed oscillation frequencies."
  The DFT numbers (511, 131/444, 70/296, 28/311/330 T; 0.1 eV shift giving 27/183 and 126/214 T) are
  unchanged (old p6, new p5).
- **Stacking faults (c12, softened).** "Stacking-fault effects further influence the observations"
  (old p6) became "may further influence" (new p5).
- **Ru magnetism (c5).** New p4 adds that α-RuCl3 remains rather insulating "and Ru atoms keeping their
  magnetic nature". The calculations are still non-spin-polarized GGA without U or SOC (new p4, p6).
- **Strain and magnetism caveat (c7).** New closing paragraph of Electronic properties (new p5): earlier
  heterostructure studies indicate tensile strain in α-RuCl3; it does not seem to affect transport but
  "may influence the magnetic behavior", left for future work.
- **Strain wording (c11, softened).** The strain in the reduced cell "impacts only" (old p4) became
  "impacts mostly" the α-RuCl3 electronic structure (new p3). "graphene bands can be reliably obtained"
  became "electronic bands can be reliably obtained" (new p3).
- **SI pointers made specific (c13, editorial).** "see Supplementary Material" became "see Figs. S3 and
  S5", "Figure S3", "Fig. S4" (new p3-p4).

## Unchanged items worth noting

  method and its "small systematic error", and the dhva cross-check on A|A only (new p3, p6).
- XRD assumptions 1-4, Table 1, the 29.5(A) typo, the "α-Cul3" typo in Table 1, and the a-axis values
  (new p2-p3, p5-p6).
- Fig. 2 frequencies and harmonic labels (β = 170/176 T, 2β = 359/347 T, α = 91 T) (new p2).
- Controls: Cl2-annealed Kish graphite, still shown only in ρ(T) (new p2).
