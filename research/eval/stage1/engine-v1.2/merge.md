Below are {n} independent reviews of the same condensed matter physics paper, written by separate runs of the same reviewer. Merge them into one review, then tier every concern yourself.

- Keep every distinct concern raised by any review. Two concerns are the same when they ask the authors for the same change; merge those into one, keeping the most specific wording and every concrete detail (figures, parameters, measurements) from each. Do not drop a concern because you would tier it low: a concern belongs in C rather than nowhere.
- Drop praise, summaries and repeated text.
- Ignore the headings the reviews put their concerns under. Those runs ranked concerns within the paper, so they put something under "must address" for every paper. Decide each concern's tier afresh, against this absolute bar:
  A. Must address: a referee at a top-tier journal would require this before accepting the paper, because without it a main claim is unsupported or likely wrong. Judge against what such referees actually demand, not against an ideal study: many good papers have no item here, and a paper ready for publication has none. Do not fill this heading to match the others.
  B. Should address: a claim is weaker, narrower or less certain without this; a referee might ask for it, but the paper's main conclusions stand without it.
  C. Minor.
  Number the concerns 1, 2, 3 and so on across all three headings, most important first. A concern raised by several reviews is not automatically more important; judge each by what it means for the paper's claims.
- Before finishing, check each A item once more: if the reviews show the paper already gives evidence that a reasonable referee would accept, even if it is not the evidence the reviewer would have chosen, move it to B.{paper_check}
- Write each concern as a self-contained paragraph: what is missing or wrong, where in the paper, why it matters, and what the authors should do.
- End each concern with a line "Raised by:" naming every review that raised it, by its label (for example "Raised by: r1, r3").

Output only the merged review.
