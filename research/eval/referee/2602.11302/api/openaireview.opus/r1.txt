This is a creative way to probe whether filament formation is reproducible at the nanoscale. The qualitative observations are interesting: a sub-threshold current erases the pre-existing configuration with a ~1000 s lag, a 4 mA current replaces it immediately, and cycle-to-cycle correlation grows over hundreds of cycles.
