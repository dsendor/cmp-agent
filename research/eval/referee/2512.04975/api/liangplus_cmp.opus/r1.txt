**Review outline:**

## 1. Significance and novelty

The paper combines high-pressure, low-temperature single-crystal XRD (ID15b, He medium, down to 20 K), high-temperature powder XRD, PBE structural relaxations and polarized Raman spectroscopy on flux-grown La₄Ni₃O₁₀. Its main claims are:
- the I4/mmm → P2₁/c (B2₁/a) transition is direct, with no intermediate Bmab phase;
- T_S falls roughly linearly from ~1030 K at ambient pressure to ~20 K at 14 GPa;
- the DW phase below ~130 K has lower symmetry than P2₁/c.

A reliable P–T structural phase diagram of La₄Ni₃O₁₀ is directly relevant to whether superconductivity requires the tetragonal phase. Mapping the boundary to 20 K under hydrostatic He pressure is a useful contribution. The novelty relative to Li et al. [22] depends on whether the Bmab/monoclinic distinction and the Raman symmetry argument hold. As presented, neither is yet secure.

## 2. Potential reasons for acceptance

- Low-temperature, hydrostatic (He-loaded) single-crystal data at 300, 80 and 20 K across the boundary. This is the first systematic tracking of T_S(P) down to near 0 K.
- Careful attention to crystallographic settings (P2₁/c ↔ B2₁/a, I4/mmm ↔ F4/mmm). This should reduce confusion in the literature about "large" monoclinic angles.
- An independent DFT estimate of P_c (~16.5 GPa at 0 K). It is broadly consistent with the extrapolated experimental boundary (~14–15 GPa).
- Polarized Raman data with a mode list (SI) that report substantially more low-temperature modes than Gim et al. [48] or Suthar et al. [49]. This is valuable for the field regardless of interpretation.
- Lattice parameters, distortions and equation-of-state fits at three temperatures (Fig. 4, Table S5).

## 3. Concerns

### A. Must address

**1. Monoclinic versus Bmab near the boundary is not decisively distinguished by the data shown.**
- *What the superlattice reflections can and cannot show.* The q = (½,½,0) reflections in the I-cell (Fig. 2b) arise from octahedral tilting at the zone boundary. They are equally present in Bmab, and B2₁/a is a translationengleiche subgroup of Bmab with the same cell. So these reflections establish the boundary of the tilted phase, not its monoclinicity.
- *What the Bmab exclusion currently rests on.* It rests on two things:
  - β deviating from 90° by a few hundredths of a degree at 300 K near 10.5 GPa (Fig. 4b, "negligible" at 300 K by the authors' own description);
  - an unspecified "much better" fit in Table S4.
- *What is needed:*
  - **β uncertainties.** Give β with realistic uncertainties near P_c. These should include pseudo-merohedral twin overlap, DAC systematics and refinement correlations, not only statistical esd's. "Error bars smaller than the symbols" is not adequate for a 0.02–0.05° effect.
  - **Significance test.** Report wR/GoF for Bmab and B2₁/a refined with identical twin models and identical reflection sets. Apply a Hamilton R-ratio test or equivalent, since B2₁/a has more free parameters.
  - **Bmab-forbidden reflections.** State whether any reflections violate Bmab-specific conditions (e.g., the b-glide) but are allowed in B2₁/a. Give their I/σ. Such reflections would be direct, model-independent evidence.
  - **Pressure sampling.** At 300 K the boundary is only bracketed between 10.5 and 11.3 GPa (Fig. 2b,c), and steps elsewhere are ~0.5–1 GPa. A Bmab window of ≲1 GPa, as Li et al. may report, could have been missed. Finer steps, especially at 80 and 20 K where β is larger, would make the claim far stronger.
- *Internal inconsistency.* The text calls the transition both "continuous" (p. 8) and "weak first-order" because of a jump in β (p. 8). These are incompatible. Hysteresis on compression versus decompression should be reported.

**2. The space-group/cell model, and the twinning explanation for B-centering violations, need explicit demonstration.**
- *Cell size relative to the literature.* The model has Z = 2 per primitive cell (8 × 4e + 2a, p. 13). As I read refs. [25, 37], the commonly reported monoclinic structure is P2₁/a with a primitive ~5.42 × 5.47 × 27.96 Å cell, i.e., twice the primitive cell used here.
- *Status of the extra reflections.* The weak reflections violating B-centering (Fig. 2c, yellow circles) are attributed to twin domains. In a primitive Z = 4 structure the same reflections would be intrinsic.
- *A sharp test.* In the twinned-B picture, each extra reflection should satisfy k + l = even (the 90°-rotated domain). No reflection should violate both h + l = even and k + l = even (e.g., h even, k even, l odd). Please:
  - tabulate the observed extra reflections against these conditions;
  - refine domain fractions explicitly;
  - show that a primitive Z = 4 model does not fit better.
- *Why it matters.* This determines how the present structure relates to refs. [25, 37]. It also controls the Raman counting argument (concern 3).

**3. The Raman evidence for a symmetry lowering below P2₁/c is not robust as presented.**
- *Mode counting depends on Z.* The argument (31 parallel modes > 24 A_g; 51 total > 48) assumes the Z = 2 cell. With a primitive Z = 4 cell, ~51 A_g + 51 B_g would be allowed and the count would be fully consistent with the room-temperature group.
- *Selection rules may not be clean.* Table I states that Y and Z "are in the (bc) plane but not aligned with a particular axis." For monoclinic 2/m with Y rotated by φ from b:
  - A_g contributes to the crossed channel in proportion to sinφ cosφ (α_cc − α_bb);
  - B_g contributes to the parallel channel in proportion to sinφ cosφ α_bc.
  The four twin domains (90° rotations) further mix the axes probed within the laser spot. Please give the polarization orientation relative to the crystal axes, the domain size relative to the spot, and angle-resolved polarization data for representative modes. The statement that "each mode clearly follows the Raman selection rules" (p. 16) should then be supported.
- *The P-1 exclusion is marginal.* It rests on 51 versus 48. In P-1 every mode appears in both channels, so the relevant number is distinct frequencies across yy and yz, with leakage-induced duplicates removed. Please demonstrate this from the SI list. The new modes are also highly asymmetric (+16 in yy, +1 in yz), which deserves comment.
- *The inference itself is overreaching.* An incommensurate DW folds finite-Q phonons to Γ without requiring any point-group change, as the authors themselves note on pp. 15–16. Assigning Pc/P2₁ and proposing a "third structural phase" is therefore not justified by Raman alone. The abstract and conclusions should be reworded, or independent evidence for inversion breaking provided (e.g., SHG, or coincidence of new Raman frequencies with IR/DFT Au/Bu modes).

**4. The DFT calculation cannot carry the weight of "unequivocally" excluding intermediate phases.**
- *It is static and T = 0.* Li et al.'s Bmab phase was found at finite (room) temperature. Entropic or anharmonic stabilization of an intermediate phase at 300 K is not addressed by a T = 0 relaxation, so "fully corroborate" should be qualified.
- *The relaxation preserves the starting symmetry.* ABINIT relaxation does this, and the authors note sensitivity to the starting structure and force threshold (p. 21). Please state:
  - how symmetry was broken in the starting structures (random displacements? multiple starts?);
  - the spin configuration (nonmagnetic? the ambient-pressure SDW is ignored?), the use of +U, the smearing and the k-mesh convergence.
- *Energy scales are not reported.* The quoted convergence (1 mHa ≈ 27 meV/cell) may exceed the relevant tilt and monoclinic energy differences. Please report total-energy differences between symmetry-constrained Bmab and P2₁/c relaxations versus pressure.
- *The Pc agreement is fragile.* "P_c = 16.47 ± 0.04 GPa" comes from fitting only three points per order parameter, with the exponent fixed, on a ~1 GPa grid. A finer grid near P_c (e.g., 0.2 GPa steps over 14–17 GPa) is needed. The X-point phonon frequencies of I4/mmm versus pressure would give an independent soft-mode determination.
- *The cell limits what can be explored.* The Z = 2 cell cannot represent a Z = 4 P2₁/a structure or other larger-cell tilt patterns. The exclusion applies only to subgroups representable in that cell.

### B. Should address

**5. A Landau/isotropy-subgroup analysis is missing.**
- A single X-point tilt irrep of I4/mmm yields orthorhombic or tetragonal isotropy subgroups (cf. LTO/LTT/LTLO in La₂CuO₄). Reaching P2₁/c generally requires a second irrep or strain coupling.
- A direct continuous I4/mmm → P2₁/c transition would then be either first order or multicritical. That bears directly on the central claim and on the "jump in β."
- Please identify the active irreps (e.g., with ISODISTORT) and discuss the DFT scaling. In the DFT, θ ∝ (P_c−P)^½ and b−a ∝ (P_c−P), consistent with secondary strain. β−90 is fitted as ∝ (P_c−P)^½ and has a maximum at 4 GPa. What does this imply about whether monoclinicity is primary or induced?

**6. Phase-diagram construction (Fig. 1).**
- *Too few points for the claimed trend.* Only four boundary points exist (0 GPa/1030 K, ~10.5 GPa/300 K, ~13 GPa/80 K, ~14 GPa/20 K). The "linear suppression" between 0 and 10 GPa above 300 K is an interpolation with no data. Either add intermediate-temperature points or state that the line is schematic.
- *The criterion changes.* Boundaries are defined variously by peak broadening (Fig. 2a), superlattice appearance (Fig. 2b) or β. Use one criterion, preferably superlattice integrated intensity versus P with an extrapolation to zero. Show bracketing pressures as horizontal error bars.
- *Protocol is not stated.* Was pressure changed at low temperature or at room temperature followed by cooling? How was the ruby read at 20–80 K (in situ, with temperature correction)?
- *Literature disagreement.* The room-temperature P_c ≈ 10.5–11.3 GPa here is well below the ~15 GPa quoted from refs. [19–22] in the introduction. This needs discussion: medium, oxygen content, criterion.

**7. The XRD null result for DW satellites needs a quantitative sensitivity limit.**
- *Upper bound.* For the DESY P24 data (50 µm crystal, 10 s/° after binning), give an upper bound on satellite intensity relative to strong Bragg peaks at the wavevectors reported in [25], including all twin-related positions. Show line cuts through the expected positions above and below T_DW.
- *Lattice-parameter anomalies.* The temperature dependence of a, b, c and β across 130 K at ambient pressure (only 230 and 80 K refinements are reported) is directly relevant. Negative thermal expansion of b was reported in [36–38]. Its absence or presence would test "no discernible structural distortion."
- *Is the DW present in these crystals?* Please provide resistivity and/or magnetization on the same batch establishing T_DW. Without this, the null XRD result and the Raman anomaly (~120–150 K) cannot be tied to the DW in these crystals.

**8. Sample characterization and phase purity.**
- *Oxygen stoichiometry.* EPMA confirms La:Ni = 4:3 but δ in La₄Ni₃O₁₀₋δ is not quantified (e.g., TGA or iodometry). Structure, T_S and the DW are known to depend on δ, and Li et al. explicitly study La₄Ni₃O₁₀₋δ.
- *Side phases and their relevance to Raman.* Powder XRD shows NiO and La₂NiO₄ side phases. La₂NiO₄₊δ hosts stripe order at ~110–220 K depending on δ, with associated new Raman modes. Intergrowths of n = 1 or n = 2 layers would also contribute. The Raman crystal's purity should be demonstrated, e.g., by spatial Raman maps, 00l scans or HAADF, together with reproducibility of the new modes on several crystals.
- *Crystal provenance.* State how many crystals were measured in each experiment (DAC runs at 300, 80, 20 K; DESY; Raman). Say whether the Raman and XRD crystals are the same or from the same batch. The text says only "the same technique" on p. 15.

**9. The superconductivity statement is stronger than the evidence.** "This affirms that superconductivity emerges only once the system is restored to the tetragonal phase" (p. 17) is not tested here: there are no transport or magnetic measurements under pressure on these crystals. The introduction notes SC onsets reported from 5 to 40 GPa depending on medium, some of which fall inside the monoclinic region of Fig. 1. Rephrase as "consistent with," or add transport data.

**10. Raman temperature scale and inconsistent transition temperatures.**
- *Temperature calibration.* The 26 K base temperature includes laser heating from 2 K. State the spot size and power density, the method of temperature determination (Stokes/anti-Stokes?), and how heating was corrected at each temperature in Fig. 6c–e.
- *Inconsistent T values.* The text gives T_DW as 135 K (abstract), 130 K, "between 120 and 150 K," and "below ~120 K." Please unify and justify.

**11. Equation-of-state fits (Fig. 4d).**
- K₀ rises from 155(4) GPa at 300 K to 182–185 GPa at 80/20 K, a ~18% stiffening on cooling. This is surprisingly large.
- Please state whether K′ was fixed and over which pressure range the fit was done. A fit across the 2/m–4/mmm boundary mixes phases.
- Compare quantitatively with [19] rather than only stating "good agreement."

**12. Consistency between figures and text.**
- *Tilt at 6 GPa.* Fig. 3b quotes Ni1–O2–Ni2 = 171.1° at 6 GPa (tilt 8.9°). The 300 K tilt in Fig. 3c at ~6 GPa appears lower; please check.
- *Point near 11 GPa.* Fig. 3c shows a 300 K point near ~11 GPa with a nonzero tilt, while Fig. 2 states the lattice is F4/mmm at 11.3 GPa. Clarify the pressures.
- *DFT–experiment tilt comparison.* The claimed agreement in θ scaling with experiment "at 80 K" (p. 11) relies on 80 K data that stop at ~9.5 GPa, well below the 80 K boundary (~13 GPa).
- *Lattice parameter b.* The text quotes b ≈ 5.43 Å, while the DFT comparison (b = 5.51 Å, "slightly above 1%") implies an experimental b ≈ 5.45 Å. Give the actual 20 K/0 GPa values used.

### C. Minor

13. Give the P2₁/c ↔ B2₁/a and I4/mmm ↔ F4/mmm transformation matrices in the main text, not only the SI. Also state explicitly which axis is the stacking axis in each setting; the DFT section says "along a and doubled" in P2₁/c, which differs from the B2₁/a convention used elsewhere.

14. "Least symmetric monoclinic spacegroup 14" (p. 10) should read "lowest-symmetry group considered." The chain P2₁/c ⊂ Bmab ⊂ Fmmm ⊂ I4/mmm should specify the cell and origin relations.

15. Fig. 5 fonts do not render in the PDF (garbled axis labels on p. 12). In the panels, distinguish fitted from guide-to-the-eye curves more clearly.

16. Typos and naming:
   - author name "Gabarino" (affiliation list) versus "Garbarino" (refs. 45, 46, 60);
   - "propoposal," "PsuedoDojo," "the the," "viceversa," "monotonously" (→ monotonically), "At on the onset" (Fig. 2 caption);
   - Table I writes "P_c" with a subscript for the space group Pc;
   - the Sm₂Ru₃Ge₅ citation [54] concerns RE₂Ru₃Ge₅ polymorphs.

18. Fig. 2a: state the integration window and color scale. Show whether apparent "broadening" exceeds the instrumental and mosaic width measured on the same reflection in the 4/mmm phase.

## 4. Suggestions for improvement

1. **Strengthen the crystallography with symmetry analysis.** Add an isotropy-subgroup/Landau analysis of the active irreps. Complement it with DFT phonon dispersions of I4/mmm at the X point versus pressure, giving a soft-mode P_c and a direct statement of which tilt pattern condenses first.
2. **Assign the new Raman modes.** Use the existing DFT setup to compute Γ-point Au/Bu (IR) frequencies and phonons at the DW wavevector of [25]. Compare these with the new low-temperature Raman modes. Matching IR frequencies would support Pc/P2₁; matching folded modes would support translational-symmetry breaking only. SHG through T_DW would independently test inversion breaking.
3. **Do one decisive diffraction experiment near the boundary.** Use fine pressure steps (≤0.25 GPa) at 80 or 20 K, where β is largest. Explicitly track Bmab-forbidden/B2₁/a-allowed reflections and twin-domain-resolved β. If possible use higher resolution (larger detector distance or analyzer), so that β − 90° can be determined to better than 0.01°.
4. **Deposit the refinement outputs.** Deposit CIFs and hkl files for representative pressures and temperatures, including Table S4/S5 refinement statistics with twin fractions. Add a short table that, for each claim (direct transition, T_S(P), DW null result, symmetry lowering), lists the decisive observable and its uncertainty.
