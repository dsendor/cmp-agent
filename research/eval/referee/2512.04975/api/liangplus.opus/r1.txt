Review outline:

**1. Significance and novelty**

The paper maps the tetragonal (I4/mmm) to monoclinic (P2₁/c) structural boundary of La₄Ni₃O₁₀ in pressure–temperature space. It uses helium-medium single-crystal XRD down to 20 K, ambient-pressure high-temperature PXRD and PBE relaxations. It argues the transition is direct, with no intermediate Bmab phase, and that the boundary reaches ~14 GPa at 20 K. It also reports Raman modes appearing near T_DW and proposes a third, symmetry-lowered density-wave phase.

A low-temperature, hydrostatic structural phase diagram is directly relevant to whether superconductivity requires tetragonal symmetry. The Bmab-versus-P2₁/c question is a live controversy (Ref. 22). As written, however, the discriminating evidence for both headline claims (no Bmab; symmetry below 2/m) is less decisive than the language ("unequivocally", "definitely ruled out") suggests.

**2. Potential reasons for acceptance**

- Single-crystal data at 300, 80 and 20 K under helium, over a dense pressure grid, give a low-temperature boundary (~13 GPa at 80 K, ~14 GPa at 20 K). This boundary is directly comparable to the reported superconducting onsets.
- Careful treatment of settings (P2₁/c↔B2₁/a, I4/mmm↔F4/mmm) is useful to the community. It removes the misleading "β ≈ 100°" impression.
- Direct images of reflection broadening and splitting (Fig. 2a) and of the emergence of q = (½,½,0) superlattice reflections (Fig. 2b) are informative.
- The DFT relaxation in the lowest common subgroup is a sensible strategy. The stated sensitivity to the force threshold is an honest and useful technical observation.
- The Raman data are high quality. The temperature tracking of anomalous modes (Fig. 6e) adds to a debated topic (Refs. 48–50).

**3. Concerns**

**A. Must address**

1. **The experimental discrimination between Bmab and B2₁/a is not yet demonstrated.**
   - *Superlattice reflections do not discriminate.* The q = (½,½,0) reflections and the "2-fold superstructure" (Fig. 2b; p. 5–6) arise from octahedral tilting. They are equally expected for Bmab, which also doubles the primitive I4/mmm cell. They show only that the structure is not I4/mmm.
   - *β cannot carry the argument at 300 K.* The text says the monoclinic distortion at 300 K is "negligible" (p. 8), and Fig. 4b shows β ≈ 90.0–90.05° across the 2/m region. Yet the room-temperature onset (10.5 GPa) is the point the authors cite against Li et al.
   - *Fit quality is not yet a test.* "Much better" fit quality (Table S4) for a lower-symmetry, multi-domain twinned model is expected simply from the additional parameters and twin fractions. The authors should:
     - report R/wR, GoF, number of parameters and refined twin fractions for both models at each pressure;
     - apply a Hamilton R-ratio or similar significance test;
     - refine Bmab with its own appropriate twin law.
   - *Reflection conditions are not analysed.* The authors should list the reflection classes allowed in B2₁/a but forbidden in Bmab (e.g. those from the b-glide ⊥ c). They should then show whether such reflections are observed at positions that cannot be generated by any of the four twin domains. The weak "centering-violating" spots in Fig. 2c are attributed to twinning. This needs to be shown quantitatively, e.g. by predicting twin-domain positions and intensities, and those spots then cannot simultaneously serve as evidence for monoclinic symmetry.
   - *Pressure resolution near the boundary is coarse.* An intermediate Bmab window narrower than the pressure step (~0.5–1 GPa at 300 K; 12.5→15 GPa at 20 K) cannot be excluded. Finer steps across the boundary at one temperature would be decisive.
   - *The order of the transition is stated inconsistently.* p. 8 calls it "weak first-order" because of a jump in β, then two sentences later "continuous". This matters: within Landau theory, a direct continuous I4/mmm→P2₁/c transition generally requires the order parameter to lie along a low-symmetry direction of the active irrep, or two coupled irreps. This is exactly why an intermediate Bmab step is the generic expectation. The authors should give the group-theoretical description (irrep(s) and order-parameter direction, e.g. via ISODISTORT mode decomposition of the refined structures) and state which scenario their data support, including any hysteresis.

2. **The DFT result is not shown to be robust enough to "definitely rule out" Bmab.**
   - *Energy convergence.* Total energies are converged to 1 mHa (~27 meV/cell), and the relaxation path depends on a force threshold of 5·10⁻⁶ Ha/Bohr. This implies an extremely flat landscape, where Bmab–P2₁/c enthalpy differences may lie below the stated convergence. The authors should:
     - show the enthalpy difference H(P2₁/c) − H(Bmab) and H(Bmab) − H(I4/mmm) versus P, from constrained relaxations;
     - demonstrate convergence of these differences with respect to k-mesh (2×4×4 is coarse for a metal), smearing scheme and width (not stated), and cutoff.
   - *Magnetism and correlation.* Whether the calculation is spin-polarized, and whether U is included, is not stated. Given the SDW, this should be specified and its effect on the tilt and monoclinic instability tested.
   - *Simultaneous vanishing is not independent evidence.* That the three order parameters vanish at the same P_c (16.47 ± 0.04 GPa) largely follows from symmetry, because strain and β are secondary order parameters coupled to the tilt. The ±0.04 GPa reflects this coupling and fits to only three points. The relevant test is whether β − 90° stays non-zero right up to P_c. The 1 GPa grid (last point at 16 GPa) leaves a possible sub-GPa Bmab window open, so a finer grid near P_c is needed.
   - *Scaling exponents.* The α = ½ scaling for β, a strain-like quantity, contrasts with the linear scaling used for (b−a)/(a+b). The authors should explain this and the β maximum at ~4 GPa in terms of the coupling terms.

3. **The Raman inference of symmetry "below 2/m" does not follow from the data as presented.**
   - *Polarization geometry.* Table I states that Y and Z are "in the (bc) plane but not aligned with a particular axis". For b-unique P2₁/c this mixes Ag (bb, cc) and Bg (bc) responses in both yy and yz channels. That undermines the assignment of 24 allowed modes per channel and the "31 > 24" argument. Combined with four twin domains, both channels may contain both symmetries. The authors should:
     - give the crystal face, the in-plane axis orientation and the domain population of the measured spot;
     - provide angle-resolved polarization data;
     - verify that no mode is counted in both channels.
   - *The P1̄ exclusion rests on a narrow margin* (51 vs 48 modes). The count is acknowledged to be extraction-dependent, so it needs the explicit mode list with fit uncertainties in the main text and a sensitivity analysis.
   - *Mode counts constrain translational, not point, symmetry.* A density wave with finite q produces new Γ-point modes by zone folding while possibly preserving 2/m point symmetry. The candidate list (P1̄, Pc, P2₁, P1) contains only translationengleiche subgroups, which is inconsistent with a finite-q density wave (acknowledged on p. 16). The statements "below 2/m symmetry" (p. 4) and "breaking of 2/m symmetry" (p. 16) should be revised to what the data support: loss of P2₁/c translational and/or point symmetry.
   - *Other origins must be excluded*, beyond calling the new features phonons:
     - DW amplitude modes, which should soften towards T_DW;
     - magnetic or two-magnon excitations associated with the SDW;
     - previously allowed but unresolved modes that sharpen on cooling.
     The authors should show that new-mode intensities grow from zero like an order parameter below T_DW, rather than gradually emerging as linewidths narrow.
   - *Reproducibility and impurities.* Given the reported contradiction with Gim et al. (no new modes) and Suthar et al. (48 modes), at least two crystals should be shown. Contributions from intergrowths or side phases (NiO and La₂NiO₄ are reported in the batch) should be excluded.

4. **The density-wave state in these specific crystals is not established, and the XRD null result is not quantified.**
   - No resistivity, susceptibility or specific-heat data on this flux-grown batch are shown to confirm T_DW and its sharpness. Oxygen content (La₄Ni₃O₁₀₋δ) is not determined; EPMA confirms only La:Ni. Both are essential before attributing a "third structural phase" to DW ordering.
   - "No satellites" should come with a detection limit, e.g. an upper bound on I_sat/I_main for the reported Zhang et al. wavevector, with targeted reciprocal-space cuts at that q.
   - The ambient-pressure P24 data at 230 and 80 K should be used to look for anomalies across T_DW in lattice parameters (e.g. the reported negative thermal expansion of b, Refs. 36–38), atomic displacement parameters, or diffuse scattering. A dense temperature series would directly connect the Raman and XRD observations.

**B. Should address**

5. **Phase diagram support and literature discrepancy.**
   - Fig. 1 rests on four own points (1030 K/0 GPa; ~300 K/10.5 GPa; ~80 K/13 GPa; 20 K/14 GPa). There is no data between 0 and ~10 GPa above room temperature, so the "linear suppression" is an interpolation.
   - Near T → 0 the boundary must become vertical (Clausius–Clapeyron with ΔS → 0). Linear extrapolation to compare with the 0 K DFT P_c should therefore be qualified.
   - The introduction states a room-temperature transition at P_c ≈ 15 GPa (Refs. 19–22), whereas the authors find 10.5–11.3 GPa. This ~4 GPa difference should be discussed (helium versus other media, oxygen stoichiometry, criteria for the transition).
   - As rendered, the shaded 2/m/4/mmm boundaries in Fig. 4 (notably at 300 K) appear at higher pressure than in Figs. 1 and 2c. Please make them consistent.

6. **Lattice-parameter and β uncertainties are understated.**
   - The Fig. 4 caption says error bars are smaller than the symbols. Yet β at 80 K and 20 K (Fig. 4b) scatters non-monotonically by ~0.3° between adjacent pressures, far exceeding the implied errors.
   - Under pseudo-merohedral twinning with partial overlap, cell parameters from peak positions carry systematic errors. The authors should explain how a, b and β are extracted (domain-resolved indexing?) and give realistic systematic uncertainties, which matter given that β − 90° ~ 0.1° is the key discriminant.

7. **The superconductivity statement is not tested here.** "This affirms that superconductivity emerges only once the system is restored to the tetragonal phase" (p. 17) relies on transport data from other groups, other samples and other pressure media; P_c spans 5–40 GPa per Ref. 19. Either add transport or susceptibility under pressure on the same batch, or soften the statement to consistency with the low-temperature boundary (~14 GPa at 20 K).

8. **Equation of state.**
   - K₀ changes from 155(4) to 182(4) GPa between 300 and 80 K, a large change for 220 K. The authors should state whether K′ was fixed, whether data from both phases were fitted jointly, and whether a volume discontinuity occurs at the (weakly first-order?) transition.
   - Separate fits per phase or an F–f plot would clarify this.

9. **Quantitative DFT–experiment comparison is overstated.**
   - DFT gives β − 90° = 0.42° at 0 GPa versus 0.1–0.2° measured.
   - The DFT orthorhombicity (~7×10⁻³) should be compared explicitly with Fig. 4c.
   - Tilt "agreement" with Fig. 3c at 80 K is questionable: experimental tilt there is ~7–10° at 9–9.5 GPa versus ~12° in DFT, and 80 K tilt data stop at ~9.5 GPa although the transition is at ~13 GPa. Please add tilt data between 9.5 and 13 GPa at 80 K, and tabulate the comparison.

10. **Sample characterisation.**
    - Report whether the XRD and Raman crystals were checked for RP intergrowths or stacking faults. The streaks along l in Fig. 2b should be addressed.
    - State whether the same crystal or batch was used for each technique.
    - The claim that the transition is "impervious" to growth method is drawn only from flux crystals here plus literature, and should be phrased accordingly.

**C. Minor**

11. T_DW is quoted inconsistently: 135 K (abstract), 130 K (introduction, conclusions), "between 120 and 150 K" and "below ~120 K" (p. 13–14), "~130 K" (Fig. 6 caption). Please unify, with uncertainty.
12. The Table I caption says "the Poynting vector is perpendicular to X", contradicting the Porto notation X(YY)X̄ and the main text. Clarify which crystal axis or face is probed. The DFT setting has the stacking axis along a and doubled, which differs from the Raman "standard setting"; state the settings explicitly for each technique.
13. In the mode counting for Pc and P2₁, subtract the three acoustic modes (e.g. 50 A + 49 B optical modes for P2₁) and adjust Table I.
14. p. 8: "At 15 GPa, the octahedra is perfect" conflicts with the preceding statement that the octahedra are strongly distorted. This presumably means "untilted".
15. Define β in the DFT analysis unambiguously. "Primitive cell in the B2₁/a setting" is contradictory, since B2₁/a is centered.
16. For ruby pressures at 20–80 K, state the temperature correction used. For Raman, state how the 26 K sample temperature under laser heating was determined (e.g. Stokes/anti-Stokes ratio).
17. Typos and consistency: "Gabarino" in the author list vs "Garbarino" in Refs. 45 and 60; "PsuedoDojo"; "propoposal"; reference formatting (lower-case chemical formulas in Refs. 4, 6, 26–34). Toning down "unequivocally" and "definitely" would better match the evidence.

**4. Suggestions for improvement**

1. Add a symmetry-mode (ISODISTORT-type) decomposition of the refined structures versus pressure and temperature, separating the tilt components and strains. Pair it with DFT enthalpy curves for constrained Bmab, Fmmm and P2₁/c structures and a fine pressure grid near P_c. This would turn the "no Bmab" claim into a quantitative, falsifiable statement.
2. Perform one dense pressure scan (≤0.2 GPa steps) across the boundary at a single temperature (e.g. 80 K). Include explicit tests of Bmab-forbidden reflection classes using domain-resolved indexing.
3. Strengthen the Raman case with:
   - angle-resolved polarization measurements on a well-oriented, single-domain-dominated spot;
   - a second crystal;
   - order-parameter-like temperature dependence of new-mode intensities;
   - transport or susceptibility on the same batch establishing T_DW.
4. Use the ambient-pressure SXRD to quantify upper limits on DW satellite intensity at the Zhang et al. wavevector. Track lattice parameters through T_DW, to connect the XRD and Raman pictures of the proposed third phase.
