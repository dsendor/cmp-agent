1. The B21/a vs Bmab discrimination, which is the central claim of a direct I4/mmm→P21/c transition with 'no trace' of Bmab, is not quantitatively demonstrated. (i) Commensurate q=(½,½,0) superlattice reflections arise in both models. (ii) β−90° is only ~0.1–0.2°, which the authors concede is close to experimental accuracy. (iii) The only model comparison (SI Table S4) is at 300 K / 10.5 GPa, where the paper itself calls the distortion 'negligible', i.e. where the models are least distinguishable. The authors should: (a) report R1, wR2, GoF, number of observed reflections and refined parameters, completeness, and refined twin fractions for both models at several (P,T) points, especially the lowest-pressure 20 K data, and apply a Hamilton R-ratio test; (b) identify reflection classes allowed in B21/a but forbidden in Bmab even after superposing the four twin domains, and show their observed vs calculated intensities with esds; (c) plot β−90° with esds vs pressure at each temperature; (d) refine the monoclinic model on tetragonal-phase data as a null test; (e) exclude Renninger multiple scattering and diamond/gasket artefacts for the weak B-centering-violating reflections, e.g. by ψ-scans or comparison of equivalents; (f) show that EVAL14 box integration of overlapping twin reflections does not bias weak discriminating intensities. Without this, the claim should read 'no evidence for Bmab within our resolution'.

2. The disagreement with prior work is not clearly framed. Li et al. [22] also observed B-centering-violating reflections at ~11 GPa and also ascribed them to twin domains, yet reached a different conclusion. The paper never states where the two interpretations diverge or which observable decides between them. The attribution of the intermediate Bmab model is also inconsistent: it is credited to ref. [37] for single crystals in one place and to Li et al. [22] elsewhere. A side-by-side comparison of the competing structural models and the data that discriminate them is needed.

3. The order of the transition is described inconsistently and never tested. The same paragraph calls the transition 'continuous, bypassing the orthorhombic phase' and reports a jump in β ('weak first-order'). This matters for the central claim. The B21/a structure involves more than the single X-point tilt order parameter that generates Bmab, so Landau theory predicts either sequential transitions (I4/mmm→Bmab→P21/c) or a direct first-order transition, not a direct continuous one. The authors should: (a) perform a symmetry-mode decomposition of the refined B21/a structures relative to the F4/mmm parent (e.g. ISODISTORT) and plot each primary mode amplitude vs pressure at 300, 80 and 20 K, showing whether all vanish at the same pressure; (b) report volume and β discontinuities with esds, compression/decompression hysteresis (none reported), and any phase coexistence; (c) use a single consistent description of the transition order.

4. The DFT does not 'unequivocally' demonstrate the absence of an intermediate phase. Only structural relaxations in a P21/c cell are reported, with no energy or enthalpy comparison of the competing phases. The methods admit that with a 5e-5 Ha/Bohr threshold the relaxation could stop in the tetragonal or an orthorhombic phase depending on the starting structure, that the phases are energetically very close, and that energies are converged to only 1 mHa. A relaxation endpoint is not proof that a phase is absent. The quoted Pc = 16.47 ± 0.04 GPa understates the uncertainty, since the authors estimate a 1–3 GPa PBE pressure error. A 0 K calculation also cannot exclude a finite-temperature Bmab phase, e.g. near 300 K and 10.5 GPa. The authors should: (a) compute ΔH(P) for symmetry-constrained, fully relaxed F4/mmm, Fmmm, Bmab and B21/a, with energy convergence well below the relevant differences; (b) compute phonons of the tetragonal phase vs pressure at the zone-boundary tilt modes, showing whether the Bmab- and B21/a-related instabilities appear at the same pressure, or map E(Q_Bmab, Q_mono) near Pc; (c) test robustness with PBEsol/SCAN, a range of +U values, spin-polarized configurations, a denser k-mesh than 2×4×4 with smearing stated, and explicitly listed starting structures; (d) compare with experiment at matched volume and discuss the 16.5 GPa (0 K) vs ~14 GPa (20 K) vs ~10.5–11 GPa (300 K) differences. Otherwise the wording should be 'consistent with a direct transition at 0 K'.

5. The P–T phase boundary is under-sampled and partly undocumented. The 'linear' suppression of TS from 1030 K (ambient, high-T PXRD on powder) to 20 K at 14 GPa is a dashed guide connecting powder data to single-crystal pressure data taken at only 300, 80 and 20 K. There are no data at intermediate temperatures under pressure and none above room temperature under pressure. The methods omit the pressure points and step size near the transition, the number of crystals and loadings, and how 80 K and 20 K were reached and controlled in the membrane DAC. The authors should tabulate all measured (P,T) points, state the pressure resolution near Pc (which bounds what 'no intermediate phase' can mean), and add isotherms at e.g. 150–250 K and ideally above room temperature with a resistively heated DAC. They should also remove the word 'linear' or justify it with a fit.

6. The room-temperature onset pressure (~10.5 GPa in the text, 11.3 GPa implied by Fig. 2(c)) differs by ~4 GPa from the ~15 GPa reported in the literature the paper cites [19–22], and the discrepancy is never discussed. The authors should reconcile this explicitly, considering pressure medium and hydrostaticity, oxygen stoichiometry, growth method, and the criterion used to define the onset. They should also give one consistent value for the onset pressure.

7. The claim that 'superconductivity emerges only once the system is restored to the tetragonal phase' is not supported by data in the paper. No resistance or susceptibility measurements are presented. The paper's own introduction notes reported critical pressures of 5–40 GPa depending on medium and Tc criterion, and the monoclinic phase here persists to ~14 GPa at 20 K, so some literature reports may lie inside the monoclinic region. The authors should ideally measure Tc(P) on crystals from the same batch in the same He medium and pressure scale, distinguishing onset from zero-resistance and filamentary from bulk superconductivity. At minimum they should overlay literature Tc(P) data on the structural boundary, annotated by medium and criterion, and discuss datasets showing superconductivity below ~14 GPa. Otherwise the claim should be softened to 'consistent with'.

8. The null result for density-wave satellites (no satellites down to 20 K, 'contrary to' Zhang et al.) lacks a detection limit and adequate temperature coverage. Ambient-pressure refinements were done only at 230 and 80 K on a single crystal, the temperatures measured within the 100–160 K DW window are not listed, and it is unclear which setup produced the 20 K ambient data. The paper itself suggests the modulation may be below detection threshold. The authors should: (a) give the minimum detectable satellite-to-Bragg intensity ratio at the literature q-vectors and state whether the reported satellites would have been visible; (b) show reciprocal-space maps along the expected q directions at several temperatures across 100–160 K with exposure statistics; (c) otherwise reword to 'not detected under our conditions'.

9. There is an unresolved tension between the XRD and Raman interpretations. The Raman section attributes the extra modes to zone-folding by a DW-driven lattice distortion. A distortion strong enough to fold finite-Q phonons into Raman activity implies a superstructure, yet XRD sees no satellites. The authors should reconcile these quantitatively, e.g. by estimating the displacement amplitude needed to produce the observed Raman intensities and comparing the resulting satellite intensity with the XRD detection limit.

10. The Raman-based proposal of a 'third structural phase' (P21 or Pc) rests on 51 observed modes vs 48 Raman-active modes in P21/c, a margin of three that is fragile. (i) The Y and Z axes lie in the bc plane but not along the crystal axes, so yy and yz each mix Ag and Bg modes, and four pseudo-merohedral twin domains add polarization leakage; the Porto notation is also used inconsistently. (ii) No spectral resolution, peak-fitting protocol, or model-selection criterion (e.g. BIC or an F-test for adding peaks) is given for decomposing ~50 overlapping peaks. (iii) NiO and La2NiO4 are present in the growth batch, and NiO has strong one- and two-phonon and two-magnon features, but no reference spectra or Raman mapping are shown; second-order and electronic/magnetic scattering are not excluded. (iv) The number of crystals measured and the repeatability are not reported, although the authors note strong sample dependence; there is no laser-power series or Stokes/anti-Stokes check of local heating. (v) Only same-cell subgroups are considered: P1 is not evaluated, and the supercell subgroups implied by the zone-folding interpretation are not analysed. Table I counts include acoustic modes. The authors should: measure with polarization along the crystal axes on single-domain regions with angular dependence; collect NiO and La2NiO4 reference spectra in the same setup; compute DFT Γ-point phonons for B21/a, P21 and Pc for assignment; measure 2–3 crystals; track each extra mode's intensity vs temperature to a single T*; and directly test broken inversion symmetry, e.g. by SHG vs temperature or IR/Raman coincidence.

11. Claims in the Abstract and Conclusion are stronger than the evidence and than the Results text. The Results call Raman 'not the most incisive probe' and say the assignment 'must ultimately be revisited', while the Abstract and Conclusion say the modes 'signify' DW-driven symmetry breaking and propose a third phase. Similar phrasings ('unequivocally demonstrate', 'fully corroborate', 'no trace', 'affirms', 'certainly rule out') should be calibrated to the evidence. Key quantities are also inconsistent across the paper: the DW / extra-mode onset is given as ~120 K, ~130 K and ~135 K; the room-temperature transition pressure as 10.5 vs 11.3 GPa; the transition order as continuous vs weak first-order; and the 300 K distortion is called 'negligible' while B21/a is claimed to fit clearly better at 300 K / 10.5 GPa. These should be made consistent.

12. Sample characterization, especially oxygen stoichiometry, is insufficient for the generality claimed. δ in La4Ni3O10−δ strongly affects structure and superconductivity, yet EPMA reports only that the La:Ni ratio is 'confirmed', with no numbers, uncertainties or oxygen content. The batch contains NiO and La2NiO4. The ambient TS (1030 K) comes from powder, while pressure data come from a single crystal. The paper claims the transition is 'impervious' to flux vs floating-zone growth, but only flux-grown crystals were studied, and elsewhere the authors say growth strategy 'plays a decisive role'. The authors should determine δ for the measured crystals (TGA reduction, neutron diffraction, or quantitative EPMA with uncertainties), state how many crystals were used for each technique and whether powder and crystals share a batch, and either measure floating-zone crystals or remove the growth-method claim. They should also discuss whether variation in δ could explain the onset-pressure discrepancy with the literature.

13. The equation-of-state analysis needs justification. Birch–Murnaghan fits give K0 = 155(4), 182(4) and 185(2) GPa at 300, 80 and 20 K. An ~18% stiffening between 300 and 80 K is unusually large and is not discussed. K0′ is not reported, nor whether it was fixed, and it is unclear whether a single EOS was fitted across the monoclinic–tetragonal transition and its possible volume discontinuity. Agreement with [19] is asserted but not shown. The authors should report K0′ and fit ranges, fit each phase separately, show F–f (normalized stress vs Eulerian strain) plots, and discuss the temperature dependence of K0.

14. **The novelty relative to prior high-pressure work is limited and not clearly delineated.**
   - The tetragonal I4/mmm phase near ~15 GPa, and superconductivity in that phase, were already reported in refs. [19–22].
   - The genuinely new elements are:
     - (i) the claimed absence of an intermediate orthorhombic phase;
     - (ii) the Ts(P) line;
     - (iii) the Raman "third phase";
     - (iv) the setting clarification.
   - The introduction should state explicitly which of these are new and which confirm prior results.
   - Item (iv) is standard crystallography, and no specific paper is named as having been misled by the β ≈ 100° non-standard setting.
     - Either cite the specific works that misinterpreted the distortion and show the consequence, or present this as a clarifying note rather than a contribution.

15. **The significance of P21/c versus Bmab for the physics is never argued.**
   - The paper's central claim is crystallographic.
   - It does not explain why the distinction matters for the electronic structure, the DW, or superconductivity. Examples would be different tilt patterns, interlayer coupling, or band-structure consequences.
   - Without this, the significance for the superconductivity community is unclear.

16. **The engagement with the closest competitor, Li et al. [22], is inadequate.**
   - Li et al. used the same probe and observed the same weak B-centring-violating reflections near ~11 GPa. Both groups attribute these to twinning.
   - Li et al. interpreted their data as an intermediate Bmab phase.
   - The present paper says only that "their interpretation differs" and that the issue is "not yet elucidated from a crystallographic point of view." It gives no reanalysis and does not identify which assumption differs (model, twin treatment, sample, pressure medium).
   - Li et al. also studied oxygen-deficient La4Ni3O10−δ, a possibly decisive difference that is not discussed.
   - A side-by-side comparison of data, models and fit statistics is needed for the central claim to be credible.

17. **The disagreement with Zhang et al. [25] is dismissed rather than resolved.**
   - Incommensurate DW satellites reported by XRD in [25] are called "not reproduced," with "limited quantitative analysis."
   - Yet the authors' own ambient data (P24, same batch as the DAC crystals) show no satellites, and no detection limit is quoted.
   - Please quantify an upper bound on satellite intensity relative to the main Bragg peaks, so that the non-observation is meaningful.

18. **The conflicts with prior Raman work are not reconciled.**
   - Gim et al. report no new low-temperature modes, and Suthar et al. report 48 modes. Deswal et al. [50] is cited only in passing.
   - The only explanation offered is that "samples' quality and exact composition seem to have important impact."
   - This needs to be substantiated, or the Raman claim softened (see C).

19. **The promised "unified framework" is not delivered.**
   - The abstract and introduction promise a framework linking the tetragonal, orthorhombic and monoclinic phases and the DW.
   - However:
     - the DW-versus-pressure boundary is not mapped (the authors call it "uncharted");
     - the DFT explicitly "cannot state anything about the possibility of a density wave";
     - the proposed DW-linked structural phase rests on Raman alone.
   - The goals stated in the introduction should match what is actually delivered.

20. **"Unequivocally" is not supported by discriminating evidence.**
   - The commensurate superlattice reflections at q = (½, ±½, 0) are expected for both Bmab and B21/a.
   - The B-centring-violating spots are attributed to 90° pseudo-merohedral twinning, which produces them for Bmab as well.
   - β − 90° is only ~0.1–0.2°, is "negligible" at 300 K at the onset, and is acknowledged to be near the experimental accuracy.
   - The analogies to SrAl4, KNbO3 and the other cited materials show that small monoclinic angles can exist, not that one exists here.
   - **Requests:**
     - Identify reflection classes that discriminate the two space groups and are robust to twinning. For example, in the hk0 layers already shown in Fig. 2a, mixed-parity hk0 reflections (k odd) are forbidden in every domain of Bmab by the b-glide but allowed in B21/a.
     - Exclude multiple (Renninger) scattering and diamond contributions with ψ-scans or orientation checks.
     - Otherwise remove "unequivocally."

21. **The model comparison is not quantified in the main text.**
   - Table S4 is summarised only as a fit that "is much better."
   - Please report the following for both models, with an equivalent twin-domain model for Bmab (4 domains):
     - R1/wR2 and GoF;
     - number of refined parameters;
     - number of unique and observed reflections;
     - DAC completeness.
   - Then apply a significance test (e.g., Hamilton R-ratio). A lower-symmetry model with more parameters will always fit somewhat better.

22. **The supercell size is ambiguous.**
   - Mixed-order satellites from combinations of q1 and q2 are reported, e.g., q1 + q2 = (1,0,0), which is I-centring-forbidden in the I4/mmm cell.
   - If these arise within a single domain, the true cell is larger than the stated "twofold" P21/c cell.
   - If they arise from domain overlap or double diffraction, this should be demonstrated.
   - "Twofold" should also be defined. Relative to the primitive I4/mmm cell, P21/c has the same volume as conventional I4/mmm but half that of B21/a/F4/mmm, which is confusing as written.

23. **There is no group-theoretical or Landau analysis.**
    - No irreducible-representation, symmetry-mode (e.g., ISODISTORT/Bilbao) or Landau free-energy analysis is given. Please confirm that none is in the reference list either.
    - A direct I4/mmm→P21/c transition is not generically expected to be continuous. Depending on the active irreps and order-parameter direction, it generally requires either coupled order parameters (and thus first-order character) or an intermediate phase.
    - This is precisely the point in dispute, so a symmetry analysis is essential.

24. **The order of the transition is described inconsistently.**
    - "Continuous" and "possibly weak first-order" (because β jumps at the onset) appear in the same paragraph.
    - Hysteresis cannot be assessed, because:
      - the number and spacing of pressure points near the transition are not stated;
      - no decompression data are reported.
    - Clarify whether "continuous" simply means "direct, without an intermediate phase."

25. **The DFT does not establish a direct transition.**
    - **Unconstrained relaxations only.** Relaxations were performed only in an unconstrained P21/c cell. The authors themselves say a looser threshold could have "stopped in the tetragonal (or even one of the orthorhombic)" phase, confirming the energy landscape is extremely flat.
    - **Missing calculations:**
      - separately constrained enthalpy curves H(P) for I4/mmm, Fmmm, Bmab and P21/c;
      - energy differences in meV/f.u.;
      - phonons or soft-mode analysis;
      - tests of spin polarisation or +U for a correlated nickelate.
    - **Convergence.** The 1 mHa (~27 meV/cell) energy convergence may exceed the relevant tilt-variant energy differences.
    - **Simultaneous vanishing of order parameters.** The distortion o, tilt θ and β − 90° are said to vanish "simultaneously" at 16.47 ± 0.04 GPa. This is expected for secondary order parameters coupled to a primary one and does not by itself rule out an intermediate phase.
    - **Error bars.** The ±0.04 GPa reflects numerical error only. The authors' own estimate of PBE pressure error is 1–3 GPa.
    - **Fit to β.** The (Pc−P)^1/2 fit for β shows a "quick breakdown."
    - **Mean-field interpretation.** Interpreting the 0.4 exponent as "departure from mean-field" is not meaningful for static 0 K DFT, which contains no fluctuations.
    - **Comparison temperature.** The DFT Pc should be compared with the 20 K experimental boundary (~14 GPa), not the 300 K onset (10.5 GPa).

26. **The mode-counting argument is fragile.**
    - **Measurement geometry.** Spectra were taken only in the bc plane, with crystal axes not aligned and four twin domains present. The "parallel" spectrum therefore likely mixes Ag and Bg modes, so the parallel count alone cannot be compared with the 24 Ag modes of P21/c.
    - **Total count.** The total of 31 (yy) + 20 (yz) = 51 > 48 assumes that no mode appears in both geometries. This is doubtful with twinned, unaligned crystals, and it is the sole basis for excluding P-1.
    - **Asymmetric standard.**
      - At 170 K, only 34 of the 48 predicted modes are observed, yet this is called "consistent."
      - The 3-mode excess at 26 K is treated as decisive.
    - **Weak test.** The proposed P21 or Pc groups allow ~102 modes, so the count barely tests them.

27. **The backfolding interpretation is inconsistent with the subgroup analysis.**
    - The authors attribute the new modes to backfolding of finite-Q phonons, which implies a cell enlargement.
    - Yet they analyse only same-cell subgroups (P-1, Pc, P21; P1 listed).
    - A supercell that retains 2/m symmetry would also exceed 48 modes, so the inference of broken 2/m does not follow.
    - The authors also concede that selection rules do not strictly apply to incommensurate structures.

28. **Alternative origins of the extra modes are not considered.** Please discuss each of the following:
    - DW amplitude modes;
    - two-phonon or two-magnon scattering;
    - impurity contributions, given that NiO (with a strong two-magnon/2LO response) and La2NiO4 are present in the growth product;
    - an independent test of inversion breaking (e.g., SHG);
    - comparison with IR-active phonon frequencies.
    - The batch of the Raman crystal is not specified. The paper says only "synthesized with the same technique," whereas the P24 XRD crystal is from the same batch as the DAC crystals.

29. **The Raman and XRD results are in tension.**
    - Ambient-pressure synchrotron refinements exist at 230 K and 80 K, i.e., on both sides of T_DW.
    - These report no structural change. The authors themselves describe the absence as "puzzling."
    - Without a quantified XRD detection limit, the "third structural phase" should be presented as a possible symmetry lowering, not an established phase.

30. **Helium-medium hydrostaticity.**
    - He solidifies near ~12 GPa at room temperature, essentially at the reported onset (10.5–11.3 GPa), and is solid over the entire range at 80 K and 20 K.
    - Because Ts is identified by qualitative peak broadening and splitting ("circular → anisotropic → split"), non-hydrostatic stress could mimic or shift the apparent onset.
    - Please address this, e.g., with ruby R1 linewidths or reference peak widths versus pressure.

31. **Pressure calibration at low temperature.**
    - Ruby (IPPS-Ruby2020) was read before and after each run.
    - No low-temperature correction or cooling protocol is described, and DAC pressure can drift by GPa-level amounts on cooling.
    - This directly affects the 20 K/~14 GPa point that anchors the Ts(P) line and the DFT comparison.
    - The ~20% increase of K0 between 300 K and 20 K (155 → 185 GPa) is unusually large and may reflect such issues. Please comment.

32. **The Ts(P) line is weakly constrained.**
    - It is built from about four temperatures, using different techniques (powder at high temperature, single crystal under pressure).
    - Ts is defined by a qualitative criterion with no quantitative threshold.
    - The line is explicitly "a visual guide," so describing Ts as suppressed "roughly linearly" over 1030 K → 20 K is an interpolation, not a measurement.
    - Further points to address:
      - Define the onset criterion quantitatively.
      - State the resolution of the high-temperature powder XRD.
      - State whether that powder data excludes an orthorhombic window below 1030 K.
    - The room-temperature onset is given as 10.5 GPa in the text, but the Fig. 2 caption marks the change at 11.3 GPa. Please reconcile.

33. **Sample characterisation and the self-contradictory sample-dependence argument.**
    - **Missing characterisation:**
      - Oxygen stoichiometry is not measured (only La:Ni by EPMA), despite its known importance (cf. Li et al.'s La4Ni3O10−δ).
      - Side phases (NiO, La2NiO4) are present.
      - Intergrowths or stacking faults (c* streaks) are not checked.
      - The number of crystals measured under pressure is not stated.
    - **Contradiction:**
      - Disagreements with Zhang, Gim/Suthar and Li are attributed to sample growth or quality.
      - Yet the conclusion claims the transition is "impervious" to growth method, "whether flux or floating zone," having measured only flux-grown crystals.
      - This generalisation should be removed or supported with floating-zone data.

34. **The superconductivity conclusion is not supported by the paper's own data.**
    - The wording escalates from "suggests" (introduction) to "affirms that superconductivity emerges only once the system is restored to the tetragonal phase" (conclusion).
    - No transport or magnetisation measurements are performed.
    - Reports of Pc as low as ~5 GPa, which would fall inside the claimed monoclinic phase, are acknowledged but not reconciled.
    - Rephrase as "consistent with" and discuss the conflicting low-Pc reports. Note any pressure-medium dependence.

35. **The language generally overstates the evidence.**
    - Phrases such as "unequivocally," "fully corroborate" and "definitely ruled out" are inappropriate given:
      - the near-zero metric distortion;
      - the unquantified model comparison;
      - single-cell DFT;
      - twin-confounded Raman counting.
    - Please temper the language throughout.

36. "Horizon" in the title is never defined.

37. T_DW is quoted inconsistently (120, 130, 135 K). Use one value with an uncertainty.

38. Figure captions for the 80/20 K panels should state the pressures. The "experiment at T = 20 K, P = 0 GPa" used in the DFT comparison should be identified as a DAC point, P24 data, or an extrapolation.

39. **Raman base temperature.** State clearly that the ~26 K Raman base temperature is set by laser heating (0.5–2 mW), and give the estimated sample-temperature uncertainty.

40. **Data availability.** For a paper whose main claim is a space-group assignment, deposit the CIFs and structure factors (ICSD/CCDC). "Upon reasonable request" is insufficient.

41. **Supplementary material.** Move the key fit statistics (Table S4) and the satellite-search evidence (Fig. S4) into the main text.

42. 1. **Oxygen stoichiometry is not reported.**
   - EPMA measured oxygen against a KTiOPO4 standard, but only La:Ni = 4:3 is given.
   - In Ruddlesden–Popper nickelates, the oxygen deficiency δ strongly affects the structural transition temperature TS, the density-wave temperature TDW, superconductivity and possibly the transition pressure Pc.
   - Without δ, readers cannot judge comparisons with the literature. Examples are the room-temperature Pc (≈15 GPa vs 10.5–11.3 GPa here), the Bmab reports, and the satellites of Zhang et al. [25].
   - Please report the O content with its uncertainty, and give the number of EPMA points and the detection limits. Please also state whether potassium from the K2CO3 flux was checked.

43. 2. **Side phases and sample identity.**
   - NiO and La2NiO4 were found in the reaction product, but no phase fractions are given. It is not stated whether the measured crystals or the BM01 powder contained them.
   - The origin of the BM01 powder ("carefully ground powder sample") is unclear.
   - ID15b and P24 used the "same batch of single-crystals". The Raman crystals were only "synthesized with the same technique".
   - The Raman crystal was not characterized (EPMA, XRD or orientation). The number of crystals measured, and whether the new modes were reproduced, are not stated.
   - This matters because para 66 itself says that sample quality and composition strongly affect the Raman response.

44. 3. **Growth recipe.**
   - The furnace atmosphere is not stated.
   - Please state whether the specific recipe (Ni:K2CO3 = 1:35, 1050 °C, 0.4 °C/h) is taken from [56] or is original.

45. 4. **The evidence for B21/a over Bmab is not quantitative in the main text.**
   - The text says only that B21/a fits "much better (Table S4)". Please give R1, wR2, goodness of fit and parameter counts in the main text, together with a significance test such as the Hamilton test.
   - Please identify any reflections that are forbidden in Bmab but allowed in B21/a, if they exist.
   - The departure β−90° is only about 0.1–0.2°. Its significance currently rests on three things:
     - "error bars smaller than symbols";
     - analogies with other materials;
     - a DFT statement that 10⁻¹° is "close to the experimental accuracy".

     Please give the β uncertainties explicitly (summarize Table S5).
   - The text says the distortion "is negligible at 300 K", yet claims B21/a fits "much better" at 300 K near 10.5 GPa. Please reconcile these statements.

46. 5. **Twinning is underspecified.**
   - Please give the four twin laws as matrices, the refined twin fractions, and how partially overlapping reflections were handled.
   - "Twin obliquity" is defined only verbally ("degree of overlap"), and "pseudo-merohedral" should be briefly explained.
   - The twin model can bias the Bmab/B21/a comparison, so it belongs in the main text or should be clearly referenced to the SI.

47. 6. **Settings, superstructure and indexing.**
   - Please give explicitly the transformation matrices for P21/c→B21/a and I4/mmm→F4/mmm.
   - Please state which cell q1 = (½,½,0), q2 = (½,−½,0) and the "mixed-order" reflections are indexed on. State explicitly whether q2 and the mixed reflections come from twin domains.
   - "3D 2-fold superstructure" should be defined precisely.
   - The 3.7 Å and 5.41 Å cells should be identified with their respective phases and pressures.
   - For "reflections that appear to violate centerings", please give example hkl and say which centring (F or B) is violated in which cell. This is confusing as written because B21/a is itself B-centred.

48. 7. **The transition criterion and pressures are undefined and inconsistent.**
   - Only "reflections in the hk0 plane" are said to broaden and then split. Please name the reflections, give a quantitative criterion, and state whether broadening or splitting defines the transition.
   - The room-temperature transition is given as 10.5 GPa ("onset", in the text) but as 11.3 GPa in the Fig. 2c caption ("F4/mmm at 11.3 GPa, below that B21/a"). The temperatures of Figs. 2b and 2c are not stated.
   - Please give the measured pressure grid, error bars on all transition pressures, and the value plotted in the phase diagram.
   - The 20 K / 14 GPa point is implied but not stated as measured, and no 80 K transition pressure is quoted.

49. 8. **Phase diagram.**
   - The boundary is a "visual guide", yet the abstract claims a roughly linear suppression from 1030 K to 20 K. The number of (P,T) points is not stated, and the boundary appears to rest on very few of them.
   - Please mark the points by method, with error bars in P and T, and perform an actual fit if linearity is claimed.

50. 9. **Discrepancy with the literature transition pressure.**
   - The introduction cites a room-temperature Pc ≈ 15 GPa [19–22], but the paper finds about 10.5–11.3 GPa. This is never discussed. Possible causes include the pressure medium, the transition criterion, δ, and single-crystal vs powder data.
   - Ref. [21] concerns Pr4Ni3O10 but is cited for La4Ni3O10.

51. 10. **First-order vs continuous character is contradictory.**
    - The same paragraph calls a jump in β a "weak first-order transition" and the transition "continuous".
    - The DFT analysis assumes mean-field square-root scaling, which implies a continuous transition.
    - No decompression or hysteresis data and no analysis of a volume discontinuity are reported. The BM01 data were collected on both heating and cooling, but no hysteresis result is given.
    - Please state one consistent conclusion and support it.

52. 11. **Equation of state.**
    - Please state the Birch–Murnaghan order, K0′ (fixed or refined), the fitted pressure range, whether the phases were fitted separately, the software used, and which cell V refers to.
    - Please discuss the ≈17% increase of K0 from 300 K to 80/20 K. It may be an artefact of K0–K0′ correlation.

53. 12. **Tilt angle and atom labels.**
    - θ is defined as 180° − ∠Ni1–O2–Ni2, and Fig. 5 uses that convention. The Fig. 3 caption quotes bond angles (180°, 171.1°), and the axis convention of Fig. 3c is not stated.
    - Ni1 and Ni2 are never assigned to inner or outer layers.
    - The wording conflicts: "octahedral sites strongly distorted" appears next to "at 15 GPa the octahedra is perfect".

54. 13. **The "no DW satellites down to 20 K at ambient pressure" claim is undocumented and inconsistently stated.**
    - The abstract and para 70 claim "down to 20 K at ambient pressure". However, the 20 K data described in the Results are the high-pressure DAC data (ID15b). The ambient-pressure P24 data are described only as "below 130 K", with refinements at 230 and 80 K and no lowest temperature given.
    - Please state the instrument, the lowest temperature, the exposure, and the detection limit (satellite-to-Bragg intensity ratio). Please also confirm that the reciprocal-space coverage includes the q-vector reported by Zhang et al. [25].
    - The absence of satellites is interpreted as a "weak" modulation rather than no modulation. This reasoning should be justified, and the contradiction with [25] discussed.

55. 14. **The mode-counting argument for a lower-symmetry phase is fragile.**
    - The total of 51 is the sum of 31 parallel and 20 crossed modes, against 48 Raman modes allowed in P21/c. The margin is only three modes.
    - Table I says Y and Z lie in the bc plane but are "not aligned with a particular axis". Ag and Bg components can therefore leak between channels, so a mode seen in both channels could be double-counted. Twin domains within the laser spot would also mix the channels.
    - Please list the distinct frequencies in the main text and describe the overlap check.
    - The stronger argument is that 31 parallel modes exceed the 24 allowed Ag modes. That argument should be made explicitly, with a leakage analysis.
    - Please state whether the 70 cm⁻¹ cutoff is considered in the comparison.
    - Please give the fit function, background treatment, Bose correction and peak-acceptance criterion. Currently "conservative… minimum numbers" appears only in the SI.

56. 15. **Alternative origins of the extra modes are not excluded.**
    - Possible origins include the NiO and La2NiO4 side phases (NiO has one- and two-phonon features near 500 and above 1000 cm⁻¹), second-order scattering, and defect- or vacancy-activated modes.
    - There are no DFT phonons to predict mode frequencies.

57. 16. **Group-theory inconsistency.**
    - The new modes are attributed to backfolding of finite-Q phonons, which implies a supercell or incommensurate modulation. However, the candidate groups P21, Pc and P-1 are k = 0 subgroups with the original cell. Supercell subgroups are not considered, and P1 is listed but not analyzed.
    - Para 70 concedes the assignment "must be revisited… superspace approach", and refs. [51]–[55] on modulated structures are cited. Yet no superspace analysis is attempted, and the conclusion asserts that P21/c is broken. This should be presented as tentative.
    - Losing the 21 screw axis or the c-glide should produce violations of the P21/c systematic absences. Please state whether these were searched for in the low-T XRD. Please also say how P21 and Pc could be distinguished.

58. 17. **The density-wave temperature is inconsistent.**
    - TDW is given as 135 K (abstract), 130 K (para 21, Fig. 6 caption, paras 70–71), 120 K (paras 66–67), and as the onset of new modes "between 120 and 150 K".
    - The temperature steps are not given.
    - TDW is not determined independently (transport or thermodynamics) on the authors' crystals, nor compared with recent spectroscopic work [48]–[50].

59. 18. **Raman experimental details are missing.**
    - Please give the grating, resolution, notch/edge filter, objective, spot size, and whether the 0.5–2 mW power is measured on the sample. Please also state which power was used for each spectrum.
    - Please explain how laser heating was estimated (2 K → 26 K) and whether the correction applies at all temperatures.
    - The Fig. 6 offsets and normalization are not stated.
    - No reference is given for the Trivista spectrometer or the PyLon CCD.
    - Please state explicitly that no Raman was measured under pressure, so the proposed low-temperature phase cannot be placed in the P–T diagram.

60. 19. **The Raman geometry description is inconsistent.**
    - Table I says the "Poynting vector is perpendicular to X". This conflicts with the Porto notation X(YY)X and with the main text ("perpendicular to the (bc) plane").
    - The angles of Y and Z relative to b and c, and how the orientation was determined, are not given.
    - The caption calls P21/c a "sub-group".

61. 20. **BM01 (high-temperature powder XRD).**
    - Please state whether the capillary was sealed, the atmosphere (oxygen loss up to 1223 K is a concern), the ramp and dwell, the temperature calibration, and the criterion for TS = 1030 K.
    - Please state which models were refined (I4/mmm, Bmab, P21/c), their R-factors, the heating/cooling hysteresis, and any post-heating changes.
    - The BM01 beamtime is not acknowledged.

62. 21. **ID15b (high-pressure single-crystal XRD).**
    - Please give the P–T path (cooled then compressed, or the reverse), the cooling method and cryostat, and the location of the temperature sensor.
    - Please state the low-temperature ruby correction, since IPPS-Ruby2020 is a room-temperature calibration.
    - Please give the gasket hole size, crystal size and number of crystals, and the total ω range.
    - Please give the data-reduction software, and the completeness, R_int and R1 for each pressure point.

63. 22. **P24 (ambient-pressure low-temperature single-crystal XRD).**
    - Please give the list of temperatures, refinement statistics and twin handling.
    - The EVAL14/EVAL15 wording ("EVAL15 failed") needs clarification.
    - P24ToolsCP v1.12 is unpublished and has no DOI or URL.

64. 23. **Key settings are missing.**
    - Please state whether the calculations were spin-polarized and with which magnetic order, and whether +U was used. Both are essential for a density-wave nickelate.
    - Please give the smearing scheme and width (the material is a metal), and whether the 1 mHa tolerance is per cell or per atom.
    - Please give the pressure grid, the starting structures, and the ABINIT and PseudoDojo versions.
    - ABINIT and PseudoDojo are not cited, and "PsuedoDojo" is misspelled.
    - The stress threshold is given in Ha/Bohr; it should be Ha/Bohr³.

65. 24. **Dependence on the starting structure and the exclusion of Bmab.**
    - The authors note that a looser threshold could leave the relaxation in a tetragonal or orthorhombic structure "depending on the starting crystal structure". This implies a very flat energy landscape.
    - Bmab and Fmmm are "ruled out" only because the P21/c relaxations never land in them. Please report enthalpy comparisons between P21/c, Bmab and I4/mmm, and relaxations from several starting structures.
    - Please give a numerical tolerance for calling a relaxed structure "tetragonal".

66. 25. **Fitting and uncertainties.**
    - The square-root fits use only the three points nearest Pc. The ±0.04 GPa on Pc is just the spread of the three fits.
    - If the free-exponent fit (α = 0.4) also used three points, it is exactly determined and uninformative. Please state the number of points used.
    - Please justify the linear form for o, for example with a Landau secondary-order-parameter argument, and state the pressure range over which square-root scaling is assumed. Note that β has a maximum near 4 GPa.
    - The error procedure is not reproducible as described:
      - the stiffness tensor is estimated from the "last four relaxation steps", although a monoclinic tensor has 13 independent constants;
      - the "elastic constants associated to internal atomic positions" are undefined.

      Please state whether these errors weight the fits. They capture only convergence error and exclude systematic errors.

67. 26. **Framing of the DFT comparison.**
    - The Fig. 1 caption says the DFT Pc ≈ 16 GPa is "in good agreement with experiment". The DFT section warns of a "large PBE error" of 1–3 GPa.
    - Please compare a PBE-corrected Pc quantitatively with the 20 K / 14 GPa point, and acknowledge that DFT is at 0 K while some data are at 300 K.
    - The claim that "θ scaling and magnitude seems in agreement" is not supported. DFT gives θ = 17.25° at 0 GPa, which implies about 14° at 6 GPa from the authors' own scaling. Experiment gives about 9° at 6 GPa.
    - The DFT section describes o as the Fmmm→I4/mmm order parameter and θ as the Bmab→Fmmm order parameter. The XRD section treats both as belonging to a single 4/mmm→2/m transition. Fmmm is never introduced in the experimental part.
    - "The primitive cell in the B21/a setting" is ambiguous, since B21/a is centred. Please state which cell the DFT β = 90.42° refers to.
    - DFT and experimental β, θ and o are never plotted together. A joint figure in a common setting would help. Fig. 5 is hard to read.

68. 27. **Superconductivity "only in the tetragonal phase"** is inferred from the literature [19, 20, 22], not measured here, and no Tc points appear in the figures.
    - The reasoning in para 22 (no Bmab, therefore superconductivity is tetragonal) does not follow.
    - Superconducting onsets reported down to about 5 GPa [19] would fall in the monoclinic region of the authors' own diagram.
    - This should be framed as a correlation, with the comparability of samples (growth, δ, pressure medium) discussed.

69. 28. **Attribution of the Bmab claim is inconsistent.**
    - The introduction cites [37], an ambient-pressure floating-zone paper. It describes [37] as concerning "single crystals" in one place and groups it with powder XRD studies in another.
    - The Results and DFT sections attribute Bmab to Li et al. [22].
    - The sentence about Li et al.'s centring-violating reflections has no clear subject, and their interpretation is never stated, only that it "differs".

70. 29. **Data availability.**
    - Data are available only "upon reasonable request", and the SI URL is a placeholder.
    - Please deposit CIFs, the raw or reduced diffraction and Raman data, and the DFT inputs and relaxed structures.

71. 30. **Software and references.**
    - FullProf is not named in its reference [59].
    - No versions are given for FullProf, JANA2006, EVAL, ABINIT or PseudoDojo.
    - Ref. [22] is incomplete ("n/a", no pages).
    - Reference formatting is inconsistent (lowercased chemical formulas, inconsistent journal abbreviations).

72. 31. **Figures.**
    - The figure numbering between Fig. 5 (DFT) and Fig. 6 (Raman) is confusing in places.
    - The Fig. 2b and 2c captions lack temperatures.
    - The plane labels and panel pressures in the Fig. 5 / Fig. 6 discussion should be checked against the text.

73. 32. **Terminology and typos.**
    - "Pseudo-tetragonal" is used for a phase described as monoclinic, next to claims of a tetragonal phase. Please use the terms consistently.
    - The statements about β precision should be unified.
    - There are numerous typos throughout.
