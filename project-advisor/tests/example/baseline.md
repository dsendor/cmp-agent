## My recommendation

Start with **local thermal transport in magic-angle graphene**. It uses your nanoSQUID, which few groups have, and a quick look at an existing device tells you a lot.

## The others

1. *Altermagnet noise thermometry* is timely but depends on films you don't control.
2. Automated stacking is safe and useful to the lab, but it is unlikely to be the result you want before job applications.
3. The new trilayer stacking is a long shot:
   it needs three twist angles controlled at once.

| Idea | Upside | Main risk |
|---|---|---|
| Thermal | High | Signal size |
| Trilayer | Very high | Fabrication |
