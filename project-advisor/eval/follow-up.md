# Follow-up, 3 to 6 months later

Participant code: P__. Date: ________. Five minutes; answer with idea numbers, not content.

1. Which idea are you working on now? Is it the one you ranked first after the session?
2. Did you run the cheapest test the advisor suggested for any idea? Which, and what
   happened? Did it change your plan?
3. Did any assumption flagged as risky turn out to matter? Did a risk neither page raised
   turn out to matter?
4. Did a paper either page cited matter in the months since? Did a paper neither page
   found turn out to be the closest prior work?
5. Looking back, which page, A or B, was more useful? (You can open the key now if you
   haven't.)
6. Would you use the advisor for your next choice? What would have to change?
