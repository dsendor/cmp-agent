# Intake: the Heilmeier questions

George Heilmeier's questions for a new research program, as DARPA lists them
(https://www.darpa.mil/about/heilmeier-catechism). Field names in `advisor.json` in brackets.

1. What are you trying to do? Say it with no jargon. [`what`]
2. How is it done today, and what are the limits of current practice? [`today`]
3. What is new in your approach, and why do you think it will succeed? [`new`]
4. Who cares? If you succeed, what difference will it make? [`who_cares`]
5. What are the risks? [`risks`]
6. How much will it cost? (Here: devices, samples, instrument time, collaborators.) [`cost`]
7. How long will it take? [`time`]
8. What are the midterm and final exams to check for success? [`midterm`, `final`]

Ask only where the answer is missing and matters for the choice. The most useful three are
usually 3 (what's new), 8 (the midterm exam, which becomes the cheapest test) and 7 (how
long). "Don't know" is a fine answer; it's information about risk.
